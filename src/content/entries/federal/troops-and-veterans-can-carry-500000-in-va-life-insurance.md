If you serve on active duty or in the Guard or reserves, your Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance can now pay your family up to $500,000 if you die, up from $400,000. The old limit hadn't changed since 2005. You buy coverage in $50,000 steps, and VA lists the monthly premium for the full $500,000 at $26.

You didn't have to apply. On March 1, 2023, every service member covered by SGLI was moved up to $500,000, including people who had earlier lowered or turned down their coverage. Anyone who wanted less could lower it again. Veterans' Group Life Insurance, the coverage you can keep after you leave the service, also went up to $500,000, but veterans have to ask for the extra coverage. Veterans under 60 who already had $400,000 can add more in $25,000 steps on their policy anniversary.

Service members pay for this coverage through their premiums, and the law delayed the increase until VA confirmed the program could carry it without running at a loss. VA kept the rate per $1,000 of coverage the same.

Sen. Tommy Tuberville wrote the bill and introduced it in September 2021. The Senate passed it in March 2022, and the House passed it 425–0 that September, with every Democrat and Republican who voted backing it. President Joe Biden signed it on October 17, 2022.
