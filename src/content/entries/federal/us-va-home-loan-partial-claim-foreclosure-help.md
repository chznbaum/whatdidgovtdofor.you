If your VA-backed mortgage is behind, VA can cover the amount needed to bring it current, capped at 25% of the unpaid principal balance. The cap is 30% when the past-due balance includes a payment missed from March 1, 2020, through May 1, 2025. The amount becomes a 0%-interest deferred balance secured by your home. There are no monthly payments; it is due when the mortgage matures, ends or is paid off—usually when you sell or refinance.

You don't apply to VA directly. Your servicer reviews you through VA's required sequence of foreclosure alternatives. If you qualify for a partial claim, the servicer puts you on a three-month trial payment plan. After you complete it, the servicer advances the money to bring the loan current and VA reimburses it. The property must qualify as your primary residence. VA generally allows one partial claim per loan, and earlier VA COVID-era partial-claim assistance can make the loan ineligible. The program opened June 15, 2026; servicers have until November 28, 2026, to implement it. If your servicer won't help, call VA at 877-827-3702, option 6.

Rep. Derrick Van Orden (R-Wis.) introduced the bill, and House Veterans' Affairs Chair Mike Bost (R-Ill.) moved it to passage on the House floor. The House and Senate passed it by voice vote, so no individual votes were recorded. President Donald Trump signed it on July 30, 2025. The law also authorized $344 million for each of fiscal years 2025 and 2026 for VA grants supporting homeless veterans.
