Palm Lake Urban Sanctuary is an 86-apartment building going up at 5401 22nd Avenue North in Disston Heights. It sits on unused land owned by Palm Lake Christian Church. The church is building it with Newstar Development and the Pinellas County Housing Authority. The one- and two-bedroom apartments are for households earning up to 80% of the area median income, and 15% of them are set aside for households earning 30% or less. Plans include a playground, a garden, and a clubhouse with a computer room and offices for service providers.

In 2024, the city committed up to $1,544,057 from its federal American Rescue Plan Act money. On Oct. 1, 2026, the City Council added up to $225,000 more and pushed back the project's deadlines. That brings the city's share to about $1.77 million. Tax credits, bank loans, and county and state housing funds pay for the rest.

The extra money passed 4–0 on the consent agenda, with Chair Lisset Hanewicz presiding. Three members were absent, and Corey Givens recused himself. Construction started in summer 2025. The housing authority's interest list sends alerts when the rental waitlist opens; joining it does not put someone on the waitlist or guarantee housing. In February 2026, the developer said it hoped to finish by the end of the year.
