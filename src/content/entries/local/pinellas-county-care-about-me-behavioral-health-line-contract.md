Finding a counselor or an addiction treatment program can mean calling one office after another. Pinellas County's Care About Me service does that for you. A specialist asks what you need, helps book an appointment with a local provider, and can follow up to make sure you got care. The county approved a 36-month operating contract, but funding after FY27 still needs annual budget approval.

Any current Pinellas County resident can call or text 727-333-CARE (2273), or use the program's online chat. Family members can also contact the service for someone they're worried about. Specialists are available weekdays from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m., and Saturdays and legal holidays from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. It isn't a crisis line: in an emergency, call 911, and if you're thinking about suicide, call or text 988.

The county started Care About Me in May 2024. In September 2026, the county commission, chaired by Dave Eggers, approved a new operator. Gulf Coast Jewish Family & Community Services had the top score of the two bidders whose proposals met the county's rules; a third bidder was disqualified. The contract pays up to $2,519,236 over 36 months and can be extended two more years. FY27 funding is budgeted in Human Services' Opioid Fund and is anticipated to come fully or partly from city and county opioid-settlement funds. Funding for later years must be approved in each annual county budget.
