If you live in Orange County and can't afford a lawyer, the Legal Aid Society of the Orange County Bar Association may be able to represent you in civil court. The county is giving it $1,567,371 for the 2026–27 budget year. The money pays for lawyers and court access for low-income residents, the working poor, children, and others with special legal needs. To get help, contact Legal Aid; it decides who qualifies.

The same vote funded two smaller programs. Seniors First gets $95,418 to serve as legal guardian for older residents whom a court has declared unable to care for themselves. That way someone is responsible for their care and money. The Orange County Bar Association gets $102,228 for its Citizen Dispute Settlement program, which helps residents settle disputes through mediation instead of a court fight.

Florida's constitution makes counties pay for some court-related costs. The chief judge of the Ninth Judicial Circuit named these three programs as local needs of the court, and the county's budget office brought the agreements to the board. The Board of County Commissioners, chaired by Mayor Jerry Demings, approved them on Sept. 15, 2026, as part of its consent agenda.
