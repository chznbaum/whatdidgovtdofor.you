Hialeah runs aftercare and summer camp programs for children and young adults. They provide supervised activities outside school hours and during the summer. On Sept. 22, 2026, the City Council accepted a $906,400 grant from The Children's Trust to help pay for them.

The money covers three city programs: Creative Learning & Play, Step Ahead, and Young Leaders with Character. It runs for one year, from Aug. 15, 2026 through Aug. 14, 2027. Families register through the city, and eligibility depends on the program: Creative Learning & Play serves children in kindergarten through fifth grade, Young Leaders with Character serves students in grades 6 through 8, and Step Ahead serves students with intellectual disabilities ages 14 to 22. The council also ratified and approved the grant agreement.

The council approved the grant as part of its consent agenda, 7-0. Council President Carl Zogby presided over the meeting, and Mayor Bryan Calvo signed the resolution. The grant comes from The Children's Trust, which Miami-Dade voters created and fund through a dedicated property-tax levy.
