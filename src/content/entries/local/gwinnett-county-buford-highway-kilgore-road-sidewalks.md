Gwinnett County awarded a construction contract for two projects: sidewalks along Buford Highway and an extension of the right-turn lane on Kilgore Road at Patrick Elementary School. The county's bid notice says the work also includes curb and gutter, minor road widening, asphalt paving and striping, and storm-drain pipe.

You don't need to apply or do anything. Once construction is finished, people walking along Buford Highway will have sidewalks to use, while drivers and school traffic at Patrick Elementary will have a longer right-turn lane.

The Gwinnett Board of Commissioners, chaired by Nicole Love Hendrickson, voted 5-0 on June 16, 2026, to award the contract to Hasbun Construction, LLC, for up to $3,718,958.84. Funding comes from the 2017 SPLOST, a one-cent sales tax Gwinnett voters approved in November 2016 for specified capital projects. The tax collected revenue from April 2017 through March 2023, including money designated for transportation projects.
