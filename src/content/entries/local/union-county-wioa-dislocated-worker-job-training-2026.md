If you live in Union County and lost your job in a layoff or because a business closed, this grant supports services that can help you get back to work. The county added $1,971,867 to its 2026 budget after receiving notice from the New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development. The money is part of the federal Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act's Dislocated Worker program.

The county says its WIOA Adult and Dislocated Worker services include career counseling and employment planning, occupational-skills training, Individual Training Accounts, on-the-job training, workshops, and support services. You must meet federal and state eligibility rules. The American Job Centers in Plainfield and at the temporary Elizabeth location accept appointments and walk-ins. The county lists the permanent Elizabeth location as temporarily closed and gives 342 Westminster Avenue as the temporary site.

Commissioner Kimberly Palmieri-Mouded sponsored Resolution 2026-514, which put the grant into the county budget. The Board of County Commissioners approved it 8-0 on July 30, 2026. Commissioner Alexander Mirabella was absent.
