Aspire Health Partners, a nonprofit, provides mental health and addiction care in Central Florida. Under this agreement, Orange County sends $4,034,459 to the state's Agency for Health Care Administration. AHCA uses the county transfer as the nonfederal share needed to obtain federal matching funds, and the combined funding pays for behavioral health services at Aspire in fiscal year 2026-27.

The money flows through Florida's Medicaid Low Income Pool, which helps pay for care for people who are uninsured, underinsured or on Medicaid. This is provider funding, not a benefit patients apply for through the county. The money goes to Aspire, which can then serve people who can't pay the full cost. For the past 26 years the county has used the same kind of transfer to fund its Primary Care Access Network of clinics. The county memo says behavioral health providers were included four years ago and again this year.

Staff in the county's Mental Health and Homelessness Division prepared the agreement. The Board of County Commissioners, chaired by Mayor Jerry Demings, approved it on its consent agenda on Sept. 29, 2026.
