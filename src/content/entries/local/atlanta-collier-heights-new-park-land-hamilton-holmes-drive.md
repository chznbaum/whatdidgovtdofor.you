Atlanta is buying about 12.6 acres of land west of Hamilton E. Holmes Drive in the Collier Heights area for a future public park. The land is in Council District 10, on the city's west side. The ordinance authorizes the purchase; it does not itself design or build the park.

The ordinance lets the city spend up to $2,340,658.39 to buy the land. It identifies the city's Park Impact Fee West account and Park Improvement Fund as the funding sources.

The Atlanta City Council approved the purchase 11-0 on Oct. 5, 2026, with Council President Marci Collier Overstreet presiding. The public record doesn't say which council member brought the ordinance. The council's vote was the first step. The city still has to close on the land and design and build the park, and no opening date has been announced.
