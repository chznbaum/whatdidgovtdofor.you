Cobb County authorized traffic-calming measures on 18 streets it maintains. Traffic calming uses street design and other measures to change driver behavior and improve safety for people walking, biking, and driving. The county approved up to $519,612.50 for the work.

The county added the work to its existing contract with Blount Construction Company for resurfacing local roads in northeast Cobb.

The work follows a traffic-calming policy commissioners adopted in 2024. That policy lowered the speed threshold for some streets to qualify and expanded the county's options beyond speed humps and speed-display signs to include raised crosswalks and intersections, roundabouts, and median islands. Chairwoman Lisa Cupid presided over the July 28, 2026 meeting, where the board approved the consent agenda 5-0. The vote also approved the corresponding budget changes and authorized her to sign the contract documents.
