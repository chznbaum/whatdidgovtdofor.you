In the first months of the pandemic, Goochland County's Department of Social Services received more Community Development Block Grant money, a federal grant program. On June 2, 2020, the Board of Supervisors voted to put $216,110 of it into the department's budget. The money was for helping residents who came to Social Services with their electric bills, rent, mortgage payments and grocery costs.

Help went through Social Services, so residents asked the department for it rather than receiving a payment automatically. The grant needed no local match, so the county didn't have to take money from other services to get it.

The board approved the money 5–0 as part of a monthly budget update it had begun hearing because of the pandemic's hit to the economy. Supervisor Susan Lascolette, who chaired the board in 2020, led the vote. Supervisors Ken Peterson, John Lumpkins, Neil Spoonhower and Don Sharpe voted with her. In the same vote, the board put $200,000 of the county's federal CARES Act relief money toward work to protect people from COVID-19 in county buildings.
