Broward County approved $21,194,708—$21.2 million rounded—for local service providers during the 2026–27 budget year. The programs provide behavioral health and substance use care for children and adults, services for residents with special health needs, and help with housing and economic stability.

Most of the funding renews or extends agreements with providers already doing this work, while some supports new agreements. In the last complete fiscal year, providers achieved 98% of the outcome indicators for these programs. Residents receive help through the providers working with the county's Human Services Department; eligibility varies by program.

The Human Services Department and its Community Partnerships Division recommended the funding, and the Children's Services Board sent a letter of support. The County Commission, chaired by Mayor Mark Bogen, approved it 8-0 on Aug. 20, 2026. Commissioner Nan Rich pulled the item from the consent agenda and abstained. The funding depended on the county budget adopted Sept. 22 and took effect Oct. 1. On Oct. 20, commissioners reduced one provider agreement by $196,389, eliminated its Medical Care Support Services program, and shifted funding among its advocacy and respite-care programs.
