HART's Route 1 runs from the Marion Transit Center downtown, up Florida Avenue and over Fletcher Avenue to the University Area Transit Center near USF. Tampa's city budget pays HART to run it more often than HART's own budget would. With that money, buses come every 15 minutes on weekday days instead of every 20. Weekday evenings and weekends get a bus every 20 minutes instead of every 30. Those improvements are funded through January 2027.

On Sept. 22, 2026, the City Council added $150,000 to the $1.35 million already set aside for Route 1 in 2027. Of the new money, $55,000 covers the full cost of the more frequent service, and $95,000 goes to HART to promote Route 1 and its other frequent routes. That brings the city's total to $1.5 million. Riders don't need to do anything. The regular fare is $2, with daily and monthly caps.

Transit Now Tampa Bay and its organizer, Dayna Lazarus, asked for the increase so HART wouldn't cut back how often buses run. Council member Guido Maniscalco made the motion at the Sept. 8 budget hearing, with Lynn Hurtak seconding it. Hurtak made the final motion on Sept. 22, with Maniscalco seconding. The council passed it unanimously as part of the budget hearing that adopted the city's 2027 spending plan.
