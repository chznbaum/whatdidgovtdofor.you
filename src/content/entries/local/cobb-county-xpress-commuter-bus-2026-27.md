If you ride an Xpress commuter bus from Cobb County into Atlanta, your service is covered for another year. Cobb County's agreement with the Georgia Transportation Efficiency Authority, the state agency that runs Xpress, keeps the buses running in the county from July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. The agreement calls for continuing the service and expanding it.

You don't apply. You pay the fare and board. The county's earlier agreement, from 2025, covered Route 484, which was redrawn to pick up riders when CobbLinc ended its own commuter routes 100, 101 and 102. Route 484 runs on weekdays from the Hickory Grove and Town Center park-and-ride lots to Midtown Atlanta, with a bus about every 30 minutes at rush hour. Under that earlier agreement, the county and the state shared the cost.

The Cobb County Board of Commissioners approved the agreement 4–0 on June 23, 2026. Vice Chair Erick Allen presided because Chairwoman Lisa Cupid was absent. The vote authorized the chairwoman to sign the agreement.
