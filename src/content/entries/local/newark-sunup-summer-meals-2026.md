School meals are unavailable to many children when school lets out. Newark's SuNuP program provided free summer meals at participating sites. New Jersey's 2026 sponsor list named Newark as an approved sponsor with 104 active sites and operating dates from June 29 through Aug. 28. Children 18 and younger could receive meals. At open sites, any child could eat free; enrolled sites served children taking part in the site's activities.

The federal Summer Food Service Program reimburses local sponsors through the New Jersey Department of Agriculture for meals served and documented operating costs. The department awarded Newark $1,918,741.75, and the council accepted the award with no city money required. Because reimbursement depends on actual meals and costs, the award does not establish that every dollar was ultimately paid.

The city's recreation, cultural affairs and senior services department applied on March 13, 2026. Council members Dupré L. Kelly and Amina Bey sponsored the resolution accepting the grant, and the Newark Municipal Council adopted it on June 17. A later city filing identified Whitsons Food Services as the catering contractor for the June 29 through Aug. 29 season and said the emergency contract was needed to avoid disrupting meal delivery. The 2026 summer service period is complete.
