Richmond Gas Works operates an old regional gas system. This $10 million grant is for replacing about 13.2 miles of cast-iron, ductile-iron, steel and older plastic gas mains with newer pipe. PHMSA says the selected lines include aging and leak-prone materials prioritized under the utility's safety plan. Replacing them reduces the risks of leaks and failures and cuts methane emissions.

You don't need to apply. The work takes place in existing street rights-of-way at nine groups of sites in Richmond, Henrico and Chesterfield. Nearby homes and businesses benefit directly from more reliable service, while the broader system benefits from having fewer old pipes. The federal grant supplies $10 million for the work through Richmond's gas utility budget.

The 2021 Bipartisan Infrastructure Law created the federal grant program. PHMSA announced Richmond's $10 million selection in April 2023 and executed the award on May 31, 2024. Mayor Levar Stoney was the sole patron of the ordinance accepting the money and adding it to the fiscal 2024–2025 gas utility budget. Richmond City Council adopted the ordinance on July 22, 2024.
