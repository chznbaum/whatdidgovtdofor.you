Many people in Miami-Dade can't afford a bus or train fare. This grant pays for free rides for some of them on Metrobus and Metrorail. From July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027, up to $8 million in state money pays for preloaded EASY Cards and tickets. The county adds a 10% match of $888,889. Available passes cover a single trip, a day, a week, a month or a whole year.

You don't apply to the county yourself. Qualifying nonprofit agencies participate in the county's Transportation Disadvantaged program, verify their clients' eligibility and distribute passes monthly. The county requires participating agencies to serve Miami-Dade residents whose documented household income is at or below 225% of the federal poverty guidelines. Its listed target groups include people who are disabled or homeless, adults and children at risk, and unemployed people needing job training. Passes are limited and go out first come, first served. People who already ride free, such as Patriot Passport holders, aren't eligible.

The money comes from Florida's Transportation Disadvantaged Trust Fund through the state Commission for the Transportation Disadvantaged, and the county renews it every year. On July 21, 2026, the Miami-Dade Board of County Commissioners, chaired by Anthony Rodriguez, approved this year's agreement 12-0.
