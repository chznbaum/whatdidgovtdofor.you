Azalea Cay is a new apartment complex going up on a former Raytheon site at 1501 72nd Street North, near Tyrone Square Mall and the Pinellas Trail. Its first phase will have 341 apartments. In February 2025, St. Petersburg approved up to $6 million for what was then called Azalea Gateway. In return, 102 apartments will carry income limits for at least 50 years: 51 for households earning no more than 80% of the area median income and 51 for households earning no more than 120%.

The city's money comes from its Housing Capital Improvement Fund. Resolution No. 2025-103 moved unspent housing appropriations and other available fund balance into the project. Pinellas County added $5.25 million from its Penny for Pinellas sales-tax funding.

Construction began on Sept. 28, 2026, and the first phase is expected to take about 24 months. The apartments are not available yet. When leasing begins, renters seeking one of the restricted apartments will have to meet its income limit. The developers plan two more phases, for roughly 1,000 apartments altogether.

The council approved the funding 6-1 on Feb. 20, 2025. Council Chair Copley Gerdes, whose district includes the site, spoke in support. Richie Floyd cast the no vote because he opposed subsidizing apartments for households at 120% of the median income. On Aug. 6, 2026, the council voted 7-0, with Chair Lisset Hanewicz absent, to reset the construction deadlines and keep the city's commitment in place.
