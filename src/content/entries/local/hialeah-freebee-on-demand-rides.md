Hialeah's on-demand Freebee service picks you up and drops you off in an electric vehicle within its mapped service area. You book it in the Freebee app. Freebee's current service page lists hours as 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on weekdays and 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. on weekends. Requests must arrive at least 45 minutes before closing, and every trip must begin or end in the inner zone. Destinations include Hialeah Hospital, City Hall, grocery stores and city parks.

In 2023, the city's transit manager said the rides are free, like the city's buses. City leaders said a half-penny transportation tax covers most of what Hialeah's transit costs. Official county reports counted 11,282 Freebee boardings from July through September 2023, the service's highest quarter of that fiscal year.

On Aug. 25, 2026, the City Council voted 7-0 to keep the service going. The resolution lets Hialeah use a contract the City of Aventura bid out with Beefree, LLC, the company that runs Freebee. That contract has been extended through Dec. 31, 2027, and the resolution authorizes a purchase order of up to $869,775.42. Council President Carl Zogby presided over the vote, which was part of the consent agenda; the minutes do not identify a member who brought the item. Mayor Bryan Calvo signed the resolution.
