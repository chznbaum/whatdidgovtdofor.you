Newark authorized the YMCA of Newark and Vicinity to give homeless residents a temporary place to stay, along with support services. The program serves both single adults and families at the YMCA's emergency residence at 600 Broad Street. The contract runs from April 1, 2026, through March 31, 2027, with a ceiling of $2,901,750.

The program is run through the city's Office of Homeless Services, part of the Department of Health and Community Wellness. The YMCA says people seeking a bed must first obtain a voucher from a city, county or state social-services agency; after hours, NJ 211 handles one-time emergency placements for eligible people.

The city chose the YMCA through a public request for proposals advertised in January 2026. The resolution initially certified $484,950 for April and May. The remaining balance depended on available money and later appropriation. Because the contract started before the vote, the resolution ratified services back to April 1 while authorizing the rest of the term.

The Newark Municipal Council adopted the resolution 9–0 on May 20, 2026, with Council President C. Lawrence Crump presiding. It came to the council as a resolution of the Council of the Whole, so no single member is named as its sponsor.
