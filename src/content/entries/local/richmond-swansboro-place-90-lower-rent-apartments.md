Swansboro Place is now open at 2008 Hull Street, with 90 one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments. Virginia Housing says every apartment is reserved for a household earning 60% or less of the area median income. The city grant agreement requires the project to keep occupancy and rents affordable for at least 30 years.

The property has begun taking rental applications; contact its leasing office for current availability. The city announced that it would authorize Richmond's housing authority to seek 18 project-based vouchers for Swansboro Place. Those vouchers use RRHA's property-specific waiting-list process, not the property's regular application alone.

The performance grant works like a property-tax rebate. The owner pays the full tax bill, then the city can return the amount above the agreement's baseline for up to 30 years while the apartments remain affordable. The city keeps the pre-project base revenue but gives up the future increase. Virginia Housing also provided permanent financing and administered federal Low-Income Housing Tax Credits.

Mayor Levar Stoney proposed the original ordinance, and City Council adopted it 9-0 on July 22, 2024. Mayor Danny Avula proposed the 2025 amendment adding 2021 Decatur Street; Council adopted that amendment 9-0. The Richmond Economic Development Authority administers the grant.
