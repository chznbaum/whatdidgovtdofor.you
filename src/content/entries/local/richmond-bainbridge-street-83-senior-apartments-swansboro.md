A new apartment building for older renters is open at 2100 Bainbridge St. in Richmond's Swansboro neighborhood, between Hull Street and Semmes Avenue. It has 83 one- and two-bedroom apartments for people 55 and older who earn no more than 60% of the area median income. At the May 2026 opening, rents were about $1,100 a month for a one-bedroom and about $1,300 for a two-bedroom. Residents also get a fitness center, a community room and a shared balcony.

You apply through the building's manager, Epoch Properties, which runs it for the nonprofit developer Project:Homes. The site used to be a coal storage yard for trains.

The building cost about $20 million. City Council member Stephanie Lynch, whose 5th District includes the site, and Mayor Levar Stoney were the ordinance's named patrons. City Council adopted it on Nov. 13, 2023, authorizing an Affordable Housing Performance Grant for the project. After the project pays its full property-tax bill, it can request an annual grant based only on the increase in taxes from the new improvements above the site's pre-construction tax bill. The city estimated the grant at $201,768 a year.
