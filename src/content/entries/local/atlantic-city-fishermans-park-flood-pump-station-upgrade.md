The Baltic Avenue Canal carries rainwater from about 775 acres of Atlantic City out to the back bays. It helps reduce street flooding in parts of Ducktown, Westside, Midtown and the Inlet and on access roads into Bungalow Park. Fisherman's Park is one of the canal's outlets, and Atlantic City Council has hired Richard E. Pierson Construction to upgrade its pump station for $1,954,300.

The upgrade adds a mechanical bar screen that catches trash and debris before the water goes into Gardner's Basin. It also adds a direct power source for the pumps. Residents don't need to do anything to benefit.

The pump upgrade and a separate project installing new grates on 170 existing storm drains share a $4 million grant from the U.S. Economic Development Administration and $1 million in local matching funds. The grant was awarded in June 2024 under a 2023 federal disaster relief law. Council approved the construction contract on Oct. 28, 2026.
