CityFHEPS is New York City's rent voucher program. It pays part of the monthly rent for eligible families and individuals leaving shelter or at risk of homelessness. The voucher used to work only inside the five boroughs. It can now be used for a whole apartment anywhere in New York State, giving households more places to look near family, work, or other support.

To move outside the city, a household in shelter or in the community needs a valid CityFHEPS shopping letter. A current participant who wants to relocate needs an approved good-cause transfer. Outside the city, the maximum rent follows the local housing authority's standard. The apartment must pass a city safety check, which may be conducted in person or by video, and its rent must be reasonable for the area. The rental term must last at least one year, and the household generally must be protected from rent increases for that year; increases allowed under government rent regulations are an exception. Single rooms do not qualify outside the city.

Mayor Eric Adams announced the expansion in September 2023, when the city had more than 113,000 people in its care and fewer than 1% of apartments renting for less than $1,500 were available. Under Department of Social Services Commissioner Molly Wasow Park, the Human Resources Administration adopted the permanent rule on Jan. 31, 2024. It took effect when published on Feb. 5. The city funds the vouchers.
