The Coalition for the Homeless of Central Florida is building a new Center for Women and Families on its campus in Orlando's Parramore neighborhood. The new building replaces the Coalition's current center for women and families. Orange County is putting $15 million toward building it. The money comes from a federal disaster-recovery grant, so the new building is designed to hold up in disasters. It will give short-term and bridge housing to about 550 homeless women and families a year. News reports say it adds 112 short-term beds, plus 20 transitional apartments with 75 beds for families who need to stay longer.

There's nothing to apply for yet. The center is under construction, and the county expects it to be finished by January 2028. Women and families who need shelter can contact the Coalition.

The county commissioners chose the project for disaster-recovery money in March 2026 after taking applications. On Oct. 27, 2026, they approved the $15 million agreement on their consent agenda, on a motion by Commissioner Maribel Gomez Cordero, seconded by Commissioner Mayra Uribe. The county had already put about $3.26 million in disaster-recovery money into the project's first two phases. The whole building costs about $57 million. The City of Orlando and a federal appropriation are paying part of it too.
