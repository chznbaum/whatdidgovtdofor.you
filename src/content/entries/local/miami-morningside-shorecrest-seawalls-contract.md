Miami approved plans to build new seawalls and raise or replace old ones where streets in Morningside, Legion Park and Shorecrest meet Biscayne Bay and nearby canals. In Morningside, the project covers walls along the bay near NE 59, 61 and 62 streets. Farther north, the planned work is at NE 64 Street by the bay, NE 84 Street on the Ademar Canal, and N Bayshore Drive at the Davis Canal. The projects also include tidal valves, dredging, rock riprap, repaving, lighting, landscaping and restoration around each site.

You don't need to apply to benefit. The city says the Morningside project affects 49 properties, 47 of them residential, and protects about $41 million in property value. The city identifies Miami Forever Bond money and state Resilient Florida grants as funding sources.

The City Commission voted 5-0 on May 14, 2026, to approve Florida Engineering and Development Corporation and authorize the city manager to negotiate a phased agreement. Christine King, whose district includes the Shorecrest and Legion Park sites, chaired the meeting. The estimated contract amount is $9.28 million, but each phase needs available funding and budget approval before the city can issue a notice to proceed.

The Morningside page's June 2026 status still says the project is in design and estimates construction from July 2026 to January 2027. Shorecrest is listed at approved design, with construction estimated from winter 2026 through summer 2027. The city says both schedules may change.
