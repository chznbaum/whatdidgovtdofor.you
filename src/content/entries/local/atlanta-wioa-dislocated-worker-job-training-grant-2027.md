If you live in Atlanta or were laid off by an Atlanta employer and meet the program's dislocated-worker rules, WorkSource Atlanta has up to $1,329,568 in federal money to help you get back to work. It can help pay for a registered apprenticeship, a paid internship, a transitional job or training provided by an employer.

You have to be 18 or older. People can qualify after a no-fault layoff or plant closure. WorkSource's rules also cover some displaced homemakers, formerly self-employed people who lost work because of economic conditions or a disaster, veterans leaving the military, and people who are underemployed after a layoff. You apply through WorkSource Atlanta and provide proof that you qualify. Veterans and eligible spouses receive federal priority for service. The money comes in two parts: $285,921 for the program year that began July 1, 2026, and $1,043,647 for the federal fiscal year that began Oct. 1, 2026. Both parts run through June 30, 2028.

The money comes from the federal Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act. The state passes it to the city through the Technical College System of Georgia. Councilmember Matt Westmoreland chairs the Community Development/Human Services Committee, which brought the ordinance to the council, and he moved to adopt it. On Aug. 17, 2026, the council passed it 14-0 at a meeting run by Council President Marci Collier Overstreet. The same ordinance added the grant to the city's job training budget.
