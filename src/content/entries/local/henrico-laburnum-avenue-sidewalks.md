Henrico County is putting in sidewalks on both sides of a stretch of Laburnum Avenue. The plan calls for about 2,500 feet on the north side, between Audubon Drive and Williamsburg Road, and about 4,200 feet on the south side. Together that's about 6,700 feet of new walkway, or roughly a mile and a quarter.

The project also adds curb ramps that meet ADA standards along the corridor. People in wheelchairs, parents with strollers, and anyone on foot get a safer path that keeps them out of the road. You don't need to apply for anything. Once the sidewalks are built, anyone can use them.

The Henrico Board of Supervisors approved the work on Oct. 13, 2026, when it awarded a contract of nearly $2 million to M&F Concrete. Construction had not finished when this was last checked.
