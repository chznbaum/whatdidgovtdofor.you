Union County approved a contract for a new playground at Warinanco Park, the big county park in Elizabeth. On June 25, 2026, the county commissioners let the county manager hire Thomas & Sons Builders, which does business as DEVELOP Builders, to build it. The contract can cost up to $2,378,919.75, and the company won it through public bidding.

The playground will be free and open to anyone who visits the park. You don't have to sign up or pay to use it. The county's plan will replace the existing playground at the same site with a completely inclusive playground for children of all abilities. It will include accessible equipment, a quiet-play area, shade, fencing and a family restroom.

Commissioner James E. Baker Jr. is listed on the resolution in the meeting minutes. The board approved it 6–0, with three commissioners absent. The county's engineering, public works and facilities department will oversee the work. We couldn't find a public timeline for when the playground will open.
