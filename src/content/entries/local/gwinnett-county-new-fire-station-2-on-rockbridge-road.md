Gwinnett County is building a new fire station on Rockbridge Road in Lilburn to replace its current Fire Station 2. The new building will have three bays for fire trucks and ambulances. Headley Construction Corporation won the contract, which can cost no more than $6.94 million.

You don't need to apply for anything. If you live or work in the area Station 2 covers, the crews who answer your 911 calls will work out of the new building once it opens. The county's bid notice set a $1,000-a-day penalty the county may keep if the builder finishes late.

The contract is funded with money from Gwinnett's 2017 and 2023 SPLOST programs. SPLOST is the 1% sales tax that county voters approve for capital projects such as fire stations. The agenda packet lists $981,009 for fiscal 2026 and says the remaining $5,956,991 for fiscal 2027 and 2028 is subject to future budget approval.

The Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners approved the contract 5-0 on November 3, 2026. Commissioner Ben Ku made the motion and Commissioner Kirkland Carden seconded it. The county hasn't announced when construction will start or when the station will open.
