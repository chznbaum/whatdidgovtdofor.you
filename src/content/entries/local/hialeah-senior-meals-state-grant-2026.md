Hialeah is using state money to help pay for meals for older residents. On Sept. 22, 2026, the city accepted a $950,000 Florida Department of Elder Affairs grant and authorized $909,625 for the Hialeah Housing Authority to provide home-delivered and congregate meals. The funding covers services from July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2027.

The Housing Authority determines eligibility and enrollment. Congregate meals are served at participating senior housing communities and other approved locations. Home-delivered meals may be available to eligible older adults who are homebound and unable to attend a meal site. Contact the Housing Authority's Elderly Services–Hot Lunch Program to ask about eligibility and enrollment.

The resolution lets the city retain $40,375, or 4.25% of the grant, as an administrative fee. It also authorized a $950,000 supplemental appropriation to the city's Alliance for Aging Program budget.

The City Council approved the resolution 7-0 as part of its consent agenda. Council President Carl Zogby presided over the meeting, and Mayor Bryan Calvo signed the resolution.
