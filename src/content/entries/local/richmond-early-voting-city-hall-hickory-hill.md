If you live in Richmond, you don't have to go to the elections office on West Laburnum Avenue to vote early in a general election. You can also vote at City Hall, 900 E. Broad St., or at the Hickory Hill Community Center, 3000 E. Belt Blvd. on the Southside. Any city voter can use any of the three sites, whatever their precinct, and there's nothing to sign up for.

The sites came out of a 2020 problem. That September, the registrar's office moved out of City Hall to a bigger office on Laburnum Avenue, and people worried about how voters without cars would get there. City Council President Cynthia Newbille sponsored an ordinance that added two satellite early voting sites, and the council adopted it on Aug. 10, 2020. Both sites opened on Oct. 24, 2020. Demand was high: more than 900 people voted at Hickory Hill on a single Saturday that October, with waits close to an hour.

The sites have stayed busy. The mayor said roughly a quarter of the city's early votes in the 2022 midterms were cast at City Hall and Hickory Hill. In 2023 the city electoral board voted to close the two sites to save money, and city officials argued that only the council could close them. The board reversed itself a week later. The city listed both sites for early voting in the November 2026 general election.
