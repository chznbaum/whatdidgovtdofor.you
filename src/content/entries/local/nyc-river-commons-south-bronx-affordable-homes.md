A 17-story building is going up on what was an underused parking lot at NYC Health + Hospitals/Gotham Health, Morrisania, along River Avenue in the South Bronx. River Commons will have 328 homes, from studios to three-bedrooms. Of those, 229 are set aside for households earning 30% to 70% of the area median income. Another 98 are permanent supportive apartments for homeless patients referred through NYC Health + Hospitals, with on-site services from the nonprofit BronxWorks.

The building also brings a 43,000-square-foot clinic for Gotham Health, community space for African Communities Together and BronxWorks, and a public green space. The city expects it to open in 2029.

The project grew out of the 2018 Jerome Avenue neighborhood rezoning. In December 2021, HPD under Commissioner Louise Carroll picked Type A Projects, BronxWorks and L+M Development Partners to build it. NYC Health + Hospitals, led by Mitchell Katz, supplied the land through Housing for Health, and its board authorized the 99-year ground lease on July 31, 2025. Council Land Use Chair Rafael Salamanca Jr. sponsored the resolution approving the lease, Landmarks Subcommittee Chair Kamillah Hanks co-sponsored it, and the council passed it 46-0 on Oct. 29. HPD, under Acting Commissioner Ahmed Tigani, put in more than $100 million, and the Housing Development Corporation, led by Eric Enderlin, issued $91.3 million in bonds. Mayor Zohran Mamdani broke ground on June 29, 2026.
