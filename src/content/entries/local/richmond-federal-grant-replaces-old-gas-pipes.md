Richmond Gas Works is owned by the City of Richmond, but this project is in Henrico County. The grant will replace about 13.25 miles of old gas mains with polyethylene and protected steel pipes. PHMSA says replacing the leak-prone pipes should reduce the risk of gas leaks and improve air quality.

You do not need to apply. The grant agreement lists a $15,733,481 federal share and no required local cost share, so Richmond Gas Works does not have to provide matching funds for this project. The award's performance period runs from April 2025 through April 2030.

PHMSA selected the project for funding in October 2024 through a program created by the 2021 Bipartisan Infrastructure Law. Richmond Gas Works Director Daniel Rifenburgh and Department of Public Utilities colleagues submitted the acceptance ordinance through Mayor Danny Avula's administration. City Council adopted it 9-0 on July 28, 2025. PHMSA completed the required environmental review on April 8, 2026; the reviewed records do not show when physical construction began.

This grant is separate from two earlier Richmond Gas Works awards: $10 million in 2023 and $39 million in 2024. The city said those two awards together would repair, rehabilitate, or replace nearly 50 miles of pipe.
