Orange County is building about 1.2 miles of new trail along Shingle Creek, from Destination Parkway south to State Road 528. The path will be 12 to 14 feet wide, so there's room for people walking, running, and biking. About 2,031 feet of it will be boardwalk, and the rest will be paved. All pedestrian facilities along the route will be built to current disability-access standards.

Once it opens, people will be able to use this public trail without applying. This stretch is one of two missing links between the first segment of the trail, which opened near Sand Lake Road in 2019, and the existing trail farther south. When Orange County's part of the Shingle Creek Trail is finished, it will run about 9.3 miles. It's part of a 32-mile trail through Orange County, Osceola County, Orlando, and Kissimmee, and it will link to the statewide Coast-to-Coast Trail.

On Aug. 25, 2026, the Board of County Commissioners, chaired by Mayor Jerry Demings, approved an agreement with the Florida Department of Transportation. It added $3,859,268 in grant money, bringing the total to $10,168,886, and the county doesn't have to put up any matching money. Construction began Oct. 19, 2026.
