Richmond agreed to help pay for a new apartment community on a mostly vacant, roughly 7-acre site off Semmes Avenue. The site at McDonough Street and West 22nd Street was once a mobile home park. Under the deal, developer Elmington must complete a project with at least 246 apartments. Occupancy and rents for 246 units must follow Virginia Housing standards, with an average income designation of 60% of the Richmond-area median income. The city describes the unit designations as ranging from 50% to 80% of area median income. The restrictions must last at least 30 years.

Prospective tenants will have to meet the income limit assigned to a unit; the available sources do not yet provide leasing instructions. Construction is underway. In November 2026, Elmington Construction reported work on all three buildings, including framing and roof installation.

The grant doesn't come out of today's city budget. The city pays it only from the new property tax the finished buildings bring in, and only if they're built and kept up as promised. The city keeps the tax the land already pays. A 2022 state law made this kind of deal possible by letting economic development authorities give grants for affordable housing.

Mayor Danny Avula's administration proposed the agreement. City Council approved it 9–0 on Oct. 14, 2025.
