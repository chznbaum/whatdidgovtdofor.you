Two of Saint Paul's oldest neighborhood libraries are getting $1 million in federal money toward renovations. The money comes from the 2024 federal spending law, which Congress passed in March 2024. It is split evenly: $500,000 for Hayden Heights Library on White Bear Avenue and $500,000 for Riverview Library on George Street. Neither building had been renovated in more than 30 years when these projects were planned.

Hayden Heights closed in March 2026 for a renovation expected to take up to 18 months. The plans include large front windows, a flexible community room, study rooms, garden areas, and rooftop solar panels. Riverview is a 1917 Carnegie library. Its plans add an accessible glass entrance, a community room, and an outdoor reading plaza. As of August 2026, the city had not secured all the money needed to build the Riverview renovation. You do not need to apply to use either free public library.

Rep. Betty McCollum and Sens. Amy Klobuchar and Tina Smith were listed as the congressional requestors for both projects in the final appropriations table. President Joe Biden signed the spending law on March 9, 2024. On Nov. 4, 2026, the Library Board accepted the money and added it to the library's 2026 budget, allowing the city to spend it. Library Board chair Saura Jost sponsored that resolution.
