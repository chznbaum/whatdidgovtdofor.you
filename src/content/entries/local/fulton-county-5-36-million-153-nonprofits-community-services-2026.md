Fulton County is spending $5,363,958 of its own general fund on 2026 contracts with 153 nonprofits. The money goes to the groups' services for county residents. The county lists the category shares in rounded amounts: $2 million for children and youth services, $831,000 each for economic stability and poverty programs and homelessness services, $681,000 for health and wellness, $447,000 for seniors, $298,000 for people with disabilities and $250,000 for veterans housing.

The groups include food providers like the Atlanta Community Food Bank, Meals On Wheels Atlanta and Open Hand Atlanta. The list also includes Atlanta Legal Aid Society, the Atlanta Volunteer Lawyers Foundation, homeless shelters and youth clubs. You don't apply to the county for this help. You get it through whichever nonprofit serves you. The contracts cover Jan. 1 through Dec. 31, 2026.

The county asked nonprofits for proposals early in the year, and a review committee recommended which ones to fund. Commissioners had set aside the full amount in January. On May 20, 2026, the Board of Commissioners, chaired by Robb Pitts, approved the committee's picks 6–0.
