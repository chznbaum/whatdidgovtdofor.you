If you work for Henrico County Public Schools, your raise for the 2026-27 school year is 4%, not 3%. That covers every school employee, not just teachers. You don't have to apply; the higher raise comes through your paycheck.

When the county passed its budget in the spring, it planned a 3% raise for school employees. The state budget later funded the state share of up to a 4% raise for Standards of Quality-funded instructional and support positions. That funding did not cover every HCPS position, so some employees would have remained at 3% without additional local funding.

On June 23, the Henrico Board of Supervisors said it wanted every schools employee to get the same 4%. On July 28, the board passed Resolution 166-26, which changed the county's 2026-27 budget and added $6.1 million to pay for the extra 1% for all school employees. Of that additional money, about $4.9 million came through the state budget and Henrico provided the remaining $1.2 million.
