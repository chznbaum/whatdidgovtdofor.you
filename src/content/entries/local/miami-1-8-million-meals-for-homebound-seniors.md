Miami is putting up to $1,787,786 toward meals delivered to the homes of older residents who can't easily get out. The money goes to Sunshine for All, a Miami nonprofit that runs the Senior Meals Program. It includes a wellness check with every delivery. Meals are free to the seniors who get them.

Sunshine for All says seniors apply through their city district. It asks applicants to be 62 or older and to bring a state ID and a Social Security or SSI letter. To ask about signing up, call Sunshine for All at (305) 285-3217.

The money comes from District 1's share of the city's Anti-Poverty Initiative. The City Commission created that program in 2014 to help poor residents, and services for older people are one of its priorities. Each commission district gets a share based on its poverty rate. District 1 covers Flagami, Allapattah and parts of Little Havana, and its commissioner is Miguel Angel Gabela. After a public hearing on Oct. 8, 2026, the commission skipped competitive bidding, which takes a four-fifths vote. It approved the grant 5-0 on a motion by Commissioner Rolando Escalona, seconded by Gabela.
