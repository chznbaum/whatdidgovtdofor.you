If you live in one of the 100 subsidized apartments at Santa Fe Villas and remain eligible, your rent help continues through 2027. The city pays part of each tenant's rent straight to the owner, G&S Santa Fe Villas, LLC. The money comes from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's Section 8 Moderate Rehabilitation program, which Atlanta runs. These homes are set aside for low-income people who were formerly homeless.

You don't apply to the city for the subsidy. It's tied to the apartment. People who want a unit fill out a preapplication at the Santa Fe Villas leasing office. Those who qualify go on a waiting list.

The new contract covers Jan. 1 through Dec. 31, 2027. The ordinance authorizes up to $1,165,008 for rent subsidies across the 100 apartments. The Community Development/Human Services Committee advanced it 6-0 on Sept. 29. With Council President Marci Collier Overstreet presiding, the full council adopted it 11-0 on Oct. 5, 2026. The federal grant pays for the subsidies, so the renewal does not take money from other city services.
