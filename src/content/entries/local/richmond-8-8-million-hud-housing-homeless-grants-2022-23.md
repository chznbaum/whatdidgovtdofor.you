Each year Richmond gets a set of federal grants from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development and decides how to spend them. This ordinance accepted $8.83 million for the year that ran from July 2022 through June 2023 and put it into the city's budget for projects in its annual action plan.

The money came in four parts. $4.71 million in Community Development Block Grant funds went to neighborhood and housing projects for lower-income residents. $1.92 million in HOME funds went to building, fixing up, or helping people afford homes. $399,434 in Emergency Solutions Grant money went to homeless services. $1.79 million in Housing Opportunities for Persons with AIDS funds paid for housing help for people living with HIV. Federal rules give that HIV housing money to the region's largest city to serve the whole metro area, and people generally qualify only if their income is low. Residents usually get this help by applying to the city or to the nonprofits it funds, not from the city directly.

Mayor Levar Stoney's administration brought the plan to City Council, and the council adopted it on June 27, 2022.
