An 18-story building with 229 affordable apartments for seniors is going up on open land at NYCHA's Morris Houses in the Bronx. Called Sol on Park, it will set aside 80 apartments for seniors who already live at Morris Houses I and II, 69 for formerly homeless seniors, and 79 for low- and middle-income seniors chosen through the city's Housing Connect lottery. The building will also have more than 8,000 square feet of community space, including a health center open to the public and a new office for the Morris Houses tenant association.

Morris seniors who want to move can apply, and NYCHA picks residents by lottery. Those who move switch from public housing to project-based Section 8 but keep the same rent formula and basic rights. No public housing is torn down. The city says NYCHA can refill the apartments residents leave with public housing assistance, so those homes go to new tenants.

The city chose the development team, led by The NRP Group with Selfhelp Realty Group and Foxy Management, in May 2022, when Adolfo Carrión Jr. ran the city's housing agency. The Morris Houses tenant association, led by Regina Carter, negotiated the 80 apartments for current residents. NYCHA under Lisa Bova-Hiatt, the housing agency under Dina Levy, and the Housing Development Corporation under Eric Enderlin worked together on the $214 million project. Mayor Zohran Mamdani's administration closed the financing in July 2026 and broke ground on Sept. 29. Move-ins are expected in early 2029.
