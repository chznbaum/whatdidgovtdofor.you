Chesterfield County is building a new sidewalk on River Road, on the south side of the road between Hickory Road and Brickhouse Drive. It will run about 1,730 feet and be 5 to 10 feet wide, with four marked crosswalks. People who walk there now have no sidewalk and share the road with traffic.

The work goes beyond pouring concrete. The project adds curb and gutter, improves drainage, rebuilds driveway entrances, and repaves and re-marks the road. It also takes out an old asbestos-cement water line and adjusts the water main and service lines. Nobody has to apply. Once it's finished, the sidewalk is open to anyone.

On May 27, 2026, the Board of Supervisors voted 5–0 to award a $2,564,589 construction contract to North Construction, Inc. The board also let the county's procurement director approve change orders up to the project's full budget. Supervisors Mark Miller, Kevin Carroll, Jim Ingle, Jessica Schneider and LeQuan Hylton all voted yes. The county's latest update, dated October 27, said the contractor was expected to start in early November 2026 and finish by August 2027.
