If you work for the City of St. Petersburg in a blue-collar or white-collar job covered by the Florida Public Services Union, your pay went up 3.5% starting Sept. 21, 2026. You don't have to apply. The raise comes with the new union contract, which runs through Sept. 16, 2029. Step raises continue, and the union can reopen wages for bargaining in summer 2027.

The contract does more than raise pay. You get 40 hours a year of leave to care for an ill member of your immediate family. Your union stewards get more time to file grievances on your behalf, and there can be more union stewards on the job. Big changes to the contract, benefits or working conditions now have to go to workers for a ratification vote.

The union bargained the deal with the city. Workers in the two units voted to ratify it Sept. 14–16, 2026. The next day, the City Council approved it 8-0 at a meeting chaired by Council Chair Lisset Hanewicz, and it took effect Sept. 21. The last contract for these workers ran from 2023 to 2026.
