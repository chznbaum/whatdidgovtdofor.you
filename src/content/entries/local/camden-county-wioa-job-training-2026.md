Camden County added $5,070,510 in federal money to its budget for job training and career services. The money comes through the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA), the main federal program that pays for local job centers, and covers the county's Program Year 2026. New Jersey's budget rules let a county add a grant like this to a budget that has already been adopted, so the county can spend it.

The money supports the Camden County One-Stop Career Center. There, job seekers can get career coaching and help with resumes, take skills assessments, and train for jobs that are in demand. It also runs a Youth One-Stop for young people ages 16 to 24 who are out of school. To get training help, you go through the One-Stop: it holds an orientation and checks whether you qualify under WIOA. The county's training services line is 856-549-0626.

The Board of Commissioners approved the resolution on July 16, 2026, as part of a block of items. Every commissioner present voted yes in a roll call. Deputy Director Edward T. McDonnell presided because Director Louis Cappelli Jr. was excused.
