If you or someone in your household is living with HIV and money is tight, this funding can help you keep a roof over your head. Orange County approved one-year contracts totaling $5,296,465 with six local nonprofits for the program, called Housing Opportunities for Persons with AIDS (HOPWA). Depending on what you need, it can help cover rent, mortgage or utility bills for a short time, cover a deposit and first month's rent on a new place, or give longer-term rent help where you pay 30% of your income. Homeless households can get hotel stays or shared supportive housing.

You qualify if someone in your household has HIV, your household earns no more than 80% of the area median income, and you live in Orange, Osceola, Seminole or Lake County. To get help, you meet with a housing case manager at one of the nonprofits, who checks whether you're eligible. The county says some of its programs are full and not taking new clients.

The money is a federal grant from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development to the City of Orlando, and Orange County runs the program on Orlando's behalf. The approved contracts go to Aspire Health Partners, Center for Multicultural Wellness and Prevention, Hope and Help of Central Florida, Miracle of Love, St. Francis House of Hospitality and Zebra Youth. With Vice Mayor Kelly Martinez Semrad chairing because Mayor Jerry Demings was absent, the Board of County Commissioners approved them 4-0 on Aug. 4, 2026. The initial term is one year, and the county authorized up to two one-year renewals.
