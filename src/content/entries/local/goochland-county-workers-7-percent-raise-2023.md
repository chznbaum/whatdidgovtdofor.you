If you worked for Goochland County government in 2023, your pay went up 7% in the budget year that started that July. The raise covered every county employee, and nobody had to apply. Sheriff's deputies and Fire-Rescue staff got an extra 4% on top of that, and the officers who answer 911 and dispatch emergency crews got an extra 6%.

The raises came from changes the Board of Supervisors made to the county administrator's proposed budget before adopting it. The board added $864,556 so that the cost-of-living raise for all employees came to 7% in total and so that the extra public-safety raises were covered. It also added four new full-time positions and money for a new ambulance.

The board paid for these additions without cutting services. It counted $1.375 million more in real estate tax revenue and $580,000 more in interest income than first projected, and it drew $750,000 from the county's savings.

Supervisor Ken Peterson made the motion and Susan Lascolette seconded it. Under Chair Neil Spoonhower, the board adopted the amended budget 5-0 on April 18, 2023.
