Bellevue Gardens is a new apartment complex with 78 apartments on Rosedale Avenue, on Richmond's Northside next to I-95. Every apartment has rent set for a household earning between 30% and 80% of the area median income. Forty-two are for households at 50% or below. Rents run from about $600 a month for a two-bedroom at the lowest income level to about $1,600 for a three-bedroom at the highest. There are one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments.

Some apartments carry extra help. Nineteen have project-based Section 8 vouchers through the Richmond Redevelopment and Housing Authority. Five are for people in the Richmond Behavioral Health Authority's permanent supportive housing program. Renters apply through the property's management, and the building was nearly full soon after it opened.

The $27.4 million project was financed mostly with Low-Income Housing Tax Credit equity and Virginia Housing loans. The city also provided $750,000 through its Equitable Affordable Housing Program. In September 2024, City Council approved the first ordinance authorizing a Bellevue Gardens performance-grant agreement, proposed by Mayor Levar Stoney and co-patroned by Council Vice President Ann-Frances Lambert. Mayor Danny Avula brought Council a second ordinance in 2025 for the development's current 3940 Rosedale Avenue address. The agreement returns the increase above the property's 2025 real estate tax bill for up to 30 years, but only after the developer pays its taxes and follows the affordability rules. Council adopted it on Nov. 10, 2025. City and state housing officials marked the formal opening on May 4, 2026.
