Newark is paying a local provider, Emergency Housing Services, to give homeless residents a place to stay through the city's Emergency Housing Program. The program serves individuals and families. The contract runs from April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027, and can pay up to $4.23 million.

The city awarded $706,990 up front to cover April and May 2026. The rest of the contract depends on the city having the money, so the full $4.23 million isn't guaranteed. The city's Office of Homeless Services, part of the Department of Health and Community Wellness, oversees the contract. The resolution does not say whether residents apply directly to the provider or enter through a city or county referral.

The city picked the provider through a public request for proposals that closed in February 2026. Council President C. Lawrence Crump led the Municipal Council on June 17, 2026, when it voted 8-0 to approve the contract, with one member absent. Because the contract started in April, the vote ratified it after it began and also authorized the rest of the term.
