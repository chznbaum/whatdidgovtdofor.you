DeKalb County commissioners approved a contract award to Diversified Construction of Georgia, a Tucker company and the lowest of six bidders, for up to $9.9 million. The work covers the site, utilities, and a new Lucious Sanders Recreation Center at Lithonia Park. The 480-day contract term starts when the contract is executed.

Plans announced in 2025 call for a gym with several courts, fitness and wellness studios, rooms for arts and classes, a technology lab, and a fully accessible design. The building is planned for public use, but organized county programs such as camps and youth sports may require registration and fees.

District 5 Commissioner Mereda Davis Johnson announced the project in August 2025 and said she allocated more than $2 million in district funds toward it. Super District 7 Commissioner LaDena Bolton publicly supported the project. The contract uses money from SPLOST II, the existing one-cent sales tax DeKalb voters approved in 2023. The Board of Commissioners approved the award 6–0 on Nov. 17, 2026, with Commissioner Michelle Long Spears absent. The center is named for Lucious Sanders, a World War II veteran and civil rights leader in Lithonia.
