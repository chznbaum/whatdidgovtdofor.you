Gwinnett County is building new sidewalks along stretches of two roads. One goes along the east side of Old Rockbridge Road, from Buford Highway to the park entrance. The other goes along the west side of Williams Road, from Bishop Circle to Singleton Road. The project also improves drainage and replaces a water main along Williams Road.

You don't need to apply or do anything. Once construction is finished, anyone walking those stretches will be able to use the new sidewalks.

The Board of Commissioners, chaired by Nicole Love Hendrickson, awarded the contract to Ohmshiv Construction on Sept. 1, 2026, by a 5-0 vote. The county will pay no more than $1,789,241. About 84% of the money comes from the county's 2017 SPLOST, a special local sales tax for capital projects. The rest comes from the county's Water and Sewer Renewal and Extension Fund, which pays for the water main work.
