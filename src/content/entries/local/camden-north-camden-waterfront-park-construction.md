North Camden is getting a new park on the Delaware River. Camden hired Capela Construction to build Sadler's Poynt/North Camden Waterfront Park, at N. 6th Street and Byron Street, for $5,770,209. The work includes a greenway path for walking and biking, lighting, fencing, landscaping, stormwater improvements, a parking lot, and fixed and floating docks.

The site contains historic fill. Crews will clear debris across the whole site and cover it with a foot of certified clean soil, which seals off what's underneath before the public uses the land. The removed material goes to a licensed disposal facility.

The park will be open to the public, and nobody has to apply to use it. It is a federal Transportation Alternatives project, run with the New Jersey Department of Transportation. A waterfront park for North Camden has been in neighborhood plans for years.

City Council approved the contract 7-0 in September 2026, with Council President Angel Fuentes presiding. The city is hiring a separate firm to manage and inspect the construction. No opening date has been announced.
