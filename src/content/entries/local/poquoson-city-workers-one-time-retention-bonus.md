If you held a permanent job with the City of Poquoson on June 30, 2026, you qualified for a one-time bonus if you were still employed on Sept. 8 and were not in a two-week notice period on that date. Eligible full-time permanent employees get $1,500, and eligible permanent part-time employees get $750. The city calls it a retention bonus, meant to help keep the people who run city services from leaving.

The bonuses are paid for with $212,779 in carryover funds from the previous budget year. The minutes say that year ended in a good financial position, making the money available for the bonuses. The council added the funds to the budget for the fiscal year that began in July 2026. Unlike a permanent raise that requires ongoing revenue, the bonus is a one-time expense.

On Aug. 10, 2026, Mayor Hux moved to approve the extra spending and Councilman Southall seconded. The council approved it 7-0. Councilmembers Hubbard, Southall, Freeman, Emmett and Feigh, Vice Mayor Bunting and Mayor Hux all voted yes.
