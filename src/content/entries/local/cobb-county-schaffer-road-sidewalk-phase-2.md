Cobb County awarded Tetrabuild Infracon, LLC a construction contract for about 1,300 feet, or one-quarter mile, of sidewalk along the north side of Schaffer Road. It will run between the Center for Children and Young Adults and Favor Road, giving people walking that segment a path off the roadway. You will not need to apply to use the public sidewalk.

The contract caps the county's payment to Tetrabuild at $719,081.56. The project is listed in Cobb's 2022 SPLOST transportation program. Cobb voters approved that one-cent sales tax for six years beginning in 2022, with part of its revenue dedicated to transportation projects.

The Cobb County Board of Commissioners approved the contract 5–0 on Oct. 13, 2026. Commissioner Erick Allen made the motion, and Commissioner Keli Gambrill seconded it. The board also authorized Chairwoman Lisa Cupid to execute the necessary contract documents.
