Chatham County approved a construction contract to rebuild the Skidaway Road intersection at Norwood and Ferguson avenues. New sidewalks will run along both sides of Skidaway Road there, so people on foot won't have to walk in the road or on the grass shoulder. The traffic signal is getting the turn lanes and signal phases the intersection needs, and new drainage will carry rainwater away.

You don't need to do anything to benefit. The work covers the intersection itself, which sits in County Commission Districts 1 and 3.

This is the first phase of a larger plan to improve walking and traffic on Skidaway Road between Montgomery Cross Road and Ferguson Avenue. The county split the plan into two projects. The second one, at Montgomery Cross Road, is still being designed. The county started buying land for the Ferguson project in fall 2023 and finished the engineering design in 2026.

On Oct. 16, 2026, the Chatham County Board of Commissioners approved a $3,853,890 construction contract with Astra Group, LLC. Chairman Chester A. Ellis led the board that approved it. Astra submitted the lowest of seven responsive bids; one additional bid was marked non-responsive. The money comes from SPLOST, the voter-approved local sales tax set aside for capital projects like roads and drainage.
