The Eastern Parkway Library at 1044 Eastern Parkway has served Crown Heights since 1914. Its Carnegie building is being renovated and expanded at a cost of $36 million. When the work is done, the branch will have more than 50% more public space. The historic building will remain, with a new glass-fronted addition bringing in more natural light.

The new branch will be fully accessible, with an elevator and entrance ramp. It will have a main reading room, a teen zone, two children's areas, a STEAM room, a community room, and a classroom in the restored fireplace room. A renovated courtyard will add outdoor seating. New lighting, plumbing, heating and cooling, and fire alarms will update the building's systems.

The branch is closed during construction. Brooklyn Public Library is providing nearby bookmobile service. A temporary pop-up library hosted by Settlement Housing Fund was expected to open in fall 2026 with books, programs, and public computers, but its opening had not been confirmed in public sources by Nov. 24.

Council Members Darlene Mealy, Mathieu Eugene, and Laurie Cumbo backed a FY2018 request to upgrade and expand the branch. Mayor Eric Adams later proposed $29 million for the overhaul in his FY2024 executive budget. The City Council, Brooklyn Borough President Antonio Reynoso, and the State Assembly also supplied funding. DDC awarded the construction contract in July 2025, and work began under Commissioner Paul Ochoa in August 2026. The city expects to finish in summer 2028.
