If you're homeless in Tampa and your income is low, EPIC can help you rent a place of your own. The city approved up to $1,275,000 over three years for EPIC to provide rental assistance and related services. At least 22 households must receive tenant-based rental assistance during the contract.

This is tenant-based rental assistance: the help is tied to the household, not to a particular building. To qualify, a household must meet HUD's definition of homeless, live in Tampa, and have income at or below 80% of the area median. How much rent help a household receives depends on its income and approved rent. EPIC takes applications, and the city lists 813-237-3066 as its number.

The money comes from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's HOME program. Tampa has $425,000 available for the first year. Of that, $280,500 is budgeted for tenant-based rental assistance and $144,500 for delivering the program, including case management and support costs. The remaining $850,000 depends on future HOME grants and annual council approval.

The Tampa City Council approved the agreement on Nov. 5, 2026, with no votes against. Council member Bill Carlson made the motion and Guido Maniscalco seconded it. The resolution authorizes the mayor to execute the agreement for the city.
