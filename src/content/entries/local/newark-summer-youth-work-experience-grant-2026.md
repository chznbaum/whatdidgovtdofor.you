New Jersey's Summer Youth Work Experience Program pays young people to work with local employers over the summer. Under the 2026 state grant, placements had to run 160 to 200 hours, and participants received job-readiness training. The program served people ages 16 to 24. The state funds it, and cities and workforce boards run it locally. In Newark it goes through the city's One Stop Career Center, which took 2026 summer job applications through April 27.

In May 2026, Newark accepted a state award of $829,600 for the program. The state Department of Labor and Workforce Development later sent a revised award that nearly tripled that amount, to $2,379,893. On July 15, 2026, the Municipal Council voted to accept the larger amount. The grant period ran from April 1 through October 31, 2026, and the city paid nothing toward it.

Council members Dupré L. Kelly and Louise Scott-Rountree are listed as the resolution's sponsors. The state has already opened the next round of funding: for summer 2027, it is putting $9 million into the program statewide and lowering the minimum age to 14.
