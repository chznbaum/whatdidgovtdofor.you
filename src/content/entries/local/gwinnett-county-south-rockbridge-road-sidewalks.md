Gwinnett County is building new sidewalks on the east side of South Rockbridge Road. They will run from the DeKalb County line north to North Deshong Road. The county says the sidewalks will make it easier to walk to Deshong Park and to nearby businesses without stepping into traffic.

You don't need to do anything to use them. The work is paid for by the 2017 SPLOST, a one-cent sales tax that Gwinnett voters approved for roads, sidewalks and other projects. That means it doesn't come out of property taxes. Under the contract, builder CMC, Inc. will be paid no more than $3,428,430.

The Board of Commissioners approved the contract 5-0 on Aug. 4, 2026, with Chairwoman Nicole Love Hendrickson presiding. Commissioners Kirkland Carden, Ben Ku, Jasper Watkins and Matthew Holtkamp also voted yes. On Sept. 2, the county described the sidewalks as forthcoming; we found no later report that they are finished.
