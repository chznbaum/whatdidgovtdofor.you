The City of Miami runs the federal Housing Opportunities for Persons With AIDS program for all of Miami-Dade County, not just the city. This vote allocated $17.26 million for the year that began Oct. 1, 2026: $14.76 million in new federal grant money plus $2.5 million in unallocated program income from previous years.

Most of the money, about $16.55 million, supports long-term tenant-based rental assistance, including rent payments, housing specialists and inspections. With tenant-based assistance, a household picks its own apartment, the unit has to pass an inspection, and the program pays part of the rent. Smaller amounts go to short-term help with rent, mortgage and utility bills, help moving into permanent housing, and operating support for permanent housing facilities. To qualify, a household must live in Miami-Dade, earn no more than 80% of the area median income, and include someone with an AIDS diagnosis. Everyone who receives HOPWA assistance also gets case management. Questions go to the city at 311 or 305-468-5900.

The city commission approved the allocation 5-0 on July 9, 2026, with modifications. Chairwoman Christine King, who seconded the motion, was presiding. Commissioners Miguel Angel Gabela, Damian Pardo, Rolando Escalona and Ralph Rosado also voted yes.
