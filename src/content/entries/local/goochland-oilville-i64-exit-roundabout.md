Drivers getting off westbound I-64 at Oilville Road meet an intersection with no signal, and traffic backs up there in the evening rush. Goochland County says that backup can cause safety problems on the interstate itself. VDOT plans to replace the intersection with a single-lane roundabout. Drivers heading north on Oilville Road will get a bypass lane where they yield and turn right. All the work stays on land VDOT already owns, so no one's property has to be taken.

You don't need to do anything to benefit. The project is meant to get cars off the interstate faster and make the intersection safer for everyone who uses the exit.

Then-U.S. Rep. Abigail Spanberger requested and secured a $4 million earmark for the roundabout in Congress's fiscal 2023 spending bill. On Feb. 6, 2024, the Goochland Board of Supervisors voted 5-0 to commit the county to paying its local share under an agreement with VDOT. Supervisor Neil Spoonhower made the motion. VDOT estimates the total cost at $7.5 million. Its last project update, dated March 20, 2026, still listed the project in design. The county's current project page lists construction for 2026-2027 but does not say that construction has begun.
