Route 1 is Tampa's busiest bus line. It runs from the Marion Transit Center downtown, north on Florida Avenue, then east on Fletcher Avenue to the University Area Transit Center near USF. It connects to about 70% of HART's network. Under this agreement, the City of Tampa reimburses HART up to $1.35 million for documented operating costs of the additional Route 1 service.

Riders don't need to sign up for anything. Buses come every 15 minutes from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekdays. They come every 20 minutes from 6 to 9 p.m. on weekday evenings and from 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. on weekends. In 2025, city money made the route free for a year, and daily ridership climbed from about 3,000 to about 5,000 rides. Fares returned on Jan. 5, 2026, at $2 a ride, with a $4 daily cap and a $65 monthly cap. HART said the city's money would keep and expand frequent service rather than extend free rides.

The council had already set aside the $1.35 million in its fiscal year 2026 budget. On Sept. 3, 2026, it unanimously approved the reimbursement agreement, which applies retroactively from Jan. 4, 2026, through Jan. 2, 2027. Council Chair Alan Clendenin presided over the vote.
