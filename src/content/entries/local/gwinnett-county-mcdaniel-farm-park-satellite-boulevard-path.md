Getting to McDaniel Farm Park from Satellite Boulevard on foot or by bike is about to get easier. Gwinnett County is building a new path along Commerce Avenue that will connect Satellite Boulevard to the park's existing trails. The work also includes new curb and gutter and drainage improvements along the road.

The Gwinnett Place Community Improvement District plans a path 10 to 12 feet wide. It is meant to give people a way to reach the park from the Gwinnett Place Mall area without driving, and to connect later to a possible transit stop on Commerce Avenue and to the county's planned Loop Trail. The path is free and open to anyone, with nothing to apply for.

The Board of Commissioners, chaired by Nicole Love Hendrickson, voted 4-0 on Oct. 6, 2026 to award the construction contract to Ohmshiv Construction for no more than $3,648,881. Commissioner Jasper Watkins was absent. The money comes from various SPLOST programs, the 1% sales tax Gwinnett voters approved for specified capital projects. By law, SPLOST cannot be used for day-to-day operating expenses. Construction is expected to begin in late 2026.
