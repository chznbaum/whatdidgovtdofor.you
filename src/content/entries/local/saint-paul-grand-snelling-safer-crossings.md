The corner of Grand Avenue and Snelling Avenue is in Saint Paul's Macalester-Groveland area. In 2026 the city rebuilt it. Each of the four corners got a curb bumpout to improve crossings for people on foot, and the old traffic signal was replaced. The crew also did sewer and water work and repaved the street. You don't need to do anything to benefit. The changes are there for anyone walking, biking, catching a bus or driving through.

Snelling is also State Highway 51, so the state had a say in work along it. In June 2026 the Saint Paul City Council approved an agreement under which the Minnesota Department of Transportation pays part of the cost of the work on and along Snelling. The agreement also defines the city's responsibilities for operating and maintaining the improvements. Council Member Saura Jost, whose ward includes the area, sponsored the resolution. The council adopted it on June 17, and Mayor Kaohly Her's office signed it the next day.

Construction began July 13, 2026. Grand Avenue reopened to through traffic on August 27, and the new signals were switched on September 23. As of the city's October 16 update, the work was substantially complete, with only minor repairs and pavement patching left. The project followed the 2025 rebuild of Grand Avenue between Fairview and Snelling.
