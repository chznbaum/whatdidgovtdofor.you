This contract authorized Circle of Life Treatment Center, a Newark nonprofit, to provide part of the city's emergency housing program for homeless individuals and families from April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027.

The council authorized up to $1,012,510, but initially certified only $169,214 for services in April and May 2026. The remaining balance depends on available funds and later appropriation. The council may approve as many as two six-month extensions if grant money is available and the services are effective. NJ 211's current listing for Circle of Life says people seeking its shelter need a County Welfare/DFAB referral and should call for an intake assessment; residents do not apply for the city grant itself.

The Department of Health and Community Wellness advertised a request for proposals in January 2026, with proposals due February 10, and found Circle of Life's proposal advantageous under the stated criteria. Because work began before the vote, the resolution also ratified services provided since April 1. The Municipal Council adopted it on June 17, 2026, while C. Lawrence Crump was council president.
