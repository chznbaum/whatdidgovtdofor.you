The speed limit is now 35 mph on 10 stretches of South Richmond road. They include Huguenot Road from Riverside Drive to the Chesterfield line, Richmond Highway from Hopkins Road to the county line, Forest Hill Avenue from Sheila Lane to the county line, Broad Rock Boulevard and Ironbridge Road near the county line, Belt Boulevard, Warwick Road, Commerce Road, the Manchester Bridge, and part of U.S. Route 1. When the city began studying these roads in 2019, several were posted at 40 mph, and Huguenot Road and parts of Broad Rock and Commerce were at 45.

You don't have to do anything. The lower limits apply to everyone on those roads. Slower traffic makes crashes less likely to injure people, which matters most to people on foot, on bikes and at bus stops along these wide roads. The city's 2025 speed-limit database showed no city street above 35 mph except Chippenham Parkway.

Council Member Michael Jones, whose 9th District holds most of these roads, pushed for the change for years. In 2019 the council unanimously passed his resolution ordering a traffic study of a 35 mph maximum. He introduced this ordinance in January 2022, and the council adopted it on April 11, 2022.
