If you're living with HIV in the Newark area, this contract helps pay for your care at Rutgers' Infectious Disease Practice, the HIV clinic at University Hospital. The money comes from the federal Ryan White HIV/AIDS Program. Newark runs the program for a five-county area: Essex, Union, Morris, Sussex and Warren. The council authorized up to $1,356,554 for care from March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027. So far $678,288 has been certified. Any additional money is subject to available funds, a progress review and the health department director's approval.

The clinic sees patients 16 and older, whether or not they can pay. Services include primary HIV care, help sticking with medications, case management, mental health and addiction counseling (including Suboxone), hepatitis care, women's health, and support groups. A financial counselor helps patients sign up for Medicaid, the state's AIDS drug program, charity care and other aid. To get care, you contact the clinic directly.

Newark's health department picked the clinic through a fair and open request for proposals. On May 20, 2026, the Municipal Council approved the contract 9–0, with Council President C. Lawrence Crump presiding. Earlier, on Feb. 18, 2026, the council had voted to accept nearly $8 million in Ryan White funds for the region's HIV services.
