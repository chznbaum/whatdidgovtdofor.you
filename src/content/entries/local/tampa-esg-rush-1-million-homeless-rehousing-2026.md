Tampa received a second-round $1,066,228 grant from HUD's Rapid Unsheltered Survivor Housing program to address the continuing housing and homelessness effects of Hurricanes Helene and Milton. RUSH fills gaps in federal disaster assistance for people experiencing homelessness or at risk of it who cannot get all the help they need from programs such as FEMA.

The city's plan sets aside $400,000 for rapid-rehousing rental assistance for 50 households, $191,757 for homelessness prevention for 100 households, and $394,504 for street outreach expected to reach 400 people. Another $79,967 is for administration. The city plans to use partner agencies it already works with. Staff said the grant requires no local match and must be spent within 24 months, around July 2028.

The City Council, chaired by Alan Clendenin, approved the plan change and its submission to HUD on June 11, 2026. Charlie Miranda moved it and Luis Viera seconded. It passed unanimously, with Lynn Hurtak absent, and no members of the public spoke at the hearing. A later city action plan said implementation was underway.
