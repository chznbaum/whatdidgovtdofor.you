The project is meant to fill sidewalk gaps around the Pulse rapid-bus stop at West Broad and Cleveland streets. It will add sidewalks, curbs, accessible curb ramps and crosswalks at locations within the station's half-mile walkshed. The construction solicitation also includes pedestrian lighting, street furniture and drainage work to improve access and safety.

You do not need to apply for anything. The improvements will be on public streets for anyone walking or rolling through the area or using the Pulse stop.

Mayor Levar Stoney was the ordinance's original patron when it was introduced in January 2023. Council member Katherine Jordan joined as a co-patron before City Council adopted it on Feb. 13, 2023. The ordinance authorized Richmond to execute a project agreement with the Virginia Department of Transportation. The city later reported that $1.6 million in federal and state Smart Scale funds had been secured, with no city matching funds required.

The Department of Public Works put construction out to bid on June 15, 2026. Bids closed July 28, and the solicitation allows 270 consecutive days for construction after the city issues a notice to proceed. As of Nov. 18, 2026, the latest sources reviewed did not confirm a construction start.
