If you work for the City of New York and get your health insurance through MetroPlusHealth's Gold Plan, your plan will cover doula care starting Jan. 1, 2027. A doula is a trained support person, not a medical provider, who helps you before, during and after birth. For each pregnancy, the plan covers up to eight doula visits during pregnancy or after the baby arrives, plus one in-person visit to support you through labor and delivery.

The Gold Plan is open to all City employees, their qualified domestic partners and eligible dependents, and to retirees who aren't yet on Medicare. It has no monthly premium and no deductible. You don't apply separately: the doula benefit comes with the plan.

MetroPlusHealth added the benefit at the request of the city Health Department, led by Commissioner Dr. Alister Martin, and NYC Health + Hospitals, led by Dr. Mitchell Katz. MetroPlusHealth CEO Dr. Talya Schwartz announced it on July 1, 2026, along with other Gold Plan changes, including $0 urgent care visits. Mayor Zohran Mamdani highlighted it in August. It builds on the city's separate Citywide Doula Initiative, which gives free doula care to income-eligible pregnant New Yorkers in high-need neighborhoods and had served more than 4,200 people as of August 2026.
