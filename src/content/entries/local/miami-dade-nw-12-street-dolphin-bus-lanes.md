Buses heading in and out of the Dolphin Park-and-Ride, next to Dolphin Mall in west Miami-Dade, are getting lanes of their own. The project will widen and resurface NW 12 Street from the park-and-ride, near NW 122 Avenue and the Dolphin Expressway, east to NW 114 Avenue. It adds bus-only lanes for the station. It also lengthens turn lanes, adds new ones, and improves the Florida Turnpike exit ramps and the right-turn exit from Dolphin Mall at NW 114 Avenue. The county says the work supports its planned East-West Corridor rapid transit line.

Riders don't need to do anything. Once the lanes open, buses using the station should spend less time stuck in mall and turnpike traffic. The county expects the project to be finished by June 30, 2030.

Commissioner Juan Carlos Bermudez, whose district includes the area, sponsored the resolution. The county commission approved it 12-0 on June 16, 2026, and Mayor Daniella Levine Cava approved the resolution. The Florida Department of Transportation will pay back up to $3,548,180 through its County Incentive Grant Program. That covers half of the estimated $7.1 million construction cost. The other half comes from the county's half-cent transportation surtax, which voters approved to pay for transit.
