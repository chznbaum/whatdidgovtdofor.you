Athens-Clarke County approved preliminary plans for a base project with sidewalks, ramps, pedestrian crossings, traffic-calming measures, drainage work, pavement restoration and signs along two stretches of Old Monroe Road: Couplet Drive to The Avenue, and Athens Collision Center to Atlanta Highway. The plans also include two possible Stonehenge Way sidewalk sections from Old Monroe Road to Marlborough Downs Road. Those are bid alternates, so they will be built only if prices and available funding allow. Traffic calming on Couplet Drive is also included, with details left to transportation staff.

You don't need to apply to benefit. The project is moving through easement acquisition, final design and permitting before construction bids. The county expects to seek approval of a construction contract in spring 2027 and begin work in summer 2027.

Athens-Clarke voters approved TSPLOST 2023, the transportation sales tax funding the project, in May 2022. The project has a $4.612 million budget, including $3 million identified for construction. Staff estimated that building both optional Stonehenge Way sections could require about $800,000 in additional TSPLOST surplus and said it may request that money when the construction contract returns for approval. Residents helped shape the project through a user group that began meeting in 2024 and public input in August 2025. With Mayor Kelly Girtz presiding, the commission approved the preliminary plans unanimously.
