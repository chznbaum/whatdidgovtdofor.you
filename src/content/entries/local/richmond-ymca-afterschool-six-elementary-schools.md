During the 2025–26 school year, families at Cardinal, Elizabeth D. Redd, J.L. Francis, Linwood Holton, Mary Munford and William Fox elementary schools had low-cost afterschool care at their schools through the YMCA of Greater Richmond. The city committed $457,000 from its parks department’s budget to keep YMCA fees equal to those at city-run sites.

Richmond coordinated afterschool care at all 26 city elementary schools. The Department of Parks, Recreation and Community Facilities directly operated 20 sites, while the YMCA operated the other six. City staff said the YMCA grant cost less than having the department run those six programs itself. The $457,000 had already been appropriated in the department’s fiscal-year 2026 out-of-school-time budget.

Mayor Danny Avula’s administration proposed the ordinance, and City Council adopted it on its consent agenda on June 22, 2026. The contract applied only to the 2025–26 school year. The city’s current program page lists the same six schools as YMCA sites for 2026–27, but that continuation is not covered by this ordinance.
