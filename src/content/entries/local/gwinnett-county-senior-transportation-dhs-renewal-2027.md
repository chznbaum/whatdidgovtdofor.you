Gwinnett County will keep providing rides for its older residents for another year. The county renewed its annual contract with the Georgia Department of Human Services, worth $757,783.36. The contract pays for transportation and related services for Gwinnett seniors from July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2027. Federal and state funds administered by Georgia DHS cover $525,076.36 of the cost, and the county puts up a local match of $232,707.

The county's senior services offer two kinds of rides. Seniors who need help getting to and from the vehicle can get door-through-door rides to scheduled medical appointments or to other activities, and they pay a fee based on distance. Seniors who don't need that help can get curb-to-curb rides within the county to scheduled, non-emergency medical appointments, and they pay a share based on income. To apply for Senior Services, you must be a Gwinnett County resident age 60 or older or the spouse of one. Applicants must qualify, and some services have waiting lists. Call 678-377-4150 for information.

No source names a commissioner who brought this renewal forward. The Board of Commissioners, chaired by Nicole Love Hendrickson, approved it 5-0 on July 14, 2026.
