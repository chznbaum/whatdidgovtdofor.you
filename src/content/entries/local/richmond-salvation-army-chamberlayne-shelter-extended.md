The Salvation Army building at 1900 Chamberlayne Avenue is where Richmond sends single adults who need a warm place to sleep. From November 15 to April 15, the inclement-weather shelter operates every night from 5 p.m. to 8 a.m. and serves dinner and breakfast. The rest of the year, the city can activate it during dangerous weather, including heat of 92 degrees or more. For the 2026-27 winter season, it can accommodate up to 106 men and women. The same building also has 50 year-round emergency shelter beds for men.

The city's grant contract with The Salvation Army started in 2024 and was set to run out on June 30, 2026. This ordinance extends it through June 30, 2027, and the city can renew it for up to three more one-year terms. Single adults can walk up for the seasonal shelter. Access to the 50 year-round men's beds is arranged through the Homeless Connection Line at (804) 972-0813.

Mayor Danny Avula's administration asked for the extension. City Council approved it 8-0 on June 22, 2026. Separately, The Salvation Army is raising money to renovate the building into a larger shelter. The city committed a $7 million matching grant, contingent on The Salvation Army raising the rest of its campaign funding by the end of 2026.
