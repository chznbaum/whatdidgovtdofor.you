Cobb County accepted $843,558 from the Technical College System of Georgia for CobbWorks, the county's local workforce development board. The money pays for training and job services for young people in its youth program.

The program, called B.O.S.S., is for Cobb residents ages 16 to 24 who meet income rules, which are checked when you apply, and who fall into one of the program's target groups, such as young people who left high school without a diploma. Depending on what you need, you can get help earning a GED, free tuition for career training, paid work experience, job readiness workshops, and career coaching. You do need to apply: call WorkSource Cobb at 770-528-4300, email bossinfo@cobbworks.org, or use the application link on its website. The program is funded under the federal Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act.

The Board of Commissioners approved the grant 5-0 on July 28, 2026, as part of its consent agenda. Chairwoman Lisa Cupid presided. All five commissioners attended, and the vote also cleared the way for the matching budget changes.
