If you live in Union County and a sudden emergency leaves you without a place to stay or about to lose your home, you may qualify for this help. The Social Services for the Homeless program serves people who are not eligible for Work First New Jersey, usually because their income is too high, and who can afford their housing after the short-term help ends. Call 2-1-1 for help and a list of agencies in the county.

The county renewed its contracts with local agencies to provide emergency shelter, motel or hotel nights, and help with rent, mortgage payments or a security deposit. The contracts run from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027, and together cannot exceed $973,400. The county's public notice does not list the individual agencies or how much each one gets.

Commissioner Stanley J. Neron sponsored the resolution. It lets the county manager award the renewed contracts to the agencies. The Board of County Commissioners approved it 6-0 on June 25, 2026, with Neron and two other commissioners absent.
