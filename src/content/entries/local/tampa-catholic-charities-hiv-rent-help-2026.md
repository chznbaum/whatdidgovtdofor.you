This agreement lets Catholic Charities Diocese of St. Petersburg receive up to $2,745,636 in federal HOPWA reimbursements over three years for a tenant-based rental-assistance program. The first-year limit is $915,212; the same amount in each of the next two years depends on available federal funds. The resolution and agreement took effect retroactively on Oct. 1, 2026.

The program helps pay rent and utilities for low-income Hillsborough County households in which someone has HIV or AIDS. The agreement requires Catholic Charities to assist at least 80 eligible households and issue vouchers to eligible applicants on its waiting list. Catholic Charities says applicants must have a medical diagnosis of HIV or AIDS and be homeless, marginally housed, at risk of eviction or moving from a residential setting. To ask about help, call 813-375-3933. Although Tampa oversees HOPWA across four counties, this agreement covers Hillsborough County.

The City Council approved the agreement unanimously on Nov. 19, 2026, with Chair Alan Clendenin presiding. That day it approved four other HOPWA agreements: two with Catholic Charities and two with the AIDS Service Association of Pinellas. Federal HOPWA funds pay these reimbursements, and the agreement says they cannot replace state or local money already set aside for the same uses.
