Hampton has a public aquatics center again. The Aquaplex at Coliseum Drive and Pine Chapel Road has a 50-meter competition pool, a 25-yard pool for lessons and programs, and an outdoor SplashDown Park, which opened in May 2023 and returns each summer. Hampton residents and nonresidents can buy day passes or monthly memberships. The center offers lessons from parent-and-child through adult classes, plus an adaptive program for children ages 6 to 12 who use an IEP at school.

The city built it partly to replace the pool lost when the Olde Hampton Community Center closed. At the October 2022 opening, Mayor Donnie Tuck said teaching children to swim mattered in a city surrounded by water, where drowning is a real risk.

The idea began with an unsolicited 2018 proposal from builder Clancy & Theys and architects GuernseyTingle. The city took competing proposals and put the project in its capital plan: $15.8 million for the community pool, $4.5 million for the splash park, and $9.15 million for the competition venue. Councilman Steven Brown argued that crime, housing and youth programs mattered more. City officials later said they covered the competition facility's added cost by reassigning staff and money from the former community center, timing debt service and adding a $1-per-room hotel tax. The council approved the $29.5 million deal 7-0 on Jan. 22, 2020, with Tuck presiding. The building was finished in 17 months, on budget.
