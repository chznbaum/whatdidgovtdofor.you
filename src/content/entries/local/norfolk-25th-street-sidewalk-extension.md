Norfolk is extending the sidewalk on 25th Street. The project gets $963,678 in Transportation Alternatives money administered by the Virginia Department of Transportation, and the city adds $240,920 of its own, for about $1.2 million in all. A sidewalk gives people a safe place to walk instead of the street or a dirt path. That matters most for people who get around on foot, push strollers, use wheelchairs, or walk to a bus stop.

You don't have to apply for anything. Once the sidewalk is built, anyone can use it. Projects funded in this round go through design and engineering first, so construction comes later.

Transportation Alternatives pays for sidewalks, trails, and bike routes. The Commonwealth Transportation Board's February 2026 selection list marked the 25th Street project for a $963,678 statewide at-large award. The board adopted the final fiscal 2027-2032 transportation program on June 16. On August 25, 2026, the Norfolk City Council voted 8-0 to accept the money, put up the city's share, and add the project to this year's budget.
