If you live or work around Westfield, you can call a county shuttle for short trips: to the train station, a doctor's appointment, downtown shops or a bus stop. RideUC runs weekdays from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. in a zone about 1.5 miles around the corner of Route 28 and South Avenue. The county says more than 35,000 people live in it.

Anyone can ride. Create an account, then book a trip in the RideUC app, on its website or by phone at (908) 304-9138 about 15 to 20 minutes before you need it. A driver picks you up at a nearby corner, called a virtual stop. Wheelchair-accessible vehicles are available, and riders who need it can ask for door-to-door pickup. A ride costs $3, plus $1 for each extra person in your group. Rides are free through Dec. 5.

Commissioner James E. Baker Jr. brought the contract to the Board of County Commissioners. The board, chaired by Joseph C. Bodek, approved it 9–0 on May 28, 2026. The county hired River North Transit, a company that operates as Via, to provide the vehicles, drivers and booking system for up to $723,002.64 during the initial contract period, which runs from July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2027. The one-year pilot began Oct. 20, 2026. The county has an option to extend the contract for one or two more years.
