In the winter of 2023–24, Richmond paid The Salvation Army to run an inclement weather shelter at its building at 1900 Chamberlayne Avenue in Northside. The shelter opened Dec. 1, 2023, and the contract covered it through April 15, 2024. It had room for 150 single adult men and women.

Single adults could walk in on a first-come, first-served basis; the shelter did not accept children. It opened at 5 p.m., guests normally left by 8 a.m., and meals were included. In earlier winters the city had trouble opening cold-weather shelters on time. This shelter opened on its Dec. 1 target date.

Mayor Levar Stoney's administration developed the plan, and Chief Administrative Officer Lincoln Saunders presented it to City Council. Companion Ord. No. 2023-321 moved $1,389,467 from four city shelter budget lines into a new grant line for the Chamberlayne shelter; Ord. No. 2023-325 authorized the contract. Council members Kristen Nye, Michael Jones, Cynthia Newbille, Reva Trammell, Andreas Addison and Ellen Robertson joined as co-patrons. Stephanie Lynch spoke in support of the shelter and services for its guests. Advocate Rhonda Sneed said advocates had fought hard for the expansion. Some Northside business owners objected, but on Nov. 13, 2023, the council approved the shelter ordinances unanimously.
