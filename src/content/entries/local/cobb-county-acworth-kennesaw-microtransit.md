If you live or work around Acworth or Kennesaw, you can book a CobbLinc Go ride by app. A van picks you up and drops you off anywhere inside a 31-square-mile zone. The zone covers downtown Acworth and Kennesaw, Kennesaw State University, Cobb County International Airport and Town Center Mall. Rides cost $2.50, the same as a local bus fare, and you can transfer to local buses for free. Service runs on weekdays from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., the same hours as the county's south Cobb zone, where ridership has grown far past the old FLEX service it replaced.

The county estimates that 87,140 more residents can now use the service. It's a two-year pilot, paid for mostly with a $3.84 million state grant. Federal grants and local funds cover the rest.

On Aug. 25, 2026, the Board of Commissioners voted 3-2 to approve the federally required equity analysis and authorize the new service. Chairwoman Lisa Cupid and Commissioners Erick Allen and Monique Sheffield voted yes. Commissioners Keli Gambrill and JoAnn Birrell voted no; they said transit should go to voters first. The board approved the operating contracts in September, and the zone opened with a ribbon-cutting in late October.
