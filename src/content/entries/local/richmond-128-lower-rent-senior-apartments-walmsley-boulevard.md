South Richmond has a new apartment building for seniors with lower incomes. The Cascade has 128 apartments. It sits at 4824 Walmsley Blvd., just west of Broad Rock Boulevard, in the Foundry Creek complex. Income limits average 60% of the area median income. To get an apartment, seniors apply with the property, like at any rental, and must meet those limits. The building opened in June 2026, and about half its apartments were leased at the ribbon-cutting.

The city helped make the project possible through its Affordable Housing Performance Grant program. Richmond collects higher property taxes once a project is built, and the program pays that increase back to the developer each year for 30 years. In return, the apartments must stay affordable. The city estimates the grant for this building at $331,246 a year. The developers are Crescent Development and Spy Rock Real Estate. The same complex also has 216 income-restricted family apartments, which were funded under a separate grant.

Mayor Levar Stoney proposed the grant agreement, which runs through the city's Economic Development Authority. The Richmond City Council approved it 7–0 on June 24, 2024, as part of the consent agenda.
