Tampa is replacing the water pipes under the Wellswood East neighborhood. The work covers water mains up to 12 inches wide, the service lines that run to homes and businesses, and the fire hydrants, valves, fittings and meter connections that go with them. The project area runs from Hillsborough Avenue south to Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard, between N. Armenia Avenue and N. Boulevard.

You don't need to do anything to get the new lines. The city's contractor, Kimmins Contracting, designs and builds the work. A full-time city inspector will be on site, and construction is scheduled to take 309 days. The money comes from the Water Department's renewal and replacement fund, which pays to replace aging water infrastructure.

This is the second neighborhood project under a citywide water main contract. The first, approved in December 2025, covered about 2,760 feet of main on W. Morrison, W. Watrous, S. Albany and W. Southview avenues. The City Council, chaired by Alan Clendenin, approved this $9.8 million phase unanimously on Sept. 3, 2026, after putting it off from the week before.
