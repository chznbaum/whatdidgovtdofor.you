If you ride the Q70 between LaGuardia Airport and Jackson Heights, your bus now has its own lane on one of its slowest stretches. The city painted an eastbound bus-only lane down the center of Broadway from 69th Street to Roosevelt Avenue, where the bus comes off the Brooklyn-Queens Expressway toward the 74th Street–Roosevelt Avenue station. Cars and trucks still have one lane in each direction.

Before the change, Q70 buses on this part of Broadway sometimes crawled at 2.7 miles per hour during the evening rush. That's slower than most people walk. About 9,000 people ride the Q70 each day. The route links the airport to the 7, E, F, M and R trains, the Long Island Rail Road and several local buses, so many of its riders are workers heading to and from airport jobs. You don't have to do anything to use it. Just ride the bus.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced the lane on May 13, 2026, as part of his administration's plan to get transit ready for the World Cup. Commissioner Mike Flynn's Department of Transportation took the plan to the local community board and installed the lane that June. By July, riders told Streetsblog the bus barely seemed affected by traffic on the stretch.
