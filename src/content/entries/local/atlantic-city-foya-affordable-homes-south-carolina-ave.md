Atlantic City approved $203,709.04 in federal HOME funds for a six-home affordable homeownership project at 303 N. South Carolina Ave. The Aug. 19, 2026 resolution directs the funding to FOYA Development Group LLC. Each home is planned with three bedrooms for a low- to moderate-income buyer.

FOYA used a lottery for the initial sales process. Its current page says the six homes are now selling, that the July 13, 2026 lottery ranking is available, and that applicants will be contacted in order. It advertises two price points but does not publish their amounts or the income limits, so eligibility and price still need to be confirmed with the developer.

FOYA, led by Adenah Bayoh, broke ground in June 2025 with the New Jersey Department of Community Affairs, the city, and CEO Enterprises as partners. At the Aug. 19 meeting, Second Ward Councilmember LaToya Dunston cosponsored Resolution 504. Council President Aaron Randolph chaired the meeting, and the council approved the resolution as part of a grouped roll call.
