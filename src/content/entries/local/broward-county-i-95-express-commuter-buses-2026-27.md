Broward County Transit runs I-95 Express buses for people who commute between Broward and Miami-Dade. The buses run on weekdays during the morning and late-afternoon rush hours. This vote added $3,096,642 in state money to run and maintain the service, and it extended the county's grant with the Florida Department of Transportation for another year. The county says the state grant and rider fares cover the cost of running the buses.

You don't have to apply for anything. Just pay the fare and board at a stop on the route. The county can add or cut trips, but it needs the state transportation department's written OK first.

The Broward County Commission approved the grant extension and the budget change 9-0 on June 9, 2026. County Mayor Mark Bogen chaired the meeting. The vote also lets the county administrator sign future changes that only add state money or extend the grant, as long as they don't add cost or risk for the county. That should make the next year's renewal easier.
