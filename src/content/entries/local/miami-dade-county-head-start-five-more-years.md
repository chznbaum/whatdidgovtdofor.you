Miami-Dade's June vote authorized $75,187,905 for the first grant year, August 2026 through July 2027, and contracts with 17 delegate agencies. Those contracts cover 6,310 Head Start preschool slots and 446 Early Head Start slots. The county's larger total of 1,238 infant and toddler slots includes two separate partnership grants. Four annual renewal options could bring this base grant's estimated value to $375,939,525 through July 2031, but each year depends on federal funding and appropriation.

Federal records show that HHS awarded $75,660,282 for the first budget year and added a $1,022,966 supplement in September 2026. The county reports that its centers are open. Families can apply year-round online or at a center. Services are free for families who qualify and include full-day early education, meals, screenings, special-needs support, and employment assistance. Applicants provide proof of age, county residence, and income or another qualifying circumstance such as public assistance, homelessness, or foster care.

Mayor Daniella Levine Cava recommended the renewal and provider contracts. Commissioner Roberto J. Gonzalez prime-sponsored the resolution, Commissioner René Garcia cosponsored it, and the commission adopted it 9–0 on June 2, 2026. The federal grant requires a 25% non-federal share. The county said delegate agencies would meet it through in-kind contributions such as facilities, donated goods, volunteer time, and professional services, with no anticipated impact on the County General Fund.
