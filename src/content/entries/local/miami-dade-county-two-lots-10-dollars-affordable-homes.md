On May 19, Miami-Dade County approved transferring two vacant county lots in Commission District 2 for $10 each: one to Voices on the Ground, LLC and one to Forge Capital Group, LLC. The deed forms require any homes for sale to go to qualified buyers earning no more than 120% of area median income. The resolution does not say how many homes will be built. At the committee meeting, county staff said the 2,200-square-foot remnant lot could realistically hold only one single-family home, while the other parcel could support more homes.

Each developer has two years from the recording of its county deed to complete construction, shown by a final certificate of occupancy. If a developer misses that deadline without an extension or violates the deed restrictions, the county can take the land back. The county's property portal still listed the Forge parcel as awaiting deed recording in November 2026, so the transfers and construction are still phasing in. The resolution does not provide a buyer application process.

Neither parcel had water and sewer service when the Housing Committee reviewed the proposal. The commission waived a county policy restricting conveyance of property without sewer access, but the attached deed forms still require the developers to connect the homes to sewer and prohibit septic systems unless the commission authorizes them.

Commissioner Marleine Bastien, who represents District 2, was the resolution's prime sponsor. The Housing Committee recommended it in April 2026, and the full commission approved it 9-0 on May 19, with four commissioners absent.
