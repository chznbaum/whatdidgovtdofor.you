Goochland County set aside $1.25 million for one-time bonuses for county and school workers. Of that, $550,000 went to county government employees and $700,000 went to the school system for its employees. The resolution lists FY22 revenues as the source for both shares.

Eligible county workers received $3,000. The resolution covered permanent full- and part-time employees in good standing who were on staff as of Sept. 27, 2021 and had not already received a bonus that November. The School Board approved $1,500 for full-time school employees and $750 for part-time employees on Dec. 14. It planned to send the payments through payroll on Dec. 17; employees on leave as of Dec. 15 were not eligible.

The Board of Supervisors, chaired by John Lumpkins, approved the budget change as part of its consent agenda on Dec. 7, 2021. The vote was 5-0. Supervisors Susan Lascolette, Ken Peterson, Neil Spoonhower and Don Sharpe joined Lumpkins in voting yes. Goochland's county and school staff had kept offices and classrooms running since the pandemic began.
