Tampa City Council authorized up to $200,000 to reimburse Feeding Tampa Bay, a nonprofit food bank, for distributing meals to people and families who do not have enough to eat. The authorization was backed by the city's General Fund through Social Action & Arts funding. An earlier budget resolution raised the FY2026 contribution from $122,000 to $200,000.

Residents did not apply to the city for this help. Under the agreement, Feeding Tampa Bay could bill the city quarterly after filing reports documenting its work. The attached plan identifies Feeding Tampa Bay's Causeway Center, but the agreement does not say how many meals the city money alone would cover.

Tampa City Council approved the agreement 7-0 on July 16, 2026, with Chair Alan Clendenin presiding. The resolution authorized Mayor Jane Castor to execute the agreement for the city. The agreement expired September 30, 2026, and required all services to be completed by then for Feeding Tampa Bay to receive payment. The public records reviewed through November 16 do not show the amount ultimately reimbursed.
