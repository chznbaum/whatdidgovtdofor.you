Orange County commissioners authorized up to $2,430,637 for a five-year contract serving people in the county's problem-solving courts. If executed, the contract would cover outpatient substance use and mental health services, including assessments, individual and group counseling, education, case management, supervision and drug testing.

People would receive the services through their court program. The county record does not say how many people the contract would serve. The Ninth Judicial Circuit operates several problem-solving courts, including adult drug court, mental health court and veterans treatment court.

Four firms submitted proposals. County staff brought three finalists to the Board of County Commissioners, and the board selected Aspire Health Partners as the top-ranked firm. Specialized Treatment Education and Prevention Services and Limitless & Infinite Transformations were ranked as alternates. With Mayor Jerry Demings presiding, all six members present voted on May 19, 2026, to authorize staff to negotiate and execute the contract. If negotiations with Aspire fail, staff may move to the next-ranked firm.
