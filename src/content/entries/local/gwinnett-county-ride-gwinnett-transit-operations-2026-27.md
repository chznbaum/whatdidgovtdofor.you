Gwinnett County's buses and other transit service are run day to day by a private contractor, Transdev Services. On June 16, 2026, the county extended that contract for one more year, from July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. The county can pay up to $27.7 million for the year to run and maintain the system.

The contract covers Ride Gwinnett, the county's public transit. Ride Gwinnett runs local bus routes, paratransit for riders with disabilities, and microtransit you book through the Ride Gwinnett app. You don't need to apply to ride. You just pay the fare, and riders with disabilities can apply to qualify for paratransit.

The county's Local Transit Operating Fund pays 65% of the cost, and Federal Transit Administration money pays the other 35%. Gwinnett's Transit Advisory Board recommended the extension 4-0 on June 3. Two weeks later, the Board of Commissioners, chaired by Nicole Love Hendrickson, approved it 5-0.
