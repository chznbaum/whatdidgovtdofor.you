Newark authorized a contract of up to $3,118,925 with Catholic Charities of the Archdiocese of Newark to run its Pathways to Housing emergency housing program for single adults and families. The contract runs from April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027. It assigns $2,349,875 to April through December 2026, but only $1 million was certified as available in the city's temporary 2026 budget when the council approved it. Further funding after the temporary budget period and in 2027 depends on availability and appropriation.

Pathways to Housing operates in the former Miller Street Elementary School, where Newark and Catholic Charities opened the shelter in February 2022. Catholic Charities now lists it as a supervised, year-round, 145-bed emergency shelter for single men, women and families. It provides meals, clothing, case management, mental health and substance-use services, employment help and links to crisis services, with the goal of helping residents secure permanent housing. The council resolution does not project how many people the 2026–27 contract will serve.

The city's Department of Health and Community Wellness, through its Office of Homeless Services, requested proposals from Jan. 13 through Feb. 10, 2026. The resolution says Catholic Charities' proposal was advantageous based on its experience and the other RFP criteria. The Newark Municipal Council, led by Council President Luis Quintana, approved the contract on Oct. 7, 2026, ratifying the months the program had already operated since April.
