During summer 2021, Hampton's Department of Parks, Recreation and Leisure Services offered grab-and-go lunches and snacks at sites across the city. All children age 18 or younger could get meals, as could people over 18 enrolled in public or nonprofit private school programs for individuals with disabilities. Meals were free and available on a first-come, first-served basis.

The public meal schedule ran from June 22 through Aug. 27. The grant itself covered April 1 through Sept. 1. Hampton expected more than 2,500 children to take part. USDA covered all direct costs, while the city supplied coordination and other indirect costs. Hampton's audited fiscal 2022 report later recorded $82,516 in direct federal Summer Food Service Program expenditures.

Hampton has received money from this USDA program since 1969. On Aug. 11, 2021, with the program already underway, the city council voted 7–0 to accept and appropriate the grant. Mayor Donnie Tuck presided over the vote. Hampton had requested $129,435.03, and the resolution said the final award could rise or fall with participation.
