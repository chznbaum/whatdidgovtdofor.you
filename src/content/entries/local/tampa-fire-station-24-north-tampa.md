North Tampa is getting its own new fire station. Fire Station 24 is going up at 4902 E. Whiteway Drive, next to Greco Park. The one-story station is about 17,000 square feet. It will have four truck bays, living quarters for crews, and a training tower. It will offer fire and advanced medical response and sit above the 100-year flood plain, a lesson from the flooding Hurricane Milton brought to North Tampa in 2024.

The station is meant to take pressure off Station 13, near Busch Boulevard and 30th Street. Calls there have climbed with North Tampa's growth, and at one point Station 13 handled about 1 in 9 of all Tampa Fire Rescue calls. A Station 24 crew has been working out of Station 13, in space that wasn't built for it. You don't need to do anything to benefit. When you call 911, the nearest unit is sent.

Council member Luis Viera, who represents North Tampa, was a longtime advocate for the station. Nick Stocco, president of Tampa Firefighters Local 754, was among the union leaders who pushed for it. Mayor Jane Castor made it part of her plan to modernize fire stations. After more than two years of design and procurement, the City Council voted unanimously on July 16, 2026, to approve the $17.4 million construction contract with J. Kokolakis Contracting, part of a project budget of about $24 million. Ground was broken that September, and the station should be finished in fall 2027.
