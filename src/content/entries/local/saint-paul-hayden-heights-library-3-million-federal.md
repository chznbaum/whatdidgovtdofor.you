The Hayden Heights Library on White Bear Avenue is being rebuilt, and $3.15 million in federal money will help finish the job. The branch closed on March 25, 2026 for about 18 months of construction. Plans call for big front windows facing the neighborhood and a new community room with movable walls for story times, author talks and large meetings. By September, crews were working on the inside walls, plumbing, sidewalks, the parking lot and a storytime garden.

You don't have to do anything to benefit. When the branch reopens, it will be a free public library for anyone on the East Side.

Rep. Betty McCollum asked for $4 million to finish the project as a Community Project Funding earmark. Congress included $3.15 million in the transportation and housing part of the FY2026 spending law, which President Donald Trump signed on Feb. 3, 2026. The final congressional funding table also lists Sens. Amy Klobuchar and Tina Smith as Senate requesters. The money comes through the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. On June 3, 2026, the Library Board, made up of Saint Paul City Council members, accepted the money and added it to the library's 2026 budget. Council Member Saura Jost sponsored that resolution. The city's capital budget committee had recommended approval in May. This money is on top of $500,000 in federal money that McCollum won for the same branch earlier.
