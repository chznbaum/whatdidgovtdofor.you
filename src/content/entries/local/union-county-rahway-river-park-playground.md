Union County is building a new playground at Rahway River Park, a county park that sits on the line between Clark and Rahway. The county hired Green Valley Group, Inc., of Pearl River, New York, after a public bid. The contract can cost up to $1,233,957.

The playground is in a public county park, so anyone can use it once it opens. There's nothing to apply for and no fee. As it planned the renovation, the county asked residents to fill out a survey about what they wanted from the playground.

Commissioner James E. Baker Jr. brought the resolution to the Board of County Commissioners. The board approved it 9-0 on May 28, 2026, and the vote authorizes the county manager to sign the contract. We couldn't find a public date for when construction will finish.
