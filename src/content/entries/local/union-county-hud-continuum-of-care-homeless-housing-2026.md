Union County can now take in up to $3,100,929 from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's Continuum of Care program. The county passes the money to local agencies under a spending plan. In Union County, this program pays for permanent supportive housing, rapid rehousing that gets people quickly into their own apartments, transitional housing, and the systems that track and coordinate homeless services.

You don't apply for the grant yourself. If you're homeless or about to lose your home, contact the county's Department of Human Services Action Line at 908-677-5405 or NJ 211 for referrals. Eligibility and availability depend on the program.

Commissioner Stanley J. Neron sponsored the resolution accepting the award, and the nine-member Board of County Commissioners passed it unanimously on May 28, 2026. The award came during a rough year for the program. A federal court blocked HUD's new rules for the 2025 funding round, and Congress then ordered HUD to renew expiring homeless housing grants. HUD announced renewals in rounds between March and May 2026.
