If you're raising a child in Cobb County and the other parent lives in another state, the child support unit in the Cobb district attorney's office can help you get support. It works with the Georgia Department of Human Services to find parents, establish parentage, get a court order for support, and collect and process payments. Parents who pay support can use it too. If both parents live in Georgia, the state's own child support office in Marietta handles the case instead.

The county's vote keeps the unit staffed for Georgia's 2027 budget year, which runs from July 2026 through June 2027. Its budget is about $1.7 million. Federal and state money covers roughly $1.06 million of that, and the rest comes from the county. Most of the money pays the people who work the cases. On top of the $1,131,874 state contract, Cobb added $571,451 of its own.

The Board of Commissioners approved the contract 4–0 on June 9, 2026, as part of its consent agenda. Chairwoman Lisa Cupid presided. Commissioner Erick Allen was absent. Earlier in 2026, a state lawmaker warned that the office's funding had fallen after the COVID-19 pandemic and was never brought back to the level it needs to run.
