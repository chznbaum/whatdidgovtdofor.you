CobbLinc Go is Cobb County's on-demand ride service. You book a trip in the CobbLinc app or by calling 770-427-4444, and a van picks you up near your door. It groups you with other riders heading the same way. A ride costs $2.50, the same as a regular CobbLinc bus, and the service runs weekdays from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Wheelchair-accessible vans are available on request.

Since October 2024, the service has covered parts of Austell, Powder Springs and southwest Marietta. This expansion added about 6 square miles reaching into northwest Mableton. The county's equity analysis estimated that about 18,105 more people now live inside the zone, a 19% increase. The county set the bigger zone to start Oct. 29, 2026, and it was approved as a one-year pilot.

The Board of Commissioners approved the expansion on Aug. 25, 2026. Chairwoman Lisa Cupid and Commissioners Monique Sheffield and Erick Allen voted yes. Commissioners Keli Gambrill and JoAnn Birrell voted no, saying transit should go to voters after a 2024 referendum failed. In September the board approved the contracts to run it, which also cover a new pilot in Acworth and Kennesaw. Most of CobbLinc Go's cost is paid with state and federal transit grants.
