Newark authorized an open-ended contract for street changes meant to improve pedestrian safety. The work includes new crosswalk configurations, pedestrian protection measures, minor changes to traffic and parking lanes, and bike-lane modifications. The city may order up to $2,051,184.77 of work during a contract term of no more than 24 consecutive months, starting when the contract is executed.

You don't have to apply or do anything. The changes will be built into the streets, and anyone who walks, bikes, or drives there will use them. The official record does not say when the contract was executed or whether construction has started.

The city's Department of Economic and Housing Development advertised the job on June 22, 2026, and received one bid for Packages B and C from J.A. Alexander, Inc. of Bloomfield. The Municipal Council, led by Council President Luis Quintana, approved the contract on Sept. 9, 2026. At that meeting, the council raised the ceiling from $1,808,441.88 to $2,051,184.77 before adopting the resolution as amended. The resolution identifies a department budget account as the funding source, but requires the city to certify that money is available whenever it places an order under the contract.
