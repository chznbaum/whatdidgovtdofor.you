Logan Fountain, at 265 Logan Street in Cypress Hills, Brooklyn, opened in May 2025 on a former gas station site. The 13-story building has 343 total units: 173 permanent income-restricted apartments, 169 shelter units, and one superintendent's apartment. It also has ground-floor retail space, play areas, a fitness room and a courtyard.

Of the 173 permanent apartments, 105 are supportive housing. Tenants get on-site case management, career counseling, mental health support and health care referrals from the Jericho Project. City agencies refer eligible low-income or formerly homeless households with special needs to those apartments; the other 68 were offered to the public through the Housing Connect lottery. HELP USA runs the 169 shelter units. The Department of Homeless Services refers eligible homeless families with children, and HELP USA provides services to help them find permanent homes.

The $214 million project was financed by city, state and private partners. Former Council Member Rafael Espinal secured $1 million in City Council capital funding. On May 16, 2022, the state Housing Finance Agency, led by RuthAnne Visnauskas, approved Logan Fountain's financing. The federal housing tax credits it allocated ultimately generated nearly $50 million in equity, and it supplied $18 million in long-term bond financing. HPD, led when financing closed by Adolfo Carrión Jr., supplied $24 million. A 30-year DSS contract, implemented under Commissioner Gary Jenkins, backed financing for the shelter units. Construction began in August 2022. The state brownfield program was expected to add about $9 million in tax credits.
