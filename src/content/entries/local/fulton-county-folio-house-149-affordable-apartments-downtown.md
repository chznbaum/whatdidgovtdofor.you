Fulton County approved money for Folio House Phase II, a new 149-apartment building planned on the vacant lot beside the old Atlanta Constitution building at 143 Alabama Street. The apartments will be income-restricted at 30%, 50%, or 80% of area median income. Thirty units are expected to have project-based vouchers, with those tenants paying 30% of their income toward rent.

The grant uses Fulton County's share of Westside Tax Allocation District revenue that had already been reserved for development. Tax allocation districts direct property-tax growth above a frozen base to development instead of schools and general government services; the base revenue continues funding those services. Invest Atlanta also approved more than $22 million in tax-exempt bond financing for Phase II.

Renters cannot apply yet. Atlanta Housing's HUD-approved plan says the project expects to close financing in December 2026, and the project's market study projected completion in August 2028. Commissioners Dana Barrett and Khadijah Abdur-Rahman sponsored the resolution, and the Fulton County Board of Commissioners approved it on July 15, 2026.
