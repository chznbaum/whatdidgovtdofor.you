If you rent in a New York City multiple dwelling with a gas stove, you may request knob covers or permanent locking knobs in writing, even without a child at home. Your landlord generally has 30 days to make your choice available. Owner-occupied co-op and condo units are exempt. Landlords need not provide an unavailable or incompatible device, or more than two replacements in one year.

Landlords must send each tenant with a gas stove an annual notice by January 16. Return it to make your written request; without one, the landlord has no obligation to provide a device. Owners must keep the records for five years. Failure to provide a device when required is a Class B hazardous violation. If your landlord does not follow through, you can complain to 311.

The original requirement came from Local Law 117 of 2018, prime-sponsored by then-Council Member Ritchie Torres after a deadly Bronx fire started by a 3-year-old playing with a stove. The bill was introduced at Bronx Borough President Rubén Díaz Jr.’s request, and Torres credited Díaz and Council Member Chaim Deutsch for their fire-prevention advocacy. Council Member Laurie Cumbo later prime-sponsored Local Law 44 of 2022, which added the locking-knob choice and required tenants to request a device affirmatively in writing. Both laws became law without a mayoral signature. HPD, led by Commissioner Adolfo Carrión Jr., published matching rule amendments on May 31; the rule took effect June 30, and Local Law 44 took effect July 14.
