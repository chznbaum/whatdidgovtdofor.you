Three Camden streets near the Port of Camden are set to be rebuilt. The project covers Mechanic Street from South Front Street to South 3rd Street, Locust Street from Mt. Vernon Street to Mechanic Street, and Jefferson Street from the Conrail crossing to Broadway. Trucks heading to and from the port use these streets, along with people who live and work nearby.

Residents don't need to apply for anything. The city hired South State, Inc. to do the work. NJDOT awarded Camden $4 million for the project through its Local Freight Impact Fund, which helps local governments improve roads and bridges that carry commercial truck traffic. A construction listing gave a projected July 2026 start date.

Camden City Council awarded the contract on June 11, 2026. The contract resolution was on the consent agenda. Council President Angel Fuentes led the council at the time.
