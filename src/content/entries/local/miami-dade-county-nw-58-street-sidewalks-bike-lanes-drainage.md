NW 58 Street between NW 97 Avenue and NW 79 Avenue runs east to west and serves the City of Doral. It has no sidewalks, no curbs, no bike lanes and guardrails that don't meet current standards. Miami-Dade County is rebuilding it from the ground up. The road keeps four lanes and a center turn lane, and it gets sidewalks, bike lanes, curbs and gutters, a new storm drain system, streetlights, upgraded traffic signals, and an upgraded guardrail along the canal on the north side.

You don't need to do anything to benefit. Once the work is done, people on foot will have sidewalks along the corridor, and the new storm drain system will carry rainwater off the road.

County Commissioner Juan Carlos Bermudez, whose District 12 includes the road, sponsored the contract. The Board of County Commissioners approved it 12-0 on July 21, 2026. Horizon Contractors had the lowest of eight bids, slightly under the county's own estimate. The county first planned to have one firm design and build the project but cancelled that plan because of utility conflicts. The Department of Transportation and Public Works then took over the design and resolved the conflicts before seeking new bids. The work is paid for with mobility impact fees, charges on development that help fund the additional public-service costs created by new construction.
