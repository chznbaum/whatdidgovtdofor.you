If you're an immigrant in New York City and have a question about your status or need a lawyer you can trust, you can call the city's free hotline any day of the week. Call 1-800-354-0365, or call 311 and say "Immigration Legal." It's open 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays and 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Saturdays and Sundays. Calls are confidential, and you can get help whatever your immigration status.

The hotline doesn't take cases itself. Counselors answer questions about immigration rules and can point you to a city-funded legal provider. They can't promise that a lawyer will take your case. The city is hiring more counselors who speak Arabic, Mandarin and Haitian Creole, and interpreters cover more than 200 other languages. You can also reach the hotline on WhatsApp in English and Spanish.

Until this fall, the line was open only on weekdays, until 6 p.m. on three of them, plus the last Saturday of each month. Mayor Zohran Mamdani's administration put nearly $1.5 million in the 2027 city budget to keep it open longer. The Mayor's Office of Immigrant Affairs, led by Commissioner Faiza N. Ali, runs the hotline. Staff at Catholic Charities of the Archdiocese of New York answer the calls.
