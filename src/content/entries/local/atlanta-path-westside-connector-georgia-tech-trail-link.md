Atlanta is paying for a new paved path for walking and biking. It will connect the Westside BeltLine Connector trail to the PATH Parkway trail, which runs through Georgia Tech's main campus. Safety improvements are part of the project too. When it's done, people on the west side will be able to get from the BeltLine network to Georgia Tech and Midtown on an off-street trail instead of on busy roads.

Anyone can use the trail for free, with no sign-up. Under the agreement, the nonprofit PATH Foundation handles design, permits, hiring the contractor and managing construction, and the city's transportation department signs off on each step. The city will pay up to $8 million, from public improvement bonds it issued in 2022. As of this fall, the city's project page lists construction starting Nov. 5, 2026 and finishing Feb. 2, 2028.

The resolution came from the City Council's Transportation Committee, chaired by Council Member Alex Wan. The full council passed it 13-0 on a roll call vote on June 1, 2026.
