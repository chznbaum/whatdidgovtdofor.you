Fiber service is available throughout Port 80's grant area in greater Crozier. Goochland's November 2025 report counted 373 serviceable locations and 148 connections: 133 inside the grant area and 15 outside it. The network reaches the Covington, Strawberry Run and Beaverdam Creek subdivisions and parts of Cardwell, Taylor, Genito and Shallow Well roads. Homes and businesses contact Port 80 to sign up. Plans reported in 2021 cost $80 to $170 a month depending on speed; current prices may differ.

Virginia awarded $567,557.98 to Goochland County and Port 80 in March 2021. On May 4, the Board of Supervisors, chaired by John Lumpkins Jr., voted 5–0 to accept it and amend the county's capital budget. Goochland committed up to $100,000 and Port 80 committed $247,707.40, making the planned project cost $915,265.38. The county reported in November 2025 that $79,243.54 of its commitment had been paid.

The original award announcement projected about 40 miles of fiber and 218 serviceable units. After the project was rescaled, the county described the plan as 18.92 miles of backbone and 12.96 miles of new drops serving at least 228 passings. By July 2023, all backbone fiber was installed and service was available throughout the grant area. The network ultimately made more locations serviceable along its route.
