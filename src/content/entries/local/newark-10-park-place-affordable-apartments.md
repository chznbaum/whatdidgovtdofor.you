The 10-story Firemen's Insurance building at 10 Park Place, next to the New Jersey Performing Arts Center, is planned for conversion into 196 apartments with retail space on the ground floor. All but one apartment would be income-restricted: 26 for households earning 30% or less of area median income, 72 at 50% or less, and 97 at 60% or less. The mix runs from studios to 10 three-bedroom units.

Newark's deal exempts the building improvements from regular property taxes for 35 years; land taxes remain due. In place of taxes on the improvements, the owner must pay the city an annual service charge equal to 3.5% to 5% of the project's adjusted gross revenue, subject to a yearly minimum. The council found that the project would not go forward without the abatement. The developer must also assist the city's goal that half of new on-site jobs after occupancy go to Newark residents. The state Economic Development Authority approved $93 million in tax credits for the conversion in 2025. Leasing has not begun, and the cited sources do not yet give an application process.

Legistar lists the Council of the Whole as sponsor rather than naming an individual council member. The council passed the ordinance 8–0 on June 17, 2026, with Council President C. Lawrence Crump presiding and Michael Silva absent.
