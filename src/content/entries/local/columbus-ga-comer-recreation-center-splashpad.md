Columbus is building a splashpad at the Comer Recreation Center. Kids will be able to cool off in its sprays and fountains during hot Georgia summers. The city runs the center, which houses its Parks and Recreation Department's recreation services division, and the new splashpad will add another to the two splash pads the department currently lists around town.

The city hired Freeman & Associates, Inc., a Columbus company, to build it for $737,849.77. The splashpad will be a public facility at a city recreation center. The city has not yet published hours or access details for it, and we haven't found a public opening date.

Columbus Council approved the construction contract on Sept. 8, 2026, as Resolution 271-26. All ten council members present voted yes. The record doesn't say which council member asked for the project. Mayor Skip Henderson presides over the council.
