Saint Paul is putting $410,000 from the Metropolitan Council toward The Aragon, a planned affordable apartment building at 470 White Bear Avenue N., across from Saint Pascal Baylon Church. The nonprofit Beacon Interfaith Housing Collaborative is building it. Plans call for 53 apartments with one to four bedrooms, most of them sized for families.

Most of the homes will be rented to working households earning about 50% to 60% of the area's median income. About 13 apartments will serve families or individuals facing housing barriers because of a disability or history of homelessness. The building isn't open yet. Beacon still lists The Aragon as in development and directs people looking for housing to general resources rather than an Aragon application.

Council Member Cheniqua Johnson, whose ward includes the site, led the council's late-2024 vote to rezone the land for apartments. She also sponsored this resolution. The grant comes from the Met Council's Livable Communities program, with the city acting as the grant's legal sponsor. On Oct. 28, 2026, the City Council accepted the money and added it to the 2026 budget. The resolution also authorizes city officials to execute the grant agreement.
