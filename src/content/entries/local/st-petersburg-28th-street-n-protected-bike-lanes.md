St. Petersburg is making 28th Street N safer for people on bikes and on foot, from 1st Avenue N up to 13th Avenue N. The plan adds bike lanes, with barriers between bikes and cars where the street has room, plus curb ramps that meet ADA rules and high-visibility crosswalks. The city won't widen the road or take land. It will repurpose some existing traffic lanes for the bike lanes.

You don't need to do anything to use it. This stretch is the first of three planned phases, which will later run south to 18th Avenue S. Local advocates say the bike lanes will also link to the Pinellas Trail.

The city split the project into phases so it could compete for state and federal grants. Before the vote, Vice Chair Richie Floyd said he had discussed the project with city administrators and engineering and urged them to set a realistic timeline and finish it as soon as possible. On Nov. 5, 2026, the City Council voted 7-0 to authorize the city to enter an updated agreement with the Florida Department of Transportation. Under it, FDOT pays $1,090,239 toward building this phase and inspecting the work. The city is aiming to start construction in late 2027 or early 2028, and it plans more community meetings before then.
