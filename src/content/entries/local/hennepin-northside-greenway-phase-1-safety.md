The Northside Greenway is a planned route of almost 4 miles along Irving and Humboldt avenues in North Minneapolis. It is designed for walking, biking and recreation, with traffic calming, safety changes and new green space. The first phase runs on Irving Avenue from 26th to 36th Avenue North, then on Humboldt Avenue from 36th up to 44th Avenue North.

Hennepin County will pay up to $496,000 of that first phase, using county bonds. The money goes toward safety changes for people walking, biking and driving, better drainage, and upgrades that make the street easier to use for people with disabilities. You don't need to do anything to benefit: the changes are built into the street. Construction is expected to begin in 2027, and a second phase is planned for 2028.

The City of Minneapolis is leading the project. It will design and build the work and maintain it afterward. The Hennepin County Board approved the county's share on Sept. 15, 2026. The county says the project supports its Toward Zero Deaths Action Plan. The resolution lets the county administrator negotiate the agreement with the city, and the board chair signs it once the county attorney approves.
