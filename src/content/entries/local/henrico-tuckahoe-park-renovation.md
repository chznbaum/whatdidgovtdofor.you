Tuckahoe Park, on Little League Drive in western Henrico, is getting a major upgrade. Henrico has owned the park since 2007, and Tuckahoe Little League plays its baseball and softball games there. The project adds an accessible paved parking lot, two accessible restrooms, and an accessible community playground. It also adds multi-use trails, sidewalks, and a pedestrian bridge. New lighting and drainage work are meant to make the park safer and help keep it from flooding.

The park is public, so anyone can use it. When it's finished, you won't need to sign up or pay to use the new trails, playground, or restrooms. The county says the plans come from residents in the Tuckahoe District who asked for more parks, trails, and ways to get around on foot.

Henrico voters authorized the bond funding for the work in a 2016 referendum. On Aug. 25, 2026, the Henrico County Board of Supervisors unanimously awarded a $13.8 million construction contract to Kenbridge Construction Co. Crews broke ground in September 2026, and the county expects the work to be done in spring 2028.
