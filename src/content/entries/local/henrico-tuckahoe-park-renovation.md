Tuckahoe Park, on Little League Drive in western Henrico, is getting a major upgrade. Henrico has owned the park since 2007, and Tuckahoe Little League plays its baseball and softball games there. The project adds an accessible paved parking lot, two accessible restrooms, and an accessible community playground. It also adds multi-use trails, sidewalks, and a pedestrian bridge. New lighting and drainage work are meant to make the park safer and help keep it from flooding.

The park is open daily from dawn to dusk, except during tournaments and scheduled games. When the work is finished, the new trails, playground and restrooms will be public park amenities; there is no application. The county says the plans reflect feedback from residents who asked for more parks, trails and connections in the Tuckahoe District.

Henrico voters authorized the bond funding for the work in a 2016 referendum. On Aug. 25, 2026, the Henrico County Board of Supervisors unanimously awarded a $13.8 million construction contract to Kenbridge Construction Co. Henrico held a groundbreaking ceremony on Sept. 9, 2026, and the county expects the work to be done in spring 2028.
