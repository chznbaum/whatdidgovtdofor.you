Camden County added $1,240,900 in grant money to its budget for the 2026–2027 grant year. The money pays for four things: the Social Services for Homeless program, a Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) grant, an Intensive Case Management grant, and the county's Code Blue work. The resolution made the grant part of the county budget.

Social Services for the Homeless is New Jersey's safety net for people who are homeless or about to be, but who don't qualify for Work First NJ, often because their income is a bit too high. It can pay for emergency food and shelter, security and utility deposits, and back rent, mortgage or utility bills, along with referrals and some case management. To get help, contact the county's social services office. Camden County calls a Code Blue when the temperature is 32°F or lower so people sleeping outside can reach a warming center or shelter. Contact your local municipality for a warming center; you can also call 211 for homeless help and current Code Blue listings.

The Camden County Board of Commissioners approved the resolution on June 18, 2026, with every commissioner present voting yes. Louis Cappelli Jr. was the board's director at the time. The minutes don't name a commissioner who brought the item.
