Richmond gave up city-owned land in South Richmond's Windsor area, near Richmond Highway and Bellemeade Road, for Lynhaven Ridge. Ordinance 2024-053 authorized the almost 4-acre lot at 2510 Lynhaven Avenue, already declared surplus, to be sold to YWCA Richmond for a nominal price. The project's tax-credit application listed the land as a $1,349,990 donation and the purchase price as $10.

The completed building has 20 two-bedroom and 30 three-bedroom apartments. All 50 are income-restricted: five at 30% of area median income, 21 at 50%, six at 60%, and 18 at 80%. Ten are reserved for participants in Richmond Behavioral Health Authority supportive-housing programs. Surber Development led the project; YWCA Richmond owns a 10% share of its managing company and has a right of first refusal after the 15-year tax-credit compliance period. Renters apply through the property's leasing office.

Mayor Levar Stoney sponsored the land-sale ordinance, and the City Council adopted it on March 11, 2024. The council later approved an affordable-housing performance grant requiring 50 apartments at a 60% AMI average. The project closed its financing in September 2025. As of November 2026, the official leasing site was accepting applications and listing apartments available; the tax-credit application projected lease-up through January 2027.
