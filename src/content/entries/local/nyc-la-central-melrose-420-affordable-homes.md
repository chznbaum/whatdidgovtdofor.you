Two new buildings are going up on Brook Avenue in Melrose, in the South Bronx. Together they will hold 420 apartments, from studios to four-bedrooms. Of those, 418 will be income-restricted for households earning between 30% and 80% of the area median income, and two will be for the buildings’ superintendents. The income-restricted homes include 114 for extremely low-income households, 106 for very low-income households and 198 for low-income households. Sixty-three are supportive homes, with services, for people who have been homeless.

The buildings are the last phase of La Central, a five-building project that will have more than 1,000 affordable homes when it's done. Three buildings, with about 660 apartments and a YMCA, are already open. The new phase adds community space, a courtyard, a small shop and a rooftop telescope run by Bronx High School of Science. The sites were vacant lots before construction.

The phase is expected to cost $343 million. HPD put in more than $137 million. On April 7, 2025, the HDC board approved up to $195.69 million in bond-backed and other financing. HDC President Eric Enderlin recommended the financing, and Acting HPD Commissioner Ahmed Tigani chaired the approving board. Council Member Rafael Salamanca, who approved the project years ago, added $4 million from his capital budget. Mayor Eric Adams' administration helped close the financing, and he joined the June 20, 2025 groundbreaking. Building C is due in December 2027 and Building E in early 2028.
