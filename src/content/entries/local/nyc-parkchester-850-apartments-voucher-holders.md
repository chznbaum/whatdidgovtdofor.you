If you use Section 8, CityFHEPS or another rental subsidy, Parkchester must consider you for its manager-owned rentals. It cannot use a minimum-income cutoff when a subsidy pays part or all of your rent directly to it. Until 593 more voucher-assisted households move in beyond the 257 counted when the settlement was signed, two out of every three apartments that become available must go to assisted applicants. The 850 is a running count since Aug. 1, 2022, not 850 apartments held aside at once. You apply through Parkchester.

New York City has banned housing discrimination based on how renters pay since 2008. The Commission on Human Rights said Parkchester kept voucher holders out by requiring incomes tied to the rent, requiring nonrefundable deposits and not counting the full voucher as income. Parkchester denied the allegations. It agreed to pay $1 million in civil penalties, the most ever under the housing provisions of the city's Human Rights Law. The 850-move-in target was also the largest voucher set-aside the commission had secured.

The case took nearly eight years. The Fair Housing Justice Center filed its complaint in December 2016 after a year-long testing investigation. The commission later filed its own systemic complaint, prosecuted the case under Chair Annabel Palma and approved the settlement on Aug. 21, 2024.
