Atlanta is building a new Fire Station 31 in the southwest part of the city. On Nov. 16, 2026, the City Council approved a task order of up to $10 million for the construction. The work goes through a contract the city already holds with the joint venture Manhattan/RFB. A city bid listing places the new station at 2406 Fairburn Road SW, the old station's address, and describes a two-story building of about 16,600 square feet. It has an apparatus bay, bunk rooms, a kitchen and a training room.

The old station needed replacing. In 2024, Atlanta's fire chief called it "very small" and "antiquated." Atlanta voters approved the city's 2022 infrastructure bond, which lists $13 million to replace Fire Station 31.

Residents don't need to do anything. A modern station with room for crews and equipment helps firefighters respond to fires and medical calls in the neighborhoods around Fairburn Road.

No council member is named as the sponsor in the meeting record. The council passed the task order 13–0 as part of its consent agenda, with Council President Marci Collier Overstreet presiding.
