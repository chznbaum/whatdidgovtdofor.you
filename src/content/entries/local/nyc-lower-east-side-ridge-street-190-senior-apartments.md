A new 16-story building is going up at 145 Broome Street on the Lower East Side. It will have 190 income-restricted studio and one-bedroom apartments for older New Yorkers, plus a two-bedroom superintendent's unit. It will also have tenant facilities, outdoor seating, and on-site social services from Catholic Charities Community Services.

Most apartments are for people 62 and older whose incomes are at or below half of the area median income. Thirty percent are set aside for low-income formerly homeless seniors 55 and older. HPD awarded project-based rental vouchers for all 190 tenant apartments, which will help residents pay the rent. Wavecrest Management will handle lease-up and manage the building.

Grand Street Guild and Catholic Homes of New York are building the project through HPD's Senior Affordable Rental Apartments program and HDC's ELLA program. HDC President Eric Enderlin recommended its bond and mortgage financing, and Acting HPD Commissioner Ahmed Tigani chaired the June 3, 2025 meeting where HDC's members unanimously authorized the package. The city said it contributed $32 million toward the $176 million project; federal HOME funds administered by HPD also helped finance it. The structure topped out in September 2026, with completion expected in fall 2027.
