Norfolk is funding new sidewalks along Tidewater Drive. This ordinance covers the first phase. It puts $2,070,768 in Transportation Alternatives funding administered through the Virginia Department of Transportation into the city budget and adds $517,692 from Norfolk, for about $2.6 million in all. The project will build about 1,360 feet of sidewalk from Easy Street to Thole Street, along with crosswalks, ADA-compliant curb ramps, curb and gutter, and pedestrian signs.

You don't need to do anything to benefit. Once the project is built, anyone walking along this part of Tidewater Drive can use it, whether they're heading to a bus stop, a store or work. The new sidewalk and crossings will give people a designated route instead of walking along the road's shoulder.

The award came through the Transportation Alternatives program. HRTPO says the program uses federal Surface Transportation Block Grant money provided through the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act, and VDOT's Local Assistance Division scored the applications. Tidewater Drive's two sidewalk phases were among eight regional projects awarded more than $14 million. The Norfolk City Council voted 8-0 on Aug. 25, 2026, to accept the Phase 1 award and provide the city's share. Public sources checked through Nov. 13 did not confirm that construction had started.
