Hennepin County put another $3.17 million toward shelter and housing help for people who are homeless and live with a disabling condition. The money runs from July 2026 through June 2027. It keeps several services going: emergency shelter at the Salvation Army Harbor Light Center in downtown Minneapolis, a medical respite shelter at Catholic Charities' Endeavors facility for people who need a place to recover, and help for homeless families from Avivo and Peoples Development Services to find a home and stay in it.

You don't apply for this money directly. People reach these services through the county's shelter and housing system and the providers it contracts with.

The money comes from Minnesota's Housing Support fund. The county requested a cost-neutral transfer from that fund for this project, and the Department of Human Services commissioner approved it. Hennepin County has received this transfer allocation since 2017. The Board approved the current agreement in October 2024, and this amendment brings its total to $9.35 million. The County Board adopted the amendment on consent on June 11, 2026.
