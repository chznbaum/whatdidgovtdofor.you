Tarpon Springs is replacing Fire Station 70, which the county says is past its serviceable and safe useful life. The new station will go up at 1201 Gulf Road. Pinellas County agreed to pay up to $640,257.78 toward it. The city can spend that money on land, design, site work and construction.

The station doesn't serve only the city. Tarpon Springs runs fire and EMS service for the nearby unincorporated area under a contract with the county, so the county pays its share of the project, 9.34%. You don't need to do anything: the station is part of the 911 response for the area. The city builds and runs the station, and the county repays costs after the city shows its invoices and proof of payment. The agreement runs through Sept. 30, 2027.

The money comes from property taxes paid by owners in the Tarpon Springs Fire Protection District. The county commission approved the agreement 7-0 on Aug. 11, 2026, with Chair Dave Eggers presiding. The payment depended on the county's next budget, which the commission adopted on Sept. 24, 2026.
