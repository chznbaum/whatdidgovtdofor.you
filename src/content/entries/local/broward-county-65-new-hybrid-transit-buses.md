Broward County Transit is getting 65 new full-size buses. They are 40-foot hybrid-electric buses made by New Flyer, and they will replace buses that have aged past their useful life. Broward says replacing those buses will help keep the fixed-route fleet in good repair, and the federal grant supporting the purchase is meant to reduce air and noise pollution.

Riders don't need to do anything. The new buses will go into regular service on the county's routes after they arrive. Deliveries are scheduled from the first through fourth quarters of 2028.

The county didn't run its own bidding process. It bought through a contract the Jacksonville Transportation Authority had already bid competitively, which the county's purchasing rules allow. The purchase is estimated at $85 million over four years. The county says it is paid for with transit grant money and transportation surtax money; the FTA awarded $20.48 million in 2025 toward replacing fixed-route buses.

The County Commission approved the contract on May 26, 2026, as part of its consent agenda. Mayor Mark Bogen chaired the commission at the time.
