Union County will repave about 17 miles of its own roads. Crews will grind off the worn top layer and lay new asphalt on 13 stretches of county road. They include East and West Seventh Street, Terrill Road, Jersey Avenue, Central Avenue, Magie Avenue, Mountain Avenue, Passaic Avenue and Chatham Road. The job will also upgrade curb ramps to meet disability access rules, upgrade storm drain inlets to meet state water rules, and repaint the lane lines.

You don't need to do anything to benefit. If you drive, bike, walk or take the bus on these roads, you'll get a smoother surface and upgraded, ADA-compliant curb ramps once the work is done. The county says the job should take about 90 days once it starts, depending on weather. For this project, daytime road closures for paving are scheduled from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., though the county says hours may vary by municipality.

The county took public bids and chose CCM Contracting of Green Brook, with a cap of $11,543,039.97. Commissioner James E. Baker Jr. (D) introduced the resolution. The Board of County Commissioners, chaired by Joseph C. Bodek (D), approved it 9-0 on Aug. 27, 2026.
