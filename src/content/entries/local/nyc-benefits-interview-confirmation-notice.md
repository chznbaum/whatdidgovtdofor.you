Applying for SNAP or cash assistance in New York City means having an eligibility interview with the Department of Social Services. Starting in June 2027, the city must send you a confirmation notice within 72 hours of that interview. It must include a confirmation number, the interview date, where the interview took place, the benefit it was for, and any other relevant details. The rule also covers interviews for emergency assistance grants.

You don't need to ask for the notice. The city sends it electronically through ACCESS HRA, the online account you use to manage your benefits. If a question comes up later about whether your interview happened, you'll have a written record with a number you can point to.

Council Member Tiffany Cabán sponsored the bill with co-sponsors Crystal Hudson, Shahana Hanif, Farah Louis, Mercedes Narcisse and Julie Won. The General Welfare Committee held a hearing on it in March 2026, amended it in May, and the full Council passed it on May 14, 2026. Mayor Zohran Mamdani returned it unsigned, and it became law on June 13, 2026 without his signature. The city has one year to prepare, so the notices are required starting June 13, 2027.
