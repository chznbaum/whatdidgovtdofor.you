HomeAgain's existing family shelter could assist 35 people a day, and its director said it was "at capacity, it always is." In late 2023 Richmond added a second shelter for families: 50 beds, open all year rather than only on cold nights. It is at 7 N. Second St., in the former Richmond Hostel.

The ordinance shifted $663,856 from a budget line for a cold-weather shelter run by United Nations Church International. These were unspent, uncommitted surplus funds carried over from the prior budget year. Richmond put $542,056 into a new HomeAgain operating grant and $121,800 toward leasing the building. Families do not walk in; they seek a bed through the region's coordinated-entry system, primarily Homeward's Homeless Connection Line.

Mayor Levar Stoney brought the ordinance to City Council in October 2023, and the council passed it unanimously on Nov. 13, 2023, along with the rest of that winter's shelter plan. The city opened the shelter on Dec. 1, 2023. In October 2026, the council extended HomeAgain's operating grant through Nov. 30, 2027.
