If you're 60 or older, live in Fulton County, and can't get out to shop or cook, the county will bring meals to your door for free. Open Hand Atlanta cooks and delivers them. This renewal keeps that going through all of 2027. The same contract pays Open Hand to make the meals served at the county's neighborhood senior centers and to provide nutrition education and counseling.

You don't buy a plan or pay a fee. For home delivery, the county's Senior Services department does a phone assessment to see how much help you need and which diets you have to follow. Eligible seniors get five meals a week. To ask about it, call the county's STARLine hotline at 404-613-6000.

Open Hand has fed Fulton seniors for more than 20 years. The contract was first awarded in 2023, and this renewal for up to $2,625,696.53 uses the fourth and last renewal option. No renewal options remain after 2027. The Board of Commissioners, chaired by Robb Pitts, approved it on Oct. 7, 2026.
