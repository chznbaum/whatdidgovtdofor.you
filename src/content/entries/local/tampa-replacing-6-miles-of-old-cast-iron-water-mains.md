Tampa is replacing about 34,000 feet (roughly 6.4 miles) of old cast-iron water mains in East Arbor Heights. The contract places the work in an area generally bounded by East Giddens Avenue to the north, Emma Street to the south, North 22nd Street to the east and North 12th Street to the west. The existing mains are 2 to 6 inches across and have surpassed their useful life. Their replacements will be ductile iron, PVC or HDPE plastic, depending on city specifications.

You don't need to apply. If work reaches your block, expect construction crews and possible brief water-service interruptions. The city's project site says residents and businesses along affected segments will receive advance notice.

Tampa City Council approved the $19.9 million contract with Westra Construction Corp. unanimously on Oct. 1, 2026, at a meeting chaired by Alan Clendenin. It is the first guaranteed-price construction package under a design-build agreement the city entered in August 2024. The money comes from Water Department capital funds: bond proceeds in the Water Future Debt Capital Projects Fund and transfers from Water Operations in the Water Renewal and Replacement Fund.
