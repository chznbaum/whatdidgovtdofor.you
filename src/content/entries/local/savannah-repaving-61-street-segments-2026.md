Savannah is repaving 61 stretches of street across the city. Crews will grind off the worn top layer of asphalt and lay down a fresh surface. The city says the goal is better road conditions, safer streets, and pavement that lasts longer. The work has to meet city and Georgia DOT standards.

You don't need to do anything to benefit. The work happens on city streets and is paid for from Savannah's Capital Improvement Fund. The city's contract with APAC-Atlantic, a paving company based in Garden City, is capped at $7,367,449. It ends 11 months after the company gets the order to start, so the paving should finish in 2027.

The city invited 402 companies to bid and received five bids. APAC-Atlantic's bid was the lowest, about $1.9 million under the next one. Savannah's Department of Transportation recommended the contract. City Council, with Mayor Van Johnson presiding, approved it 7-0 on Sept. 24, 2026, as part of a group of items passed together. The city's agenda doesn't list which streets are included.
