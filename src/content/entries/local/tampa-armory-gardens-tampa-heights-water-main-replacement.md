Tampa is replacing old water mains under two neighborhoods. In Armory Gardens, about 4,136 feet of mains ranging from 2 to 6 inches in diameter will be replaced and upgraded. The Jefferson project in Tampa Heights will replace and upgrade about 7,479 feet of mains ranging from 6 to 8 inches in diameter. Together that's about 2.2 miles of mains.

The city says the upgrades should improve water pressure and water quality, increase system capacity, and reduce service interruptions caused by main breaks or leaks. You don't need to do anything to benefit. The work is paid for through the city's PIPES water and sewer program.

On June 4, 2026, the Tampa City Council voted 6–0 to award the $4,589,195.42 contract to Midsouth, Inc. Council member Bill Carlson was absent for the vote. Chair Alan Clendenin ran the meeting. The resolution also authorized the mayor to execute the contract. The city's project pages list construction for fiscal year 2027.
