TASCO, short for Teen Arts, Sports and Cultural Opportunities, is St. Petersburg's parks and recreation program for teens in grades 6 through 12. This grant pays for TASCO's center-based teen programs for the city's 2027 budget year, which runs from Oct. 1, 2026, through Sept. 30, 2027. The money comes from the Juvenile Welfare Board of Pinellas County.

TASCO has offered free after-school tech classes, such as robotics, photography and 3D printing. The parks department says its after-school program can be free for residents and nonresidents who qualify for fee assistance and meet attendance requirements. The Juvenile Welfare Board provides full scholarships to qualified applicants. For more information, contact a city recreation center or TASCO at 727-892-5060.

The City Council approved the agreement on Sept. 10, 2026, as part of its consent agenda, where routine items pass together in one vote. Council Chair Lisset Hanewicz presided, and the vote was 7-0, with Councilmember Gina Driscoll absent. The resolution lets the mayor sign the one-year agreement with the board.
