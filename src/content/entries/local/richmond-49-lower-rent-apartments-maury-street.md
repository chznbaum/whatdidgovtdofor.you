A new six-story apartment building has opened next to the Maury Street roundabout off I-95 in Manchester. Of its 162 apartments, 49 (about 30%) are set aside for households earning up to 80% of the area's median income. The rest rent at market rates, which run from $1,235 a month for a studio to $1,950 for a two-bedroom. Residents began moving in in April 2026. To rent one of the affordable units, you apply through the building's leasing office and show that your income qualifies.

The city's money comes through Richmond's Affordable Housing Performance Grant program. Building on the land raises its property tax bill, and the city pays most or all of that increase back to the developer for up to 30 years. The developer gets the money only while the units stay affordable and only if it keeps reinvesting in the property. The estimated grant for this project is about $94,977 a year. The developers, Dodson Development Group and Fountainhead Real Estate Development, also used a Virginia Housing loan to pay for the roughly $35 million building.

Mayor Levar Stoney's administration brought the grant agreement to the City Council, which adopted it on July 22, 2024. The city's Economic Development Authority pays out the grant.
