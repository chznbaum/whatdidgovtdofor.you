St. Petersburg is paying the Community Law Program, a legal aid nonprofit, $500,000 to help tenants who are at risk of losing their homes. The agreement covers eviction prevention and diversion: working to settle a case or stop it before it ends with a family put out. The city's Codes Compliance Assistance department runs the agreement.

Tenants facing eviction can contact the Community Law Program to ask for help. The council record doesn't list eligibility rules. The city has worked with the group before. In 2023, it funded the group's eviction help in South St. Petersburg, which included legal advice, negotiated payment or move-out agreements, help filing court papers, representation in court, and work to keep evictions off tenants' records.

The City Council approved the agreement 8-0 on Sept. 3, 2026, as part of its consent agenda, with Chair Lisset Hanewicz presiding. The council record doesn't name a member who brought the item.
