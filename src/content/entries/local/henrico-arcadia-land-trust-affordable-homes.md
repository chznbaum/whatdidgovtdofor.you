Arcadia is a new neighborhood of nearly 800 homes going up in Varina, in eastern Henrico. When the county approved it, the developer had to set aside at least 20 homes for lower-income households. Henrico's $500,000 grant helps the Maggie Walker Community Land Trust buy those 20 single-family homes and keep their prices within reach.

A land trust works differently from a regular home sale. The trust sells you the house but keeps the land under it and leases it to you. That keeps the price down for you and for whoever buys from you later. To buy one of these homes, your household can earn no more than 115% of the median income for the Richmond area. Buyers apply through the land trust.

The Henrico Board of Supervisors approved the grant on Oct. 27, 2026, along with an updated agreement with the land trust. Under that agreement, the nonprofit will seek at least $300,000 in matching donations. The land trust still must acquire the homes before qualified buyers can purchase them.
