Newark is getting more federal money to help residents living with HIV. A Sept. 3, 2026, notice from the U.S. Health Resources and Services Administration awarded the city an additional $3,131,050 through Ending the HIV Epidemic, part of the Ryan White HIV/AIDS Program. The money supports HIV health care and support services for people in Newark and Essex County. Newark's Department of Health and Community Wellness administers the program.

This is the third part of Newark's FY2026 award. The council accepted $679,332 on April 15 and another $2,042,112 on July 15. With this award, the total reaches $5,852,494. The adopted acceptance resolution says the grant period now runs through Feb. 28, 2030. You don't apply to the city for the grant itself; people receive help through providers in the city's Ryan White program.

On Oct. 21, the Municipal Council adopted both the amendment accepting the award and a companion resolution adding the same amount to Newark's temporary 2026 budget. State law required a two-thirds vote of the full council for the emergency appropriation. The federal award requires no municipal funds.
