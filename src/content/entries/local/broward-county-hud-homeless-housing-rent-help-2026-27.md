Broward County approved the use of about $14.65 million in federal homelessness grants from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) and a county match of about $3.18 million. That is about $17.8 million across the 2026 and 2027 fiscal years. The package supports housing, rent help, supportive services and the costs of operating and administering the programs.

This is not a direct payment that residents apply for. It funds existing programs and agreements with service providers. Agreements marked for renewal can be renewed after the HUD award is received, and the county administrator can execute new agreements with specified providers. Broward's Homeless Continuum of Care Board recommended the renewals and new agreements in April 2026. The county meets its match with staff salaries already in the Human Services Department budget.

The County Commission approved the package 9-0 on May 26, 2026, with Mayor Mark Bogen presiding. The records don't name a commissioner who brought the item. Federal homelessness grants were uncertain in 2026: a federal court blocked HUD's new rules for awarding them, and Congress's 2026 spending law required HUD to renew expiring grants. HUD announced its renewal awards between March and May 2026.
