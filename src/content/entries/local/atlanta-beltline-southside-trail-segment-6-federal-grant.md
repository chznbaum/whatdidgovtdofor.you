A short stretch of the Atlanta BeltLine's Southside Trail, about half a mile along Bill Kennedy Way from Glenwood Avenue to Memorial Drive, is going to be built for good. For now, people use a temporary path there. Atlanta has accepted up to $5 million in federal highway money to help pay for construction. The money comes through the Atlanta Regional Commission's transportation program.

Plans for Segment 6 replace the temporary path and improve the section through Glenwood Park. They also add a new walking bridge next to the existing bridge over I-20. A study of nearby streets and intersections is meant to cut down on conflicts between people on the trail and drivers. The trail is free and open to anyone, so no one needs to apply.

The ordinance came from the City Council's Transportation Committee, chaired by Council Member Alex Wan, who moved its passage. The council approved it 13-0 on Sept. 21, 2026. The vote also confirmed the city's earlier application and added the money to the 2027 budget.

Building the trail will take time. Atlanta BeltLine Inc. split the work into a locally funded part, which took bids in September 2026, and a federally funded part, which it plans to put out to bid in early 2028. It expects each phase to take 16 to 18 months to build.
