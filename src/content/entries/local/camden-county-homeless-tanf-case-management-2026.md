Camden County approved $936,000 in agreements with nonprofit providers for three 2026 programs: Social Services for the Homeless, Temporary Assistance for Needy Families, and Intensive Case Management. The county’s homelessness guide says eligible residents may receive help such as emergency shelter, rent or utility assistance, food and case management; the exact help depends on the program and its eligibility rules.

If you need help, call the Camden County Board of Social Services at (856) 225-8800. The county says applicants may need identification, Social Security numbers, proof of income and proof of homelessness. New Jersey also directs people experiencing homelessness to call 2-1-1 for county-linked help.

The county sought providers through Competitive Contracting Request for Proposals #25-43 in fall 2025. Resolution 54 describes the June agreements as a one-year option under that procurement. The Board of Commissioners, led by Director Louis Cappelli Jr., approved the agreements on June 18, 2026, with every member present voting yes. In the same vote, Resolution 47 inserted $1,240,900 in grant funding for these programs and Code Blue activities into the county budget, satisfying Resolution 54’s funding condition.
