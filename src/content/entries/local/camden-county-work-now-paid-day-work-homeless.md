If you're homeless in Camden County, Work Now lets you earn a day's pay with no long hiring process. In 2024, 10 people a day, five days a week, signed up to pick up litter for about five hours. They earned $75 and a free lunch from Cathedral Kitchen. The pay is only the start. Volunteers of America Delaware Valley, which runs the program for the county, also helps workers get IDs, find steady jobs and look for housing.

The county started the program in 2018 under its old Freeholder Board. In its first year, 396 people earned a day's pay, and 127 of them accepted social services. In 2024, local news reported that 15 participants had a steady income and five had landed full-time jobs. People who do well can move on to Bridge to Work, the program's second phase, which offers part-time jobs.

On June 18, 2026, the Camden County Board of Commissioners voted to use the first one-year option on the contract, for $568,945. The board's director, Louis Cappelli Jr., presided over the board, and all members present voted yes.
