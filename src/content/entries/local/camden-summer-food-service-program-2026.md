When school lets out, many kids lose the breakfast and lunch they get at school. Camden runs sites under the Summer Food Service Program to fill that gap. For summer 2026, the city hired Masso Group, LLC to provide the meals under a contract worth $341,385.84. The city's Department of Human Services administers the program.

Meals under the program are free. The federal program serves children 18 and under. At open sites, any child can eat free. Open sites have to be in neighborhoods where at least half the children come from families earning no more than 185% of the federal poverty level. Some sites are tied to an activity program and serve only the children enrolled there. The U.S. Department of Agriculture funds the program, and the New Jersey Department of Agriculture runs it in the state, reimbursing approved sponsors for the meals they serve and documented operating costs.

Camden City Council approved the contract on June 11, 2026. Angel Fuentes was council president when it acted. The state’s sponsor list recorded Camden as a government sponsor with 30 active sites and operation dates from June 29 through Aug. 14, 2026. That listed service period is complete.
