If you delivered for DoorDash in New York City between April 22, 2022 and June 28, 2026, the company may owe you money. The city found that DoorDash left out some trip and waiting time when it figured the minimum hourly pay. It also paid less for parts of multi-order trips, and it paid some workers late or not at all. Of the $131.5 million settlement, more than $115 million goes to workers, and the rest goes to penalties and costs.

You don't need to apply. The city found who is owed money in DoorDash's own records. Emails with each worker's amount went out on Oct. 29, 2026. Workers can choose an electronic payment, and anyone who doesn't choose gets a check in the mail. Workers whose pay was late or missing get about double what they lost on top of the pay itself. Every eligible worker gets at least $10, and DoorDash says the typical payment is about $48. More than 27,000 workers will get over $1,000.

The Department of Consumer and Worker Protection opened the investigation after workers complained about missing pay. Commissioner Sam Levine said the case began under the previous administration and moved faster once Mayor Zohran Mamdani's team added enforcement resources. For three years, DoorDash must also send the city monthly pay data and change its app so that all delivery time counts toward the minimum pay.
