During the summer of 2022, Hampton's Department of Parks, Recreation and Leisure Services ran a free-meal program at sites around the city. The program period ran from April 1 through Sept. 1, and the city expected more than 2,500 children to take part.

The meals were available to children 18 and under, plus older people enrolled in public or private nonprofit school programs for people with disabilities. At open meal sites, the requirement that at least half of children qualify for free or reduced-price school meals was used to qualify the site or area—not to screen each child who arrived.

USDA reimbursed the program's direct service costs. Hampton's FY23 council-approved budget recorded a $129,453 Summer Food Service Program grant award. The parks department covered coordination and other indirect costs itself.

David McCauley led the parks department that operated the program. After the summer program period ended, Hampton City Council accepted and appropriated the federal money on Sept. 28, 2022. The council voted 7–0 with Mayor Donnie Tuck presiding.
