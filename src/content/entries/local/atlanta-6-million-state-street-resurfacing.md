Atlanta has up to $6.05 million in state money to resurface its streets. The money comes from Georgia's Local Road Assistance program, run by the state Department of Transportation. Atlanta doesn't have to match any of it, so the city can use the grant for resurfacing without putting up matching dollars.

You don't need to apply for anything. The money is restricted to eligible street-resurfacing projects. The council record doesn't name the streets, so contact ATLDOT for current project details.

Georgia lawmakers and Gov. Brian Kemp funded the 2026 round through the amended 2026 state budget, which added $250 million for local roads statewide. The amended budget drew on Georgia's large cash surplus. Atlanta's Transportation Committee, chaired by Council Member Alex Wan, sponsored the ordinance. Wan moved it before the full council, which approved it 15–0 on June 15 with Council President Marci Collier Overstreet presiding. The vote also confirmed the city's grant application and added the money to the city's budget.
