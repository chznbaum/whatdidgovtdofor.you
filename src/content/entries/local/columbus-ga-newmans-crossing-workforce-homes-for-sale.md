A new neighborhood of homes for sale is going up off Farr Road in Columbus. It's called Newman's Crossing, and it's being built on the site of a former mobile home park that had fallen into blight. The nonprofit NeighborWorks Columbus plans 62 single-family homes with three bedrooms each. 51% of them are reserved for households earning 80% or less of the area median income.

The homes will be sold, not rented. The sources do not yet give a sales date or application process for buyers. The developer expects all 62 homes to be built and sold within three years of September 2026. Before houses can go up, the land needs streets, water and sewer lines, and drainage. The $2.5 million state grant helps pay for that infrastructure.

In October 2025, the city asked to apply for a $1.5 million infrastructure loan and a $2.5 million infrastructure grant from Georgia's Rural Workforce Housing Initiative on NeighborWorks Columbus's behalf. The state awarded the $2.5 million grant. On May 26, 2026, Columbus Council voted 10-0 for an agreement directing that award to NeighborWorks Columbus for the project, with Mayor Skip Henderson presiding.
