Savannah is preparing the old Coastal Empire Fairgrounds on Meding Street for new housing. On October 22, 2026, the City Council hired BRW Construction Group for $2.73 million to build the site's housing infrastructure. Under its 2024 deal with developer P3JVG, the city is responsible for public infrastructure on the site, including sewer work and widening Meding Street, at a cost of about $6 million. A $2.5 million state housing grant pays for part of the sewer work.

The plan calls for about 400 homes and apartments. The deeds set affordability restrictions for 30 years. For-sale homes must go to households earning no more than 80% of the area's median income. The deed defines a maximum resale price based on keeping monthly housing costs at no more than 30% of the income of a household at 80% of median income. Rentals are reserved in shares for households at or below 40%, 60% and 80% of median income. The first phase has 30 single-family homes and 20 townhomes, and about 64 senior apartments are under construction. The city has not yet published application instructions.

Alderwoman Estella Shabazz, who represents the 5th District, pushed for the project for more than a decade. She held 14 public meetings with neighbors to shape the plan. The city bought the land in 2016, and the council approved this contract unanimously.
