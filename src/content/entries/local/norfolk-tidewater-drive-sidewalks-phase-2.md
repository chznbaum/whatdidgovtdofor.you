Norfolk is funding about 600 feet of sidewalk along Tidewater Drive, from existing sidewalk past the I-64 eastbound off-ramp to planned sidewalk at Thole Street. Phase 2 also includes four crosswalks, ADA-compliant curb ramps and pedestrian signs. A separate Phase 1 received about $2.07 million in the same funding cycle.

On August 25, 2026, Mayor Kenny Alexander presided as the Norfolk City Council voted 8-0 to accept $1,920,689 administered through the Virginia Department of Transportation for the project. The council also added $480,172 from the city as Norfolk's share. Together, that is about $2.4 million, with the Transportation Alternatives award covering about 80% and the city paying the rest.

The award came through the Transportation Alternatives Program. The Hampton Roads Transportation Planning Organization says the program uses federal Surface Transportation Block Grant money provided through the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act, while VDOT's Local Assistance Division scored the applications. The Commonwealth Transportation Board, chaired by Transportation Secretary Nick Donohue, adopted the final fiscal 2027-2032 transportation program on June 16.

Nobody needs to apply for anything. Once the sidewalks and crossings are built, anyone walking along this part of Tidewater Drive can use them. Projects funded in this cycle go through design and engineering before construction, and public sources checked through November 14 did not confirm that construction had started.
