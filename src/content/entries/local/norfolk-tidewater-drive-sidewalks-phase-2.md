Norfolk is funding about 600 feet of sidewalk along Tidewater Drive, from existing sidewalk past the I-64 eastbound off-ramp to planned sidewalk at Thole Street. Phase 2 also includes four crosswalks, ADA-compliant curb ramps and pedestrian signs. A separate Phase 1 received about $2.07 million in the same funding cycle.

On August 25, 2026, the Norfolk City Council voted 8-0 to accept $1,920,689 from the Virginia Department of Transportation for the project. The council also added $480,172 from the city as Norfolk's share. Together, that is about $2.4 million, with VDOT covering about 80% and the city paying the rest.

The project competed through Virginia's Transportation Alternatives Program. VDOT scored the applications, and the state's selection table lists $1,920,688 for Phase 2 as an at-large award. Norfolk's later appropriation ordinance lists the state amount as $1,920,689.

Nobody needs to apply for anything. Once the sidewalks and crossings are built, anyone walking along this part of Tidewater Drive can use them. Before construction starts, the city has to design and engineer the project, and no construction date has been announced yet.
