When Newark declared a Code Red because of extreme heat or poor air quality, city services included emergency shelter, cooling centers, street outreach, transportation and referrals. Essex County made up to $113,000 available to reimburse eligible costs for those services from July 1 through Sept. 30, 2026.

The help was for residents at risk during dangerous heat or air quality, including people experiencing homelessness or living in unsafe conditions. During a Code Red, residents could use listed cooling centers or call NJ 2-1-1 for information about local resources.

Council members Dupré L. Kelly and Louise Scott-Rountree sponsored the resolution. The Health and Community Wellness director had electronically signed the county agreement on Aug. 17, and the Municipal Council approved and ratified it on Sept. 9. The vote was 9-0. The grant required no municipal matching funds.
