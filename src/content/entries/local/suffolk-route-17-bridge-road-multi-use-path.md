Route 17, which Suffolk calls Bridge Road, is a busy highway. Suffolk plans a multi-use path along it for people walking, biking, or using a wheelchair. The path will give people a safer way to get along the road without a car. Anyone can use it once it's built, and no one has to apply.

The project received $2,184,448 through the Transportation Alternatives program. The Hampton Roads Transportation Planning Organization says the program uses federal Surface Transportation Block Grant money provided under the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act. HRTPO allocated this award, and the Virginia Department of Transportation administers the money. The same round selected eight Hampton Roads projects worth $14,403,006 in all.

On October 7, 2026, Suffolk City Council voted 8-0 on its consent agenda to accept the award and put it in the city budget. The ordinance also moved money from the city's Route 17 widening project into the path project. The path is not built yet: Suffolk lists preliminary engineering as in progress, right-of-way work for winter 2027 through winter 2029, and construction for summer 2029 through summer 2031.
