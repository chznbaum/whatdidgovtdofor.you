Richmond is helping pay for a new apartment building in Manchester, at Hull Street and Commerce Road. The View at Belle Isle will have 116 apartments: 85 one-bedrooms and 31 two-bedrooms. Every unit is income-restricted. The city agreement requires the project's income and rent designations to average 60% of the area median income for at least 30 years. The building also has a clubhouse, fitness room, bike storage, covered parking, and an on-site bus stop.

The $383,928 annual grant figure is an estimate, not a fixed payment. After construction is complete, Richmond agreed to grant back 100% of the building's property-tax revenue above the existing $15,672 annual base for 30 years, subject to yearly appropriations. Payments go through the Economic Development Authority after the owner pays its taxes. The project also uses federal low-income housing tax credits. Applicants must apply through the building's leasing office and meet its income limits.

Council member Ellen Robertson and Mayor Levar Stoney were the ordinance's patrons. City Council adopted it 9-0 on Oct. 10, 2023. As of Nov. 18, 2026, Lawson was accepting applications through an open on-site leasing office and described the building as coming in November, but no reliable source had yet confirmed resident move-ins.
