Broward County set aside $20,823,637 for support services during the budget year that began October 1, 2026. The money goes to specified service providers under new or renewed county agreements. Those providers serve individuals and families experiencing homelessness in Broward County.

In separate 9-0 actions under the same agenda item, commissioners approved The Salvation Army as the sole source for emergency shelter services for families and authorized an amendment to HOPE South Florida's contract to add housing case management at no additional cost to the county.

People experiencing homelessness can call Broward's Homeless Helpline at 954-563-4357. The county and its community partners provide services including emergency shelter, rapid rehousing, rental assistance, permanent supportive housing and housing case management.

The county's Housing Options, Solutions and Supports division recommended the funding package. The commission approved it on August 20, 2026, with County Mayor Mark Bogen presiding. The first tally was 8-0 because Vice Mayor Robert McKinzie couldn't be heard during the vote. He later asked to be recorded as a yes, making the final tally 9-0. The commission adopted the overall FY2027 budget on September 22, and the fiscal year began October 1.
