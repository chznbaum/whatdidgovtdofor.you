Atlanta's council authorized WorkSource Atlanta to accept up to $876,827 in federal youth workforce money awarded through the Technical College System of Georgia. The ordinance allows the grant to fund employment and training opportunities for eligible city residents ages 14 to 24. WorkSource Atlanta's current Youth R.I.S.E. page advertises year-round, no-cost services for ages 16 to 24, including GED and high school support, short training courses, recognized certificates, internships and paid work experience.

Under federal WIOA rules, in-school youth are generally ages 14 to 21 and must be low-income and face a qualifying barrier. Out-of-school youth are ages 16 to 24 and must meet one of several criteria; low income is required only for some. WorkSource Atlanta's current enrollment page starts both tracks at age 16. Applicants complete an orientation, create a WorkSource Georgia profile, submit documents and meet with an eligibility specialist. Participation is first-come, first-served and depends on available funding.

The ordinance came from the council's Community Development/Human Services Committee, chaired by Matt Westmoreland. The committee recommended it unanimously, and the full council approved it 13–0 on August 17, 2026, with two members away. The ordinance also authorized the city to add the grant to its program-year 2026 job-training budget.
