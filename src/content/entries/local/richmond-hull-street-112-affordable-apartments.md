Richmond is helping pay for a new five-story apartment building at 1501–1511 Hull Street, between Manchester and Blackwell on the Southside. The agreement requires at least 112 apartments. Rent and occupancy limits across the project must average 60% of the area median income for at least 30 years. The developer plans one- and two-bedroom units and is using federal low-income housing tax credits. In late 2025, the state also awarded $3.47 million in affordable-housing loan funding to the project. When leasing begins, applicants will have to meet the property's income limits.

The city estimated the grant's first-year payment at $248,512, but the amount can change with the property's assessment. Payments begin only after construction is complete and equal 98% of the yearly real estate tax revenue above the site's $12,336 baseline, for up to 30 years. The developer must complete the building within three years after construction begins and continue meeting the affordability rules.

Mayor Levar Stoney was named as the ordinance's patron when it was introduced in December 2024. A City Council committee recommended it, and the full council adopted it on January 13, 2025. By November 2026, general contractor KBS reported that crews were framing the five-story building.
