The Shingle Creek Trail is a paved walking and biking path that runs through southwest Orange County. It still has a gap between Destination Parkway and Taft-Vineland Road. This grant pays to build the southern part of that gap: about 0.93 miles from State Road 528 down to West Taft-Vineland Road. The path will be up to 14 feet wide and meet current accessibility standards. About 2,600 feet of it is boardwalk, and the rest is asphalt and concrete trail.

The money comes as a grant through the Florida Department of Transportation. The August agreement added $4,446,571, bringing the total grant to $9,711,240, and the county doesn't have to put up any local matching money. When this segment and the one north of it are done, the trail will connect the finished stretch at Destination Parkway to the finished section that runs south from Taft-Vineland Road toward Osceola County.

The county's Public Works Department brought the agreement to the Board of County Commissioners. The board approved it on Aug. 25, 2026, with Mayor Jerry Demings presiding. Construction began on Oct. 19, 2026. When the trail opens, anyone can use it for free, with no sign-up needed.
