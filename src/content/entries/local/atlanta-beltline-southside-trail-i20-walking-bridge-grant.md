The Atlanta Beltline's Southside Trail is getting a permanent half-mile stretch along Bill Kennedy Way, between Glenwood Avenue and Memorial Drive, in place of the temporary trail there now. The project includes a new bridge over I-20 for people walking and biking, built beside the existing road bridge, so trail users won't have to share the crossing with car traffic. Up to $2 million in federal highway money will help pay to build it.

You don't need to apply for anything. Once it's built, the trail and bridge are open to anyone, free. It isn't built yet. Atlanta BeltLine expects each of the project's two phases to take 16 to 18 months, and its schedule has the federally funded section going out to bid in early 2028.

Sens. Jon Ossoff and Raphael Warnock asked Congress to set aside the $2 million for the I-20 bridge. The money was in the bipartisan federal funding law that took effect in February 2026. In Atlanta, the City Council's Transportation Committee, chaired by Council Member Alex Wan, sponsored the ordinance that accepted the grant. The full council approved it 13-0 on Sept. 21, 2026.
