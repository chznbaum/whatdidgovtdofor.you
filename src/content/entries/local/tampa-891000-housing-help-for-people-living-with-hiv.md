Tampa authorized up to $891,000 over three years to reimburse Metropolitan Charities, the St. Petersburg nonprofit doing business as Metro Healthy Communities, for housing and support services. The services are for low-income people living with HIV/AIDS in Hillsborough County and family members who live with them. The money comes from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's Housing Opportunities for Persons with AIDS program, known as HOPWA. Tampa administers that program across Hernando, Hillsborough, Pasco and Pinellas counties, but this agreement covers Hillsborough County.

HOPWA housing aid is for people with low incomes. Under this agreement, Metro provides short-term rent, mortgage and utility help and supportive services. To get help, people contact the agency directly. Metro also offers HIV medical care and case management.

The Tampa City Council approved the agreement unanimously on October 15, 2026. Council member Naya Young moved to adopt it, Luis Viera seconded, and Lynn Hurtak and Bill Carlson were absent. The agreement permits reimbursements of up to $297,000 in each of three fiscal years, but all payments depend on Tampa receiving the federal HOPWA funds from HUD.
