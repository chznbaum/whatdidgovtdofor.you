New York City is spending more than $1 billion over five years to fix its public pools and build new ones. The city calls it the most it has put into swimming since the 1970s. The money covers 39 pools. It pays for two new indoor pools, full rebuilds of three others, and about $85 million in repairs to old pool tubs, wiring, ventilation, decks, lighting and filters. Astoria Pool, the city's largest, was rebuilt for about $19 million.

The city's outdoor pools are free and do not require membership. Indoor pools at Parks recreation centers require a recreation-center membership. The Shirley Chisholm Recreation Center in East Flatbush opened in February 2026 with a six-lane indoor pool you can get into by ramp, chairlift or stairs. Membership there is free for anyone 24 or younger. In Central Park, the new Gottesman Pool at Harlem Meer opened in June 2025, replacing the old Lasker Pool.

Mayor Eric Adams and Parks Commissioner Sue Donoghue launched the program in June 2024. The city's capital plan funds it, with the investment scheduled across fiscal 2024 through 2028.
