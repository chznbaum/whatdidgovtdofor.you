If you're a lower-income DeKalb County water customer, the Water Rate Assistance Program may give you a monthly credit that keeps your water and sewer costs at or below 4.5% of household income. A family of four can meet the posted income rule with income up to $68,520, but eligibility also depends on household size and water use. If your bill is already below 4.5% of income, you do not qualify.

Apply online or by mail through the Urban League of Greater Atlanta at DeKalbWaterAssist.org, or call 404-371-6261. Current program guidance says to allow about 30 to 90 days for review. Applicants may receive protection from disconnection during review; it is not automatic. The same application can request Debt Amnesty, under which the county pays $1 toward an eligible past-due balance for every $1 you pay after signing an installment agreement. The contract also funds an advocate who reviews billing disputes after the county's Customer Assurance Unit makes its determination.

The county launched WRAP in August 2025 under CEO Lorraine Cochran-Johnson, when the first 10% water and sewer rate increase took effect. On Aug. 25, 2026, the Board of Commissioners, led by Presiding Officer Chakira Johnson, renewed the Urban League's contract for a year at $1,564,359. The prior $1,254,048 contract covered the startup period; the county said the increase mainly reflects a full year of operations and higher personnel, contract-service and indirect costs.
