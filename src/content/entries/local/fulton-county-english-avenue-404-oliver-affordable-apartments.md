A cleared lot in Atlanta's English Avenue neighborhood, just north of Kathryn Johnston Memorial Park, is planned for 54 income-restricted rental apartments. The county-supported project is called The 404 @ Oliver Street. Westside Future Fund, a community nonprofit, is developing it. The rental mix will be 30 one-bedroom, 12 two-bedroom and 12 three-bedroom units. Six additional for-sale single-family homes are planned on the site but are not part of the 54 rentals.

The 404 @ Oliver Street has no market-rate apartments. Its 54 rentals will be designated for households at income levels between 30% and 80% of the area median income. Invest Atlanta separately approved a $2.5 million Westside Tax Allocation District grant to help finance the project. The cited project updates have not listed specific monthly rents.

Commissioner Dana Barrett sponsored the county resolution. It approved a grant from money the county had previously set aside. The Board of Commissioners passed it 5-0 on Sept. 16, 2026, with three Democrats and two Republicans voting yes; Vice Chair Khadijah Abdur-Rahman was absent. As of Nov. 15, groundbreaking was scheduled for Nov. 17, and construction was expected to take roughly 18 months.
