Some of the big sewer lines in Atlanta's eastside Peachtree Creek basin do not have enough capacity. During wet weather, that can lead to sanitary sewer overflows. Eastside 2B will replace capacity-limited trunk and outfall sewer segments so the system can carry a 2-year, 3-hour design storm without overflowing. It is the last Sewer Group Five capacity-relief project Atlanta must complete under its federal consent decree.

You don't need to apply. Atlanta added this work to its existing progressive design-build contract with Ruby-Collins/Benchmark for the Westside 2A project. The ordinance authorizes up to $24.04 million for Eastside 2B. The city had tried twice to procure Eastside 2B separately, but both solicitations failed; one was canceled because the bids exceeded the available budget.

The substitute ordinance came from the City Utilities Committee, chaired by Council Member Dustin Hillis. The full Atlanta City Council adopted it 12-0 on May 18, 2026. The ordinance waived the usual competitive-selection rules so the city could add the work to the existing contract, avoiding another procurement delay ahead of a consent-decree milestone.
