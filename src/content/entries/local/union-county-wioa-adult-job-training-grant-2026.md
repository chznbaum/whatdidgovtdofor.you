Union County added $1,468,418 to its 2026 budget for adult job training and career help. The money is a federal Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) Adult grant passed through the New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development. The county runs the program through its American Job Centers.

The program offers career counseling, job-search planning, and workshops. It can also pay for job training: occupational skills courses funded through Individual Training Accounts, plus on-the-job training with local employers. Eligibility follows federal and New Jersey rules, so adults should check with a job center first. The Plainfield center at 200 West 2nd Street takes appointments and walk-ins on weekdays at 908-757-9090. The county lists a temporary Elizabeth site at 342 Westminster Avenue but also says the Elizabeth center is temporarily closed, so call before you go.

Commissioner Kimberly Palmieri-Mouded's name is on the resolution that put the grant into the budget. The Board of County Commissioners approved it 8-0 on July 30, 2026. Commissioner Mirabella was absent.
