Virginia Beach charges real estate tax as a rate per $100 of a property's assessed value. In May 2024 the City Council lowered that rate from 99 cents to 97 cents for the budget year that began July 1, 2024. On a home assessed at $400,000, that's $80 a year less than the old rate would have charged. Property owners didn't need to do anything; the lower rate showed up on their bills.

The city said the cut was meant to help offset rising real estate assessments. When assessments climb and the rate stays the same, tax bills go up, so lowering the rate keeps some of that increase from landing on homeowners.

The council adopted the 2024–25 budget, including the lower rate, on a 10–1 vote on May 14, 2024. The same budget raised the curbside trash fee by $3.05 a month to pay for expanded recycling and added 30 full-time positions to the fire department. In May 2026 the council kept the rate at 97 cents for the 2026–27 year and chose a vehicle tax credit as its main form of relief instead.
