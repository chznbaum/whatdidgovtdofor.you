Henrico County is building four more sections of the Fall Line Trail in the Lakeside area and along Hilliard and Brook roads. The work adds a 1.6-mile paved path that people on foot and on bikes share, plus bridges and boardwalks. It also adds a pedestrian hybrid beacon, a signal people walking can press to stop traffic so they can cross. The project includes new drainage to handle stormwater.

Once these sections open, people will be able to use them without signing up. When the full Fall Line Trail is done, it will run 43 miles from Petersburg to Ashland. It will link neighborhoods across the Richmond region with a route for walking and biking.

The Henrico Board of Supervisors, chaired by Roscoe Cooper, approved the $15.9 million contract with Crowder Construction on Oct. 27, 2026. The county expected work to start in November and take about two years. Since 2021, the Central Virginia Transportation Authority, made up of the region's localities, has set aside more than $100 million in regional transportation money for the trail.
