In 2026, employers can pay a new hire aged 14 to 17 a $13.95 youth rate during their first 90 days. The regular city minimum depends on employer size: at the start of 2027, it will be $14.25 for micro businesses, $16.37 for small businesses and $17.02 for employers with at least 101 workers. Starting January 1, 2027, the youth discount is gone. A teen gets the same city minimum for their employer's size as everyone else, starting on day one.

Teens don't have to apply or do anything to get the higher rate. Employers simply can't use the general youth rate anymore. One exception remains: city-approved youth training and apprenticeship programs can still pay workers under 20 a lower training wage.

Councilmember Molly Coleman sponsored the ordinance with Councilmembers Nelsie Yang and HwaJeong Kim. Coleman spoke for it and moved its adoption, and Council President Rebecca Noecker and Yang also spoke in favor. The council passed it 7–0 on August 12, 2026, and the mayor's office signed it August 20.
