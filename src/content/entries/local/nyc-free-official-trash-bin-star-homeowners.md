As of June 1, 2026, owners of properties with one to nine residential units must put their trash in an official NYC Bin. After a warning period, $50 fines began Sept. 8, 2026. A 2025 city law created a reimbursement program for owners of one- and two-family homes who received STAR or Enhanced STAR in 2024 or 2025.

The city offered $59.30 for one 45-gallon bin. Eligible owners who bought directly from the manufacturer online or by phone received checks automatically. People who bought at Home Depot or through a delivery app had to request reimbursement using their voucher number and proof of purchase. The deadline was Sept. 8, 2026, and the program is now closed.

Council Member Yusef Salaam was the prime sponsor, with Democratic and Republican council members among the cosponsors. The Council passed the bill by consent roll call on Feb. 27, 2025. Mayor Eric Adams neither signed nor vetoed it, so it became law without his signature on March 29. His administration began sending checks that fall, and the city's November financial plan added $9.1 million in city funding for the reimbursement program.
