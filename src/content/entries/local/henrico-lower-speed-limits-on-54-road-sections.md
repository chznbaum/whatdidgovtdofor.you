Henrico is lowering the speed limit on 54 sections of county-maintained roads. Many of the changes are on roads serving residential neighborhoods that drop from 35 to 25 mph, such as Lucas Road, Eubank Road and Sleepy Hollow Road. These roads may have narrow widths, frequent driveways or people walking. Other roads, including Hermitage, Ridge and Oakland roads, drop to 30 mph to better support people walking or biking. Roads including Hungary Road and parts of Nuckols and Harvie roads go from 45 to 35 mph to reduce crash and run-off-road risks. One narrow stretch of Portugee Road drops from 55 to 45 mph.

You don't need to do anything. When a road changes, a new speed limit sign goes up with orange flags and a "New Regulation" plate, and both stay for at least 14 days. Police don't enforce the new limit until 30 days after it's posted. Public Works says the rollout will begin in November 2026 and take about six months. As of Nov. 23, the county's tracking page had not posted an implementation date for any road; it will list those dates as each change is completed.

The Department of Public Works, led by director Terrell Hughes, chose the roads after a countywide speed study. The study looked at crash history, road design, how land along each road is used, and pedestrian activity. The county says the goal is fewer crashes involving drivers and pedestrians and fewer run-off-road crashes, particularly on rural roads.
