If you're living with HIV or AIDS in Hillsborough County and need help keeping a stable home, you may qualify for assistance through this agreement. Tampa approved up to $2.85 million in federal HOPWA reimbursements for EPIC, legally named AIDS Service Association of Pinellas, over three years. The agreement covers tenant-based rental assistance and housing-focused case management and supportive services.

The agreement calls for EPIC to keep 64 rental-assistance slots occupied each month. Housing help is for low-income Hillsborough County residents living with HIV/AIDS and their families. Supportive services—including housing placement, mental health or substance-use counseling, nutrition help, and assistance reaching government benefits—may be provided regardless of income. Assistance is not automatic; EPIC says to call 813-237-3066 about qualification and referrals.

The Tampa City Council approved the agreement 6-0 on Oct. 1, 2026, at a meeting chaired by Alan Clendenin. Council Member Lynn Hurtak was absent for the vote. The resolution authorizes Mayor Jane Castor to execute the agreement and caps reimbursement at $950,000 in each of fiscal years 2027, 2028, and 2029, contingent on Tampa receiving the federal HUD funds.
