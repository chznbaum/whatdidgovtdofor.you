East Cobb is getting a new home for Fire Station 20, but the Sept. 8 vote did not set the station's full construction price. Cobb County hired John W. Spratlin & Son as the design-builder for the site at Sewell Mill and East Piedmont roads. The approved $673,000 is the initial amount for design and the builder's services, not the total cost of the finished station. The county's solicitation calls for the team to prepare construction documents and a detailed estimate that can be developed into a guaranteed maximum construction price. The money comes from the county's capital contingency fund.

The station it replaces sits on Hilton Drive, about eight-tenths of a mile west. It was built in 1984 and covers a wide, busy part of east Cobb with 12 firefighters across three shifts. The county bought the 3.5-acre new site for $975,000 in 2022, when the fire department said the station had to move to better meet response needs. Construction was left for a later budget cycle. You do not need to do anything: the station's area will keep getting fire and emergency service while the replacement is planned and built.

Chairwoman Lisa Cupid led the Board of Commissioners as it approved the agreement 4-0 on its consent agenda on Sept. 8, 2026. Commissioner Erick Allen was absent.
