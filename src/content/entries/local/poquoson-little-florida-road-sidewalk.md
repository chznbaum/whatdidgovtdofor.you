Poquoson is building a sidewalk on Little Florida Road, from the CVS to Gay Lynn Drive. Today people walking that stretch, including those headed to the pharmacy and nearby shops, have to use the edge of the road. The new sidewalk gives them a separate place to walk, push a stroller or use a wheelchair.

Nobody needs to apply or pay anything to use it. When the work is done, the sidewalk is open to everyone.

Mayor David Hux directed the city manager in January 2025 to scope and survey this stretch. The city put the project out to bid in August 2026. On September 28, 2026, the City Council voted 6–0 to let the city manager sign a $533,640.10 construction contract with Finley Asphalt & Concrete. Councilman Freeman made the motion and Councilwoman Emmett seconded it. Councilmembers Southall and Hubbard, Vice Mayor Bunting and Mayor Hux also voted yes. By then, the city had spent $135,859 on surveys and design plans for this and another sidewalk project.
