Cobb County is rebuilding the spot where Florence Road meets Hiram Road and Gaydon Road, near Powder Springs. The plans call for a roundabout, a raised median, new lane striping and street lighting, along with county water system work. Roundabouts slow traffic and reduce conflict points; the Federal Highway Administration says they can substantially reduce crashes that cause serious injuries or deaths. The county lists this as an operational and safety improvement.

You don't need to do anything to benefit. Once the work is finished, people who drive through the intersection will use the new layout and lighting.

On Sept. 8, 2026, the Cobb County Board of Commissioners, chaired by Lisa Cupid, approved a contract with Bartow Paving Company worth up to $753,051.96 to build it. The vote was 4-0, with Commissioner Erick Allen absent. The contractor has 270 days to finish once the county orders work to begin. B2448 is one of Cobb County's 2024 LMIG Supplement projects, funded through a Georgia Department of Transportation Local Road Assistance grant that requires no local match. The latest county report found, dated Oct. 1, said construction was scheduled to begin in late October.
