Atlanta's Department of Parks and Recreation runs after-school and summer programs at city recreation centers. Its summer meal program serves about 3,000 meals a day to the kids taking part. This ordinance lets the city accept up to $790,680 from the U.S. Department of Agriculture's Child and Adult Care Food Program to pay for those meals. The city's meal vendor, Meals Pro, Inc., makes the food.

Families don't apply for the grant. Kids in the department's programs get meals at the centers they already go to. The grant works by paying the city back: Atlanta bills the USDA for eligible meal costs. The grant runs from July 14, 2026 to July 15, 2027. If the USDA won't cover a cost, the city pays it from its own accounts, including the general fund.

The council's Community Development/Human Services Committee sponsored the ordinance. Councilmember Matt Westmoreland chairs that committee. The full council passed it 10–0 on May 18, 2026.
