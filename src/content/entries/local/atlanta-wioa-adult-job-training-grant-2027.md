Atlanta's City Council authorized WorkSource Atlanta to accept up to $802,734 to help adults find work and train for better jobs. The money comes from the federal Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act Adult program, and the state passes it to the city through the Technical College System of Georgia's Office of Workforce Development. The council also added the same amount to the city's job training grant fund for the 2027 budget year.

Under federal rules, the Adult program pays for job search help and training. When staff hand out its one-on-one career services and training, they have to serve people on public assistance, other low-income people, and people who need basic reading or math skills first. Veterans also get priority. To get services, you sign up with WorkSource Atlanta and show that you qualify.

The city council authorized accepting the grant 12–0 on Aug. 17, 2026, with Council President Marci Collier Overstreet presiding.
