On July 7, 2026, Ramsey County put $1.2 million in federal money toward F2F@Arcade. Face to Face plans the four-story building at 1170 Arcade Street in Saint Paul's Payne-Phalen neighborhood. The current plan calls for 24 permanent supportive apartments for young people ages 18 to 24 earning less than 30% of the area's median income: 12 studios, nine one-bedroom apartments and three two-bedroom apartments. The building would also include 6,000 square feet of office and service space for Face to Face.

The apartments must stay affordable for at least 30 years, and the county will check compliance annually. Saint Paul reported in June 2026 that construction could start in late 2027 or early 2028 if the project secures Minnesota Housing funding.

The money comes from HUD's PRO Housing program. The Metropolitan Council applied as the lead grantee in partnership with Hennepin and Ramsey counties and won a $4 million regional grant. Ramsey County's Housing and Redevelopment Authority accepted the county's $1.3 million share in January 2026. It directed $1.2 million to F2F@Arcade in July; the other $100,000 was reserved for administering the federal grant. The county had already awarded the project $500,000 from its housing levy in 2025.
