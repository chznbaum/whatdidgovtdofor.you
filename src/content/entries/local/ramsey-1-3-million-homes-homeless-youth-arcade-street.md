Young people who are homeless in Ramsey County are getting a building of their own. On July 7, 2026, the county put $1.2 million in federal money toward F2F@Arcade. The nonprofit Face to Face plans the building on Arcade Street in Saint Paul's Payne-Phalen neighborhood, across from its health clinic for young people. The plan calls for 24 studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments. All 24 are reserved for people ages 18 to 24 who are homeless. Four will have rents set for households at 30% of the area's median income, and 20 at 50%.

The apartments must stay affordable for at least 30 years. The county will check every year that the rules are being followed. The building plan includes services on the ground floor to support residents.

The money comes from HUD's PRO Housing program. The Metropolitan Council applied as the lead grantee in partnership with Hennepin and Ramsey counties and won a $4 million regional grant. Ramsey County's Housing and Redevelopment Authority accepted the county's $1.3 million share in January 2026. It directed $1.2 million to F2F@Arcade in July; the other $100,000 was reserved for administering the federal grant. The county had already awarded the project $500,000 from its housing levy in 2025.
