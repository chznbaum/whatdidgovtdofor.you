Cobb County Senior Services runs meals, in-home help and caregiver support for older residents. Much of that money comes through the Atlanta Regional Commission, which hands out federal and state aging funds across metro Atlanta. This contract covers one year, from July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2027.

The budget totals about $2.35 million. Roughly $1.33 million is federal (Title III aging funds and federal money for senior meals), and about $850,000 comes from the state for meals, home- and community-based services, case management, respite care and a $150,000 Alzheimer's program. The county adds a $142,110 match. Planned spending includes about $624,000 for food, $151,000 for in-home respite through the Share the Care program, and the staff and vehicles that keep these services running. In 2024, Cobb Senior Services delivered more than 62,000 meals through home delivery, mobile markets and senior center lunches.

You don't apply to this contract. If you're an older Cobb resident or a family caregiver, you contact Cobb Senior Services about the specific program you need.

The Board of Commissioners approved the contract 5-0 on its consent agenda on July 14, 2026. Chairwoman Lisa Cupid led the meeting, and the vote authorized her to sign the contract.
