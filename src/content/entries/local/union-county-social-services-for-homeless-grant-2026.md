Union County added $1,054,322 to its 2026 budget after New Jersey's Department of Human Services told the county it would receive the money through the Social Services for the Homeless grant. The money comes from the state, and adding it to the county budget lets the county spend it.

Social Services for the Homeless helps families and individuals who are homeless or at risk of losing their homes. It pays for emergency food and emergency shelter, covers security and utility deposits, and can pay back rent, mortgage, and utility bills. It is meant for people who don't qualify for Work First New Jersey, usually because their income is too high. If you live in New Jersey and are homeless or about to be, you can call 2-1-1 to be connected with help in your county.

Commissioner Kimberly Palmieri-Mouded sponsored the resolution. The Board of County Commissioners approved it 6-0 on June 25, 2026, with three members absent.
