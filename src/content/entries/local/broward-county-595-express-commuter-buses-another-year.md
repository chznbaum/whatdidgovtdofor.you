The 595 Express is a set of commuter buses that run between Broward and Miami-Dade counties on weekdays, during the morning and afternoon rush hours. Broward County Transit runs the buses and the Florida Department of Transportation pays for them through a grant. This vote added $2,977,540 to that grant and extended it by one year, so the service can keep running in the county's 2027 budget year.

Riders don't need to apply for anything. You board and pay the fare as usual. The county says the service is designed to shorten trips and make service more reliable. The state grant and fare revenue cover what it costs to run and maintain the buses, so the extension doesn't take money from other county services.

Broward County's Transportation Department brought the grant amendment to the County Commission. Mayor Mark Bogen chaired the commission when it approved it 9–0 on June 9, 2026. The commission also put the $2,977,540 into the county budget. The vote lets the county administrator sign future amendments that only add state money or extend the grant, as long as they bring no new cost or risk to the county.
