If your kids walk or bike to school on Saint Paul's West Side, six of the places where they cross the street are getting pedestrian safety improvements. The work is mostly curb extensions, also called bumpouts. They push the curb out into the street, so kids have a shorter way to cross and drivers can see them better. The sites include Page Street and Waseca Street, Livingston Avenue and Page Street, Bellows and Morton streets, two spots on Clinton Avenue, and George Street at Humboldt Avenue.

The improvements serve families at Riverview Spanish/English Dual Immersion Elementary, Cherokee Heights Elementary, Open World Learning and Humboldt High School. Nobody needs to apply for anything. The changes will be built into the streets. As of November 13, 2026, the city had advertised the construction work, with contractor bids due November 18.

The project comes from the West Side Safe Routes to School Plan, completed in 2021 by Saint Paul Public Schools, city staff and community partners. Council President Amy Brendmoen sponsored the 2023 resolution authorizing Public Works to apply for regional money. The Metropolitan Council then awarded $777,400 in Active Transportation funding, and the city committed a 20% local match plus engineering. Council President Rebecca Noecker sponsored the resolution accepting the grant. The council adopted it on May 20, 2026, and Mayor Kaohly Her signed it six days later.
