If you're an older homeowner in Hialeah with a modest income, the city now pays 25% of your city water and sewer bill. The program covers fiscal year 2027, from Oct. 1, 2026, through Sept. 30, 2027.

To qualify, you must be 65 or older and own your home. Your city water account must be in your name and have its own meter, your homestead and senior exemptions must be approved, and your annual household income must be below $38,686. The city says it applies the savings automatically, so no application is required.

Hialeah separately lowered the rates charged on the first 10,000 gallons used each month, even when an account uses more than that. The city says that lower rate plus the senior credit cuts an eligible senior's average water and sewer bill by about half.

Mayor Bryan Calvo's administration proposed the senior program, which he announced with the rate reduction on Sept. 8, 2026. Two weeks later, the City Council, led by President Carl Zogby, adopted it 7–0.
