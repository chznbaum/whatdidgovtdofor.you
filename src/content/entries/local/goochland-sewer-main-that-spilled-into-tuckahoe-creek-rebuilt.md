The pressurized sewer line that runs from the Eastern Goochland Pump Station carries wastewater from eastern Goochland and nearby parts of Henrico more than 10 miles to Richmond and Henrico treatment systems. Its first two miles had broken many times over the years, causing sewer overflows. On the evening of July 27, 2021, it ruptured near River Road and sent about 300,000 gallons of raw sewage into a ditch that drains to Tuckahoe Creek. Health officials told people and pets to stay out of the creek and about 12 miles of the James River, from Robious Landing Park to Belle Isle.

To fix the line for good, crews slid new 42-inch plastic pipe inside the old one. That approach needed less digging in nearby neighborhoods and cost less. Engineers expect the new pipe to last more than 75 years. Customers didn't have to do anything. The work reached substantial completion in July 2024.

The Goochland Board of Supervisors, chaired by Neil Spoonhower, approved the $7,997,175 contract with Spiniello Companies 5-0 on Dec. 6, 2022. The resolution also moved an additional $1 million from the county's utility capital-improvement fund into its utility repair fund to cover the contract. It requires that $1 million to be repaid to the utility capital-improvement fund within ten years.
