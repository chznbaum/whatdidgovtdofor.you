The Bx6 Select Bus Service runs across the Bronx on East 161st Street, right past Yankee Stadium, and about 25,000 people ride it every day. The city is rebuilding the street so buses can get through more easily. The plan adds westbound bus-only lanes and a protected bus lane down the middle of East 161st Street from Concourse Village West to just west of River Avenue. The tunnel under the Grand Concourse now carries eastbound buses only; it will carry buses only, in both directions. Bus stops get concrete boarding islands, sidewalk extensions, shelters and benches.

People on foot get safer crossings: curb extensions, medians and refuge islands where there was only paint before. The work also adds 370 accessible pedestrian ramps, 57 trees, more than 100 new street light and signal poles, upgraded water mains and sewers, and new catch basins. Part of East 163rd Street is being rebuilt too.

Riders don't need to do anything. The changes arrive in phases as construction moves along the corridor. The city expects the work to last through 2028.

Design work began in 2019 and was completed in September 2024. Construction started in spring 2026. The Department of Transportation, under Commissioner Mike Flynn, is overseeing the bus and safety changes. The Department of Design and Construction, led by Acting Commissioner Eduardo del Valle when construction began, is building the project and phasing work around the baseball season.
