Camden County awarded Volunteers of America Delaware Valley a new contract to keep the county-owned Regan Center running. The 23-bed program serves single adult men experiencing homelessness: 15 beds provide emergency shelter and eight provide medical respite, including for men leaving a hospital. Both programs require a referral. People seeking shelter can call 211 or the VOA Navigation Center at 856-671-6101.

The contract is worth up to $6,735,057 and began Aug. 1, 2026. The county made $909,156 available right away. The rest has to be approved in the county's 2027, 2028 and 2029 budgets, so later years depend on those votes.

Shelter beds are one piece of a bigger county push on homelessness. In October 2026 the county also opened the Martin McKernan Supportive Housing Center in Blackwood, part of its plan to reach “functional zero” homelessness by 2030.

Deputy Director Edward McDonnell presided when the Board of Commissioners approved the contract on July 16, 2026; Director Louis Cappelli Jr. was excused. Resolution 56 passed in a block of resolutions by roll call, with all six commissioners present voting yes. The minutes do not name a commissioner who brought the item.
