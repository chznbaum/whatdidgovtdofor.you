If your household gets SNAP, Supplemental Security Income, TANF or Work First New Jersey and also meets a utility program’s rules, this law requires the state to enroll you without a separate application. The programs include the Universal Service Fund, which provides $20 to $200 a month in combined gas and electric bill credits. They also include shutoff protection: gas, electric, water and wastewater service in winter, and electric, water and sewer service in summer. Other state programs offering ongoing utility help can be included; one-time grants cannot.

To do that, the Department of Human Services must share recipient records under an agreement with the Department of Community Affairs and the Board of Public Utilities. Those agencies review program rules and recipient data to identify eligible households. The law took effect July 1, 2026, and tells the agencies to issue any rules they need. As of November 14, the BPU’s current instructions still tell people to apply for USF and call their utility to request winter shutoff protection, so full automatic enrollment has not been confirmed. If you need help now, apply or call your utility.

Assembly members Verlina Reynolds-Jackson, Linda Carter and Carmen Theresa Morales carried the bill in the Assembly. Senators Angela McKnight and Shirley Turner carried it in the Senate. Both chambers passed it on January 12, 2026, 52–23 in the Assembly and 28–10 in the Senate. Gov. Phil Murphy signed it on January 20, 2026, his last day in office.
