If a hospital or a licensed health care provider in New York sues you over an unpaid bill and wins, it can't collect by taking part of your paycheck. It also can't put a lien on your primary residence, the home you live in. Other New York creditors who win a judgment can have up to 10% of your gross pay withheld. This law sets that amount at zero for medical debt judgments that hospitals and licensed providers win.

You don't need to apply or file anything. The protection is written into the state's rules for collecting court judgments, and it has applied since the day the law was signed. The governor's office said more than 50,000 New Yorkers had been sued for medical debt in the previous five years, and that these lawsuits were most common upstate.

Assembly Health Committee Chair Richard Gottfried and Senate Health Committee Chair Gustavo Rivera sponsored the bill, and the Community Service Society of New York campaigned for it. The Assembly passed it in March 2022, and the Senate passed it 47–15 that May. Gov. Kathy Hochul signed it on November 23, 2022.
