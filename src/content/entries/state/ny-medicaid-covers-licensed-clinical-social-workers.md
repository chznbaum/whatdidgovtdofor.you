If you're on Medicaid in New York, care from a licensed clinical social worker (LCSW) can be covered whenever Medicaid would cover that same service from another provider. LCSWs are a large share of the people who diagnose and treat mental, emotional and behavioral conditions, and many of them work in their own practices instead of clinics. This law made their care eligible for Medicaid coverage. That means more choices for therapy close to home.

You don't need to apply for anything beyond Medicaid itself. The social worker has to be licensed and registered with the State Education Department and enrolled in Medicaid. Payment in private practice started March 1, 2023, for fee-for-service Medicaid and April 1, 2023, for Medicaid managed care plans. An LCSW can also supervise up to six licensed master social workers and bill for their services.

Sen. Diane Savino sponsored the bill and Assemblymember Harry Bronson carried it in the Assembly. A 2019 version passed but was vetoed. The 2021 bill passed the Senate 63–0 and the Assembly 136–12, and Gov. Kathy Hochul signed it on December 21, 2021.
