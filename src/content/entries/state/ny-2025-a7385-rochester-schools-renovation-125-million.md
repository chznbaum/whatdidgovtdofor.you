Nearly half of Rochester's public school buildings are more than 75 years old. Since 2007, state law has let Rochester's city government and school district finance renovations in phases. This law raises Phase III's authorized cost ceiling by $125 million, from $475 million to $600 million. It also moves the deadline for the final report on the phase's fiscal and educational results from 2031 to June 30, 2033.

The current Phase III plan covers six schools: School 9, Frederick Douglass, Wilson Commencement, East, Edison Career & Tech, and Padilla. Planned work includes safer entrances and exits and more room for career and technical classes. The project intends to use a project labor agreement with local building trades. Families don't need to do anything. Officials said construction-cost inflation and other changes pushed the planned work above the old cap; the increase lets them revise the work plan and move ahead.

Sen. Jeremy Cooney and Assemblymember Harry Bronson carried companion bills. They had also spearheaded the 2021 law that authorized Phase III after years of delay. The Assembly passed the bill 141–3 on June 4, 2025, and the Senate passed it 54–5 on June 12. Gov. Kathy Hochul signed it on November 21. Preliminary design work was still underway in June 2026, and construction was not expected to start before the second half of the 2026-27 school year.
