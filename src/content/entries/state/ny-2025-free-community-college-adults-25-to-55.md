If you're 25 to 55, live in New York, and don't have a college degree, you can earn an associate degree at a SUNY or CUNY college without paying for tuition, fees, books or supplies. The program must be in a high-demand field. For 2025–26, those fields were nursing and allied health, teaching in shortage areas, technology, engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, advanced manufacturing and green energy.

The state pays whatever your other aid leaves unpaid. To qualify, you file the FAFSA and apply for state TAP or the NYS DREAM Act, if eligible, take at least six credits a semester, and keep up your grades. Some students who already hold a degree can still qualify if they're studying nursing. Apply through your SUNY or CUNY campus. The colleges call it SUNY Reconnect and CUNY Reconnect.

Gov. Kathy Hochul proposed the program in her 2025 State of the State address. It became law as part of that year's state budget, which put $47 million into it. The State Senate passed the budget bill 44–18 on May 8, 2025, and Hochul signed it the next day. Classes started in fall 2025, and more than 15,000 adults have used the program since then.
