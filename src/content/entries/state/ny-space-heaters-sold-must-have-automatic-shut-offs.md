If you buy an electric space heater from a store or manufacturer in New York, it has to meet three safety requirements. It must have a thermostat. It must have an automatic shut-off. And it must be certified by a testing and certification lab that the federal Occupational Safety and Health Administration recognizes. Businesses that make, distribute, or sell space heaters can't sell ones that miss any of the three.

You don't have to do anything to get this protection. It applies to the heaters themselves, so any new heater on a New York store shelf should already meet the rules. The law took effect in April 2023, 120 days after it was signed.

The law came out of the January 2022 Twin Parks fire in the Bronx. A faulty space heater started the fire, and it killed 17 people. State Sen. Cordell Cleare of Manhattan and Assemblymember Kenny Burgos of the Bronx wrote the bill, and Cleare called it the first bill she ever introduced. It passed both chambers without a single no vote that May, 62–0 in the Senate and 149–0 in the Assembly. Gov. Kathy Hochul signed it on December 8, 2022.
