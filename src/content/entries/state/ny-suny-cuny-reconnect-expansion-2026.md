If you're 25 to 55, qualify for in-state tuition and either do not have a college degree or want to return for nursing, SUNY Reconnect and CUNY Reconnect can pay for an associate degree in a field with lots of job openings. They cover tuition, fees, books and supplies. The programs started at community colleges in fall 2025, and more than 12,000 adults had enrolled by June 2026.

The 2026-27 state budget added three fields: supply chain and logistics, air traffic control and aviation management, and emergency management. Associate programs at eight more SUNY colleges are now covered, including Alfred State, Farmingdale and Maritime. So are programs at three more CUNY colleges: City Tech, Medgar Evers and the College of Staten Island. Adults who already have a degree in another field can now come back for nursing.

You apply to the college rather than through a separate Reconnect scholarship application, take at least 6 credits a semester, apply for TAP or the NYS DREAM Act, and file the FAFSA unless an exception applies. Reconnect then pays the remaining covered costs after other grants and scholarships are applied.

Gov. Kathy Hochul proposed the new fields and the nursing pathway in her January budget, and she signed the final budget on May 28, 2026. In October she announced that, starting with the spring 2027 semester, people with a degree in any field can also enroll.
