The Route 50 bridge over Cedar Swamp Creek in Upper Township is 65 years old and in poor condition. It's one of the main ways in and out of coastal Cape May County, an official evacuation route when storms hit, and it carries heavy shore traffic. The county's population grows from about 90,000 to more than 700,000 in summer. New Jersey is replacing it with a new bridge that has 10-foot shoulders, room for a future sidewalk, and better drainage. It will also sit higher, to handle a 100-year storm.

You don't need to do anything to get the benefit. Drivers, emergency crews, and anyone fleeing a storm will use the new bridge. Route 50 is closed at Cedar Swamp Creek during the first stage of construction, with cars sent onto Tyler and Tuckahoe roads and larger vehicles detoured by Route 9. County officials expect the full closure to make the work go much faster.

The New Jersey Department of Transportation, led by Commissioner Priya Jain, awarded the $47.7 million contract in May 2026 to Agate Construction Company and a joint venture of A. Servidone and B. Anthony Construction. It's the department's first design-build project: a single contract covers both design and construction, so the two can overlap. The department says this method can cut delays and costs. Work began Nov. 16, 2026, and is expected to finish by the end of 2028.
