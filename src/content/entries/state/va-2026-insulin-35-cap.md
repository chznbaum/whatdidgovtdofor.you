If you use insulin and your health plan is regulated by Virginia, your copay or coinsurance for a 30-day supply will be capped at $35, no matter how many kinds of insulin you take. A separate $35 aggregate cap will apply to a 30-day supply of covered diabetes equipment and supplies: glucose meters and test strips, syringes, continuous glucose monitors, and insulin pump supplies. You don't need to apply, and your deductible can't push your cost above the cap. The limits apply when your policy is delivered, issued for delivery, reissued, amended, or extended on or after Jan. 1, 2027.

Virginia first capped insulin costs at $50 per 30-day supply in 2020. The 2026 law lowers that cap by $15 and adds the first cost-sharing limit for diabetes equipment and supplies.

Del. Karrie Delaney sponsored the bill, and it passed with bipartisan support. The Senate passed the amended bill 40–0, and the House agreed to the Senate amendment 97–0. Gov. Abigail Spanberger approved it on April 13, 2026, and held a ceremonial signing in May with other health care cost bills.
