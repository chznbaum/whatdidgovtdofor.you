The George P. Coleman Bridge carries Route 17 across the York River between Yorktown and Gloucester Point. Since the bridge was rebuilt in the mid-1990s, drivers heading north into Gloucester paid a toll of 85 cents to $4. Since August 8, 2025, crossing is free. You don't need to do anything to get the savings.

Gov. Glenn Youngkin, a Republican, proposed ending the toll in his 2025 amendments to the state budget, and the General Assembly adopted the change with a deadline of January 1, 2026. The state had already paid off the bonds that financed the bridge, and the tolls brought in about $6 million a year but cost about $3 million a year to collect. The toll equipment was also wearing out and would have needed about $5 million to keep running. Citing those costs, the state stopped collecting nearly five months early.

State officials estimated that drivers would have paid about $60 million more if tolls had continued to 2034. Republican legislators from the area, including Sen. Danny Diggs of York County, backed the change. Diggs pointed out that it cost "$3 million to collect the toll, so you're only netting $3 million."
