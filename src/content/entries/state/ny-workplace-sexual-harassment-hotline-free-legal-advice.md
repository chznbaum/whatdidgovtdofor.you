If you're facing sexual harassment at work in New York, you can call 1-800-HARASS-3 (1-800-427-2773) for free, confidential help. Staff at the state Division of Human Rights answer during regular business hours and explain how to file a complaint with the Division. If you want to talk to a lawyer, they can put you in touch with one who has experience in harassment cases and has volunteered to give limited advice for free.

You don't have to apply or already have a lawyer. A lawyer who advises you through the hotline can't use the call to sign you up as a client for that same complaint. The law also has the Division work with the Department of Labor to add the hotline number to the sexual harassment materials employers must post or give to workers.

Sen. Alessandra Biaggi and Assembly Member Yuh-Line Niou carried the bill. Biaggi's version passed the Senate unanimously in May 2021 but stalled in the Assembly. In 2022 it passed both chambers with no votes against: 138–0 in the Assembly and 63–0 in the Senate. Gov. Kathy Hochul signed it on March 16, 2022. The Division, led by Commissioner Maria Imperial, worked with the New York State Bar Association and other lawyers' groups to recruit volunteers. It opened the line on July 19, 2022.
