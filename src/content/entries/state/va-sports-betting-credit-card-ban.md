If you bet on sports through a licensed app or sportsbook in Virginia, you can no longer load your betting account with a credit card. The Virginia Lottery, which regulates sports betting, can't approve credit cards as a way to fund an account, and each sportsbook has to take reasonable steps to refuse them on its platform. You can still use other ways to pay, such as a debit card or a bank transfer.

The point is to stop people from using credit-card debt to fund bets. Credit cards can delay when a loss is paid and can add interest if the balance is not paid in full. You don't have to do anything to be covered, since the rule applies to every licensed sportsbook. It took effect July 1, 2026.

Del. Marty Martinez carried the bill. Delegates Nadarius Clark, Jackie Glass, Phil Hernandez, Adele McClure, and Cia Price joined as co-patrons. Martinez argued that letting people gamble on credit encourages them to bet money they don't actually have. Regulators noted the Lottery already bars credit cards for lottery purchases. The ban had support from both parties: the House passed it 94–3, the Senate 40–0, and the House agreed to the Senate's amendment 93–4. Gov. Abigail Spanberger signed it on April 13, 2026.
