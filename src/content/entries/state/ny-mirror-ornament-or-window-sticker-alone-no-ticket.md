New York bans stickers on your windshield or rear window that the state hasn't approved, along with objects in your car that block your view, like an air freshener or rosary hanging from the mirror. Since late 2022, those two rules are secondary violations. An officer can write you a ticket for one only if they also have reason to believe you broke some other state law. You don't need to do anything; the limit is written into the Vehicle and Traffic Law.

Supporters said these minor equipment rules were often used as a reason to pull drivers over. Assembly Member Taylor Darling pointed to a Stanford study of nearly 100 million U.S. traffic stops. It found that Black drivers were stopped about 20% more often than white drivers, relative to their share of the population, and searched more often once stopped. Majority Leader Crystal Peoples-Stokes noted that many drivers hang disability placards from their mirrors.

Darling carried the bill, which passed the Assembly in 2021 but died in the Senate. It passed the Assembly again in March 2022, 96–44. Sen. Tim Kennedy sponsored the Senate version, which passed 40–21 that May, with all Republicans present voting no. Gov. Kathy Hochul signed it on December 23, 2022, and it took effect right away.
